# Chat points, bumps, votes, memes and invites

You earn experience and prizes just by taking part in the server: talking, bumping and voting for it, posting memes and inviting friends.

## Your account

Your first chat message in a realm creates your account there: wallet, pouch, quests and daily energy.

## Chat points

- A message counts as a **chat point** when it has **5 or more words of at least 4 characters** that weren't in your last counted message. Short or repeated messages earn nothing. The same message sent twice at once counts once.
- Your **6th chat point within 12 hours** pays **12 experience** and a roll on the chat prize table, once, announced in the experience channel with your name. Later messages in those 12 hours only count chat points. When the 12 hours are over, a new round starts and pays again at its 6th point.
- **40 chat points in the last 30 days** make you an active chatter (a role); it goes again when old points drop out of the 30 days.
- Chat points count on the chatter leaderboards.

Prize tables on this page are without utilities (a Wood Axe, Skinning Knife or Mining Pick raises the scrap rows; see [utilities]({{ '/expeditions/' | relative_url }}#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)).

### Spam control

From your **5th message within a minute** you get a warning; at the **8th** you're timed out for a day. Admins are exempt.

## Bumps

Bump the server with Disboard's `/bump` (or Discord Home's). The bumper gets **10 experience**, a roll on the bump prize table and the **bumper role for 2 hours**, announced in the experience channel.

- A server can be bumped **once every 2 hours on each bump service** (Disboard, Discord Home): a `/bump` answered in that window pays nobody, and the bot says the server was already bumped.
- Two members' bumps answered at the same moment pay only one of them.
- The bumper role is taken back when its 2 hours are over (also across a bot restart).

## Memes

A meme posted in a meme channel pays **8 experience** and a roll on the meme prize table, once per cooldown.

- Deleting one of your memes while your latest meme is under **4 hours** old takes **10 experience** back. Deleting a meme within **a week** of posting takes its point off the monthly meme board.
- Your **25th meme in the last 30 days** makes you a memeologist (a role).

## Invites

When someone joins through your invite, you earn **25 experience** once they reach **100 chat points**. Once per invited member.

## Joining and leaving

- A new member of the home server gets the inactive-chatter role and is welcomed in the welcome channel once verified; others can wave at them.
- **Leaving the server costs 250 experience** (never below 0), announced in the server, and takes you off every leaderboard and its roles right away. Coming back puts you back in the server's lists: your rank role, your scores on the boards and the top roles you rank for.

## Polls

`/poll` posts a poll with a button per answer. **Your vote weighs your experience ÷ 350** (rounded down); under 350 experience you can't vote. You vote once. When the time is up, the result shows every answer's weight and voters, and the winner. A poll running when the bot restarts keeps going.

## Inspect

`/start` → **Activities** → **Inspect** lets you look at other players' Hack & Slash characters, if they allow it. Nothing is open by default: turn on Hack & Slash Inspection under **Inspect** → **Inspect Settings** to let others see your characters, their stats, skills and equipment. The equipment view lists every slot (Helm, Amulet, Main Hand, Off Hand, Armor, Gloves, both rings, Belt, Boots…) with the item worn there or "Empty Slot", under a picture of the character's gear.

## Tips

- Talk in full sentences: five real words of four letters or more make a chat point.
- Bump when the 2-hour window is over: only the first bump in a window pays.
- Vote every 12 hours: each vote pays 20 experience and a roll on the vote prize table.
