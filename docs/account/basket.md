# 🧺 The basket

`/basket` shows what you gathered and cooked. **<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/swordfish-kebabs-with-lemon-and-rosemary.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Swordfish Kebabs with Lemon and Rosemary](../items/consumables/fish-dishes.md#swordfish-kebabs-with-lemon-and-rosemary)</span>** can be eaten from the basket: each one gives **15 experience**. More than you have, or an amount that isn't [a plain whole number](credits-and-runes.md#typing-an-amount) (`0x2`, `1e1` and words like `five` aren't), eats nothing, and the basket says why. Eaten from two devices at the same moment with dishes for one: eaten once, the experience once, and the other basket says you have too few. A dish eaten just as the bot restarts is eaten once or not at all, and its experience counts once (on the 30-day board too).
