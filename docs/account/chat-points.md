# 🗣️ Chat points

You earn experience and prizes just by talking in the server.

- A message counts as a **chat point** when it has **5 or more words of at least 4 characters** that weren't in your last counted message. Short or repeated messages earn nothing. The same message sent twice at once counts once.
- Your **6th chat point within 12 hours** pays **12 experience** and a roll on the chat prize table, once, announced in the experience channel with your name. Later messages in those 12 hours only count chat points. When the 12 hours are over, a new round starts and pays again at its 6th point.
- **40 chat points in the last 30 days** make you an active chatter (a role); it goes again when old points drop out of the 30 days. A restart of the bot, even one in the middle of your message, never takes a chat point off early or counts it twice.
- Chat points count on the chatter <span class="gi-m">🏅&nbsp;[leaderboards](leaderboards.md)</span>.

The prize table is without utilities (a <span class="gi-m">🪓&nbsp;[Wood Axe](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🔪&nbsp;[Skinning Knife](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span> or <span class="gi-m">⛏️&nbsp;[Mining Pick](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span> raises the scrap rows). A payout rolls its table once and pays at most one row, so the chances add up to at most 100%. The scrap rows come last: a utility makes them likelier and never takes anything from the other prizes (<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#rope)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#nail)</span>, the pet tokens and the credits keep their chances at every utility level). ## Spam control

From your **5th message within a minute** you get a warning; at the **8th** you're timed out for a day. Admins are exempt.

## Tips

- Talk in full sentences: five real words of four letters or more make a chat point.
