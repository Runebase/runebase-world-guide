# Chat points

You earn experience and prizes just by talking in the server.

- A message counts as a **chat point** when it has **5 or more words of at least 4 characters** that weren't in your last counted message. Short or repeated messages earn nothing. The same message sent twice at once counts once.
- Your **6th chat point within 12 hours** pays **12 experience** and a roll on the chat prize table, once, announced in the experience channel with your name. Later messages in those 12 hours only count chat points. When the 12 hours are over, a new round starts and pays again at its 6th point.
- **40 chat points in the last 30 days** make you an active chatter (a role); it goes again when old points drop out of the 30 days.
- Chat points count on the chatter [leaderboards](leaderboards.md).

The prize table is without utilities (a <span class="gi-m">🪓&nbsp;[Wood Axe](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🔪&nbsp;[Skinning Knife](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span> or <span class="gi-m">⛏️&nbsp;[Mining Pick](../games/expeditions.md#utilities-wood-axe-skinning-knife-mining-pick)</span> raises the scrap rows). A payout rolls its table once and pays at most one row, so the chances add up to at most 100%: with utilities the scrap rows grow and the rows after them shrink. `/loottables` shows each prize's real chance for you, 0% for one that can't drop. Every row of this table is played in the tests and pays exactly what it shows. This table adds up to exactly 100%: from the first utility level its <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope](../items/materials.md#rope)</span> shrinks, and with all three utilities at level 3 it can't drop any more (<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail](../items/materials.md#nail)</span> at level 16, the pet tokens from level 20 to 40).

## Spam control

From your **5th message within a minute** you get a warning; at the **8th** you're timed out for a day. Admins are exempt.

## Tips

- Talk in full sentences: five real words of four letters or more make a chat point.
