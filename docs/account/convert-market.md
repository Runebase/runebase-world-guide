# The convert market

`/convert` swaps scrap and dishes at fixed rates.

Pick what you want and what you pay with: the price is in the column of what you pay with. For example, **2 <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wood Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#wood-scrap)</span> buy 10 <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/anchovy-with-anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../items/consumables/fish-dishes.md#anchovy-with-anchovy-powder)</span>**, and **20 Anchovy with Anchovy Powder buy 1 Wood Scrap**. Each conversion moves exactly the listed amounts; without enough to pay, or without room in your pouch for scrap, nothing moves, and only you are told why (also when an older screen still shows the Convert button). A double click converts once.
