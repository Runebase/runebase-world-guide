# 🎁 Gifts

`/gift` sends credits to another member. How credits get in and out of the game is in <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/pouch.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Credits and RUNES](credits-and-runes.md)</span>.

The receiver gets exactly what you send, it's recorded on both sides, and nothing is created or lost. A gift reaches a member who never used the bot too.

Nothing moves for an amount that isn't valid (a gift's amount is the rule for [every amount you type](credits-and-runes.md#typing-an-amount): a plain number, rounded down to 8 decimals), a gift to yourself or to a bot, a member who can't be found, or more than you have, and only you see why (the receiver doesn't see it). A gift that goes through is shown in the channel as **🎁 Gift #N Success!**. Two gifts at the same time can never spend the same credits twice.
