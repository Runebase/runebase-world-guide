# 📚 The Info menu and the Hall of Honor

`/start` → **Info** has the bot's reference pages. Each page replaces the menu (privately if you play privately) and has **Main Menu** and a way back to the Info menu.

- **Game Guide:** this guide. **Airdrop Schedule:** the server's airdrops, on Hermes.
- **About RuneBase:** what Runebase is, its wallets, explorers and exchanges.
- **Network Info:** the Runebase network as the bot's node sees it: the block height, the block reward, the network's difficulty and weight, the coins in circulation and the estimated yearly staking return, with links to the website and the explorer, and the <span class="gi-m">🧱&nbsp;[super stakers](super-stakers.md)</span>.
- **Loot Tables:** the prize table of each activity, with each prize's real chance for you (your utilities can push the last prizes out). Chess has one table per difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard); a long table, like Loot Reels', comes in pages of 35 with **Next** and **Previous**.
- **Consumables:** one page per consumable, picked in its menu: what it does, and how many you hold.
- **Treasure Hunting:** how keys spawn and what the treasure hunter role gives (<span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[Piles and the key hunt](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md)</span>), with your own role's time left (each key you find adds 4 hours, up to 7 days).
- **Talkative:** how <span class="gi-m">🗣️&nbsp;[chat points](chat-points.md)</span> work, with your points this round, this month and all time.
- **Memeologist:** how <span class="gi-m">🎨&nbsp;[meme points](memes.md)</span> work, with your cooldown and your points.

A server that hasn't set up one of these roles or channels yet shows its name in words (the treasure hunter role, the treasure chest channel, the talkative and memeologist roles and their top 10 roles) instead of a mention.
- **Stellar (xRUNES):** **xRUNES LP** lists the xRUNES liquidity pools on Stellar, each pool's reserves and trustlines as Stellar has them (read every minute), and whether it earns the LP reward (checked every 4 hours; a check Stellar couldn't answer keeps the pool's last answer); the LP reward shares each payout by your shares in the pools as Stellar has them (set from Stellar once at the update, and for a pool new to the bot when it first appears, then followed through every deposit and withdrawal); **xRUNES Daily Trade Contest** ranks the day's xRUNES buyers, 10 a page: **Previous Day** goes back a day at a time, **Next Day** comes back up to today. A buy of xRUNES in one of the pools counts once for the contest (a sale of xRUNES doesn't count; nor does a buy paid with an asset whose check Stellar couldn't answer just then, and the next buy paid with it is checked again), and each pool trade is posted once in the Stellar market channel, up to a minute after it happens. A pool new to the bot counts and posts its trades from the moment the bot first sees it; its earlier trades aren't counted or posted.

## The Hall of Honor

`/start` → **Hall of Honor** thanks the people and tools behind the bot's art and code; **Donations** shows how to support its development, and **Back to Hall of Honor** returns.
