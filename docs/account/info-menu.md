# 📚 The Info menu and the Hall of Honor

`/start` → **Info** has the bot's reference pages. Each page replaces the menu (privately if you play privately) and has **Main Menu** and a way back to the Info menu.

- **Game Guide:** this guide. **Airdrop Schedule:** the server's airdrops, on Hermes.
- **About RuneBase:** what Runebase is, its wallets, explorers and exchanges.
- **Network Info:** the Runebase network as the bot's node sees it: the block height, the block reward, the network's difficulty and weight, the coins in circulation and the estimated yearly staking return, with links to the website and the explorer, and the <span class="gi-m">🧱&nbsp;[super stakers](super-stakers.md)</span>.
- **Loot Tables:** the prize table of each activity, with each prize's real chance for you (your utilities can push the last prizes out). Chess has one table per difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard); a long table, like Loot Reels', comes in pages of 35 with **Next** and **Previous**.
- **Consumables:** one page per consumable, picked in its menu: what it does, and how many you hold.
- **Treasure Hunting:** how keys spawn and what the treasure hunter role gives (<span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[Piles and the key hunt](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md)</span>), with your own role's time left (each key you find adds 4 hours, up to 7 days).
- **Talkative:** how <span class="gi-m">🗣️&nbsp;[chat points](chat-points.md)</span> work, with your points this round, this month and all time.
- **Memeologist:** how <span class="gi-m">🎨&nbsp;[meme points](memes.md)</span> work, with your cooldown and your points.

A server that hasn't set up one of these roles or channels yet shows its name in words (the treasure hunter role, the treasure chest channel, the talkative and memeologist roles and their top 10 roles) instead of a mention.
- **Stellar (xRUNES):** **xRUNES LP** lists the xRUNES liquidity pools on Stellar, each pool's reserves and trustlines as Stellar has them (read every minute), and whether it earns the LP reward (checked every 4 hours; a check Stellar couldn't answer keeps the pool's last answer), 10 pools a page (**Next**, **Previous**), a pool with no trustline left not listed; the LP reward shares each payout by your shares in the pools as Stellar has them (set from Stellar once at the update, and for a pool new to the bot when it first appears, then followed through every deposit and withdrawal); **xRUNES Daily Trade Contest** ranks the day's xRUNES buyers, 10 a page: **Previous Day** goes back a day at a time, **Next Day** comes back up to today. A buy of xRUNES in one of the pools counts once for the contest (a sale of xRUNES doesn't count; nor does a buy paid with an asset whose check Stellar couldn't answer just then, and the next buy paid with it is checked again), and each pool trade is posted once in the Stellar market channel, up to a minute after it happens. A pool new to the bot counts and posts its trades from the moment the bot first sees it; its earlier trades aren't counted or posted. A trade's post gives its price as the other asset per xRUNES, whichever side of the trade xRUNES is on.
- **The xRUNES rewards** are paid on Stellar to accounts with an xRUNES trustline, and posted in the Stellar payout channel with a link to each transaction (a button for each of the first 25, the others linked in the post's text, or, when there are too many to list, on the payout account's page on Stellar Expert): the **LP reward** every 4 hours (a third of the 0.3% fee on the last 4 hours' xRUNES buys, at most 50 xRUNES), shared by each holder's xRUNES in the rewarded pools (the share of a holder without a trustline isn't paid to anyone; with too little xRUNES in the payout account, nothing is sent, the post says so, and the 4 hours' buys wait for the next run); the **Daily Trade Contest** after midnight UTC (0.1% of the day's xRUNES buys, at most 100 xRUNES), shared by each buyer's volume among the buyers with a trustline, and run once a day (a day with too little trading to pay, or too little xRUNES in the payout account, is posted once and its buys count toward the next day's contest). A share under 0.0000001 xRUNES, the smallest amount Stellar can send, isn't paid. Each payment is sent once: a transaction is never signed twice, and one Stellar didn't confirm is looked up before anything is sent again. When a transaction isn't paid, the post says how much wasn't sent, the bot's admins are told and settle it, and the Daily Trade Contest's page for that day shows the payout as "(not paid: the admins were told)"; while its transaction is still being sent, as "(being sent)". If the bot stops in the middle of a payout, its next start looks each transaction up on Stellar: one that arrived counts as paid, one that didn't is never sent again by the bot, and the admins are told to settle it; a payout due in the few minutes before that's known waits for it, so its own transactions go through.

## The Hall of Honor
