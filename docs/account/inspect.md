# 🔍 Inspect

`/start` → **Activities** → **Inspect** lets you look at other players' Hack & Slash characters, if they allow it. Nothing is open by default: turn on Hack & Slash Inspection under **Inspect** → **Inspect Settings** to let others see your characters, their stats, skills and equipment. The equipment view lists every slot (Helm, Amulet, Main Hand, Off Hand, Armor, Gloves, both rings, Belt, Boots…) with the item worn there or "Empty Slot", under a picture of the character's gear.

## What others can see

Each part of your account has its own switch under **Inspect Settings**: Pouch, Basket, Buildings, Hack & Slash, Utilities, Fishing Gear, Hunting Gear and Crown of Chaos. When you pick someone in **Select a user!**, the screen lists what they allow, with an **Inspect** button for each: their pouch (credits, keys, cards and scrap), their basket, their buildings, utilities, fishing and hunting gear, and their Crown of Chaos points. **Back to Inspect** returns to them.

Every number on a tab is theirs, exactly: their credits to the last 0.00000001, their keys, cards and scrap with what their pouch holds at most, every item in their basket (every fish and meat is listed, 0 when they have none), the level of each building and utility, each rod and rifle with its enhancement and which one is equipped, their Crown of Chaos points, streaks and skill points, and, while they hold the Crown, its health out of its maximum (the same as the Crown of Chaos menu's "Crown HP"; until 2026-11-05 it said out of 300).

## Your whitelist

With **whitelist mode** on (**Settings** → Inspect Whitelist), only the members on your whitelist can inspect you; everyone else is told they're not on it and sees no Inspect buttons. **Inspect** → **Manage Whitelist** adds and removes members (up to 200) and shows whether whitelist mode is on.
