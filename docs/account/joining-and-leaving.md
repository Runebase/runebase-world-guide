# 👋 Joining and leaving the server

What happens when you join the server and when you leave it.

## Joining

A new member of the home server gets the inactive-chatter role and is welcomed in the welcome channel once verified; others can wave at them.

## Leaving

**Leaving the server costs 250 experience** (never below 0), announced in the server, and takes you off every <span class="gi-m">🏅&nbsp;[leaderboard](leaderboards.md)</span> and its <span class="gi-m">🎭&nbsp;[roles](roles.md#top-roles)</span> right away. Coming back puts you back in the server's lists: your <span class="gi-m">🥇&nbsp;[rank](ranks.md)</span> role, your scores on the boards and the top roles you rank for.
