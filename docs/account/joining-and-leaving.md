# Joining and leaving the server

What happens when you join the server and when you leave it.

## Joining

A new member of the home server gets the inactive-chatter role and is welcomed in the welcome channel once verified; others can wave at them.

## Leaving

**Leaving the server costs 250 experience** (never below 0), announced in the server, and takes you off every [leaderboard](leaderboards.md) and its [roles](roles.md#top-roles) right away. Coming back puts you back in the server's lists: your [rank](ranks.md) role, your scores on the boards and the top roles you rank for.
