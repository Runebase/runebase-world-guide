# Quests

Quests are long-running goals behind almost everything you do: a vote, a fishing trip, a cooked dish, a chess win, a heist all count toward a quest. When a quest reaches its goal it completes on its own, pays its experience and rewards, and starts its next level.

**Where:** `/quests` opens your quest log. Pick **Normal** or **Progressive**, then a quest: it shows what counts, your progress toward the goal, and the rewards of its next completion with their chances.

## Two kinds of every quest

Every quest exists twice, and what you do counts toward both:

- **Normal Repeatable:** the same goal and the same rewards at every level. Some rewards are certain, others have a chance (the tables below).
- **Progressive Repeatable:** every level asks more and pays more. The goal grows by 60% of the first level's goal a level and the experience and rewards by 40% (Runes Pattern and the Crown of Chaos quests: 50% and 30%). Every reward is certain. Level 10 of Vote asks 128 votes.

## Completing a quest

- When your progress reaches the goal, the quest completes by itself: its experience and rewards are paid **once**, the quest goes up a level, and the progress beyond the goal is kept for the next level.
- **Quests never reset.** A quest keeps its level and progress until you complete it: no new day, week or month starts it over.
- **One action can complete many levels:** a cooking batch of 1,500 dishes completed 52 levels, each paid as its level says. A quest completes at most **100 levels** in one action (normal and progressive alike); the rest stays as progress, and your next action that counts toward it goes on (601 Cooking levels waiting complete 100 a cook).
- A quest's experience comes on top of the action's own (a chess win that completes Chess - Win (Easy) pays the win's 15 and the quest's 50).
- **No single action pays more than 15,000 experience**, quests included, in every game alike; within it, a quest's experience is paid in full, never cut (a chess win, a Loot Reels spin and the Crown of Chaos used to cut an action at about 9,000). Nothing in the game comes close (a batch of 5,000 dishes pays about 9,300 with its quests), and 100 levels of a normal quest stay below it. An action that would still pass it is stopped whole: nothing is spent or paid, your quest progress stays, and the cooking table tells you.
- The action's result lists what its quests paid, in one message. Each quest shows once, with its levels together: "Quest Complete: Cooking #1-#20" (20 levels), their experience and rewards added up. At most 4 quests get a box of their own; with more, 3 do and the last box lists the others.
- The <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/nail-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail Factory](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> and the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/rope-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope Factory](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> show the quests a collect completes on the collect screen. The nightly production (your resource buildings' scrap, counted toward Scrapper) completes its levels without a message: you find them paid in `/quests` and in your pouch.
- **One action can complete several quests**, and each pays once: a fishing trip completes Fishing and each fish's quest, a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/heists.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[heist](../games/heists-and-clans/index.md)</span> completes quests for both players, the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/crown-of-chaos.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Crown of Chaos](../games/crown-of-chaos/index.md)</span> round for the Crown and every player whose bleed or doomseed hit. Their experience is added up with the action's own and paid once.
- A heist's public log lists both players' quests in their loot summaries, with a box of their own for those that fit in the message.
- **A reward your pouch has no room for** (keys, scrap, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](../items/keys-and-cards.md#mystery-card)</span>) **is not received**, and the result says so. When many levels complete at once and the pouch fills partway, the rewards that fit are received and the rest aren't: 15 levels of Reel of Fortune with room for 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gold Keys](../items/keys-and-cards.md#gold-key)</span> pay 2 and show the other 13 as not received; their credits are paid in full.
- Two actions at the same moment one short of the goal complete the quest once, and everything both did counts (a supply pile and the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/nail-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail Factory](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> collected together, two cooks at two fires).
- A restart in the middle of a trip's completion loses nothing: the trip completes at the next start, and its quests count and pay once.
- The Hnefatafl quests pay their rewards but no experience.
- Every player has every quest: players who joined before a quest existed get it at level 1 with this release, and keep the quests they had.

## What counts

Each quest's own text says what counts (the tables below). In short:

| You | Counts toward |
|---|---|
| go [fishing](../games/fishing.md) or [hunting](../games/hunting.md) | Fishing / Hunting: the trip's energy; the quest of each fish or animal: what you catch |
| go on an [expedition](../games/expeditions.md) | Expeditions and that expedition's quest: the expedition's seconds |
| [cook](../games/cooking.md) | Cooking and the recipe's quest: every dish, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/cuisine-cottage.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cuisine Cottage](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> extras included |
| [vote](community.md#votes), [bump](community.md#bumps) | Vote, Bump: each rewarded one |
| win a [quick battle](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md) | QuickBattle: 1, once |
| roll the [dice](../games/dice.md) | Dice: each paid roll |
| spin [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md) | Reel of Fortune: each spin |
| solve a [WordWeaver](../games/wordweaver.md) word | WordWeaver - Solved |
| win a [chess](../games/chess.md) or [Hnefatafl](../games/hnefatafl.md) game against the bot | the Win quest of that difficulty: each paid win |
| win [Runes Pattern](../games/runes-pattern.md) | Runes Pattern - Win |
| collect a key [pile](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) | the Treasure Hunt quest of that key |
| collect a scrap pile | the Scrapper quest of that scrap; your resource building's nightly scrap counts too |
| collect a supply pile | Collector - Nail / Rope: the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nails](../items/materials.md#nail)</span> or <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Ropes](../items/materials.md#rope)</span> you got; the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/nail-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail Factory](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> and the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/rope-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope Factory](buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> count what they make (28 nails, 7 ropes) |
| [attack the Crown of Chaos](../games/crown-of-chaos/attacking.md) | Damage Dealt: your attack's damage, and your bleeds' and seeds' damage in the rounds |
| [hold the Crown](../games/crown-of-chaos/holding-the-crown.md) | Points Earned: each point the Crown earns in a round |
| [heist](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md) | Experience Stolen (what you gained), Experience Lost (what the heist took: its full amount, even when you had less), Block, Recover (the attacker's energy kept: a defender never gets energy back from a counter-attack, so only your own attacks count), Counter-Attack |

What is refused counts nothing: a trip or a cook that doesn't start, a roll on cooldown or with the tipbot offline, the 16th spin of the day, a wrong guess, a lost game, a 6th chess or Hnefatafl win of the day, a vote within its 12 hours, a bump while the server is still bumped, a factory collect over the day's limit, a missed attack on the Crown. Credit piles and treasure chests count toward no quest.

## Tips

- Quests never get in the way: just play, and check `/quests` to see what's close to completing.
- Big actions (a long expedition, a large cooking batch) can complete several levels at once.
- Keep room in your pouch: a key or a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#mystery-card)</span> from a quest is lost when the pouch is full.
