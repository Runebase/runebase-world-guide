# Ranks, leaderboards and roles

Nearly everything you do pays **experience**. Experience gives you a **rank** in the realm, and ranks unlock building levels and more. Most games also have **leaderboards**, all-time and monthly, whose top players hold a role.

## Ranks

- Your rank is **the highest rank of the realm whose experience you have reached** (each server sets its own ranks). One big gain across several ranks takes you straight to the highest, with one announcement.
- `/ranks` shows your rank, your experience and how much is left to the next rank.
- Your rank comes with the rank's role; the role of your old rank goes. Rank changes are announced once, in the ranks channel: **Level Up!** ("You leveled up to …") or **Level Down!** ("You lost a level! You are now …").
- You can lose experience (heists, leaving the server, a deleted meme). Below the first rank's experience you are **Unranked**, without a rank role.
- Ranks unlock building levels ([Buildings](buildings.md)).

## Leaderboards

`/leaderboard` opens the experience leaderboard, with two menus to pick an all-time or a monthly board.

- Boards show the top players in score order; on a tie, the newer Discord account comes first (the key hunt ladder puts whoever got there first). Only players with points are listed, and only players who are in the server.
- **Monthly boards count the last 30 days:** every point you earn leaves the monthly board about 30 days later. (The key hunt ladder counts the calendar month, and so does the heist ladder on `/heistboard`.)
- The **Monthly Accelerator** board counts your experience gains of the last 30 days, minus what you lost.

## Top roles

Many boards give a role to their **top 10** players (the experience board also to the top 20 and top 30). When you pass someone, the role moves to you; when you drop out, it goes to the next player.

- A board with fewer than 10 players who scored gives its role only to those who scored.
- After a restart, the roles match `/leaderboard` again.
- Leaving the server takes you off the boards at once, and your top roles go to the next players. Points you'd lose while away don't put you back; coming back does.

## The role panel

`/rolepanel` shows the roles you can give yourself, one at a time (pick one in **Select Role**). Press **Earn** to take it or **Remove** to give it back. Some roles need a minimum amount of experience: below it, the panel shows the role with the button off. Only the player who opened the panel can use its buttons.

## Tips

- `/ranks` tells you how far the next rank is.
- Monthly boards forget points after 30 days: keep playing to keep your place.
