# 🥇 Ranks

Nearly everything you do pays **experience**. Experience gives you a **rank** in the realm, and ranks unlock building levels and more.

- Your rank is **the highest rank of the realm whose experience you have reached** (each server sets its own ranks). One big gain across several ranks takes you straight to the highest, with one announcement.
- `/ranks` (or `/start` → **Ranks** → **Ranks**) shows your rank, your experience and how much is left to the next rank, on the page of the realm's ranks that holds yours: **10 ranks a page**, with **Previous** and **Next** through all of them.
- Your rank comes with the rank's role; the role of your old rank goes. Rank changes are announced once, in the ranks channel: **Level Up!** ("You leveled up to …") or **Level Down!** ("You lost a level! You are now …").
- You can lose experience (heists, [leaving the server](joining-and-leaving.md#leaving), a deleted <span class="gi-m">🎨&nbsp;[meme](memes.md)</span>). Below the first rank's experience you are **Unranked**, without a rank role.
- **The Active role** (in a server that has one): earning experience gives it, for 24 hours from your latest experience. When the 24 hours end, the role goes and the experience channel says so (**Lost … Role**); in a server without an experience channel it goes unannounced. Losing experience by [leaving the server](joining-and-leaving.md#leaving) doesn't give it; leave while you hold it and come back within your 24 hours, and the bot's daily check (04:35 UTC, or its next start) gives it back, announced.
- Ranks unlock building levels (<span class="gi-m">🏗️&nbsp;[Buildings](buildings.md)</span>).

## Tips

- `/ranks` tells you how far the next rank is.
