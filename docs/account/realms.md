# 🌍 Realms

A **realm** is a Discord server the Runebase bot plays in. The **Runebase Discord server** is the home realm, and every partner server that added the bot is a realm too. Everything in this guide happens inside one realm: the server you're in when you type a command or press a button.

## One account in each realm

Joining a realm's server, or your first message there, opens your account in that realm: a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/pouch.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[credits](credits-and-runes.md)</span> wallet, a pouch, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/quest.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[quests](quests.md)</span> and daily energy. Play in two servers and you have two accounts, each with its own:

- experience and <span class="gi-m">🥇&nbsp;[rank](ranks.md)</span> (each server sets its own ranks and rank roles);
- credits: withdrawals, deposits and credits bought with Hermes go from and to the wallet of the realm you're in, and <span class="gi-m">🎁&nbsp;[gifts](gifts.md)</span> go to members of that realm;
- pouch (keys, scrap, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span>), <span class="gi-m">🧺&nbsp;[basket](basket.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🏗️&nbsp;[buildings](buildings.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🐾&nbsp;[pets](pets.md)</span>, quests and daily energy;
- <span class="gi-m">👹&nbsp;[Hack & Slash](../games/hack-and-slash/index.md)</span> character, with its items, stash and <span class="gi-m">🔨&nbsp;[auction house](../games/hack-and-slash/auction-house.md)</span> listings;
- <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/heists.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[heist](../games/heists-and-clans/index.md)</span> role and clan;
- places on the <span class="gi-m">🏅&nbsp;[leaderboards](leaderboards.md)</span>, the monthly boards and the ladders, and the <span class="gi-m">🏷️&nbsp;[roles](roles.md)</span> they give;
- `/settings`.

Nothing moves from one realm to another by itself. Leaving a realm's server costs 250 experience there and takes you off its boards; your account stays and is yours again when you come back (<span class="gi-m">👋&nbsp;[Joining and leaving](joining-and-leaving.md)</span>).

## What every realm has of its own

- **Its own <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/crown-of-chaos.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Crown of Chaos](../games/crown-of-chaos/index.md)</span>:** one Crown, its pool and its weekly ladder.
- **Its own <span class="gi-m">🗿&nbsp;[Jotnar](../games/hack-and-slash/jotnar.md)</span> giants**, fought by that server's players.
- **Its own <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](store.md)</span>**, with its own stock.
- **Credit piles** every hour in its channels (<span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[Piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md)</span>).
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/bronzekey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md)</span>**, opened and dealt with the keys and <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span> of that realm's pouch, in its own chest and scratch card channels (a realm whose admins haven't set them opens and deals none).
- **Its own RUNES wallet**, kept by the tipbot (Hermes): a [RUNES prize](credits-and-runes.md#runes-prizes) (<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/diceroll.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[dice](../games/dice.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/bronzekey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[chests](../games/treasure-chests.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md)</span>, the Crown's reward) is a share of the wallet of the realm you play in, so a realm whose wallet is empty (1 RUNES or less) pays no RUNES: everything else of those games is paid as usual.
- **Its own channels and roles** for each game, set by its admins.

## Only in the Runebase server

- Treasure keys, scrap piles and building supply piles appear only there (credit piles appear in every realm).
- <span class="gi-m">✉️&nbsp;[Invite](invites.md)</span> rewards are counted only there.
- New members are welcomed there (the inactive-chatter role and the welcome channel).
