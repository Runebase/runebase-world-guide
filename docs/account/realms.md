# 🌍 Realms

A **realm** is a Discord server the Runebase bot plays in. The **Runebase Discord server** is the home realm, and every partner server that added the bot is a realm too. Everything in this guide happens inside one realm: the server you're in when you type a command or press a button.

## One account in each realm

Joining a realm's server, or your first message there, opens your account in that realm: a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/pouch.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[credits](credits-and-runes.md)</span> wallet, a pouch, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/quest.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[quests](quests.md)</span> and daily energy. Play in two servers and you have two accounts, each with its own:

- experience and <span class="gi-m">🥇&nbsp;[rank](ranks.md)</span>: each server has its own ranks, and the rank roles you get are that server's;
- credits: each realm's wallet has its own deposit address (**Pouch → Buy Credits** shows the one of the realm you're in, and RUNES or Credits sent to it land in that realm's wallet only; **Recent Deposits** lists that address's deposits); withdrawals and credits bought with Hermes go from and to the wallet of the realm you're in; a <span class="gi-m">🎁&nbsp;[gift](gifts.md)</span> is paid from your account in the realm you send it in, to the receiver's account in that realm, and only to a member of that server (anyone else is refused, and nothing moves);
- pouch (keys, scrap, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span>), <span class="gi-m">🧺&nbsp;[basket](basket.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🏗️&nbsp;[buildings](buildings.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🐾&nbsp;[pets](pets.md)</span>, quests and daily energy;
- <span class="gi-m">👹&nbsp;[Hack & Slash](../games/hack-and-slash/index.md)</span> character, with its items, stash and <span class="gi-m">🔨&nbsp;[auction house](../games/hack-and-slash/auction-house.md)</span> listings: a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/class/warrior.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Warrior](../games/hack-and-slash/classes/warrior.md)</span> in one server, you pick a class again in the other;
- <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/heists.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[heist](../games/heists-and-clans/index.md)</span> role and clan: joining the heist in one server doesn't put you in another's, and each server's role panel sets the experience it asks, counted in that server;
- <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/diceroll.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[dice](../games/dice.md)</span> cooldown: a roll in one server doesn't stop you rolling in another;
- places on the <span class="gi-m">🏅&nbsp;[leaderboards](leaderboards.md)</span>, the monthly boards and the ladders, and the <span class="gi-m">🏷️&nbsp;[roles](roles.md)</span> they give;
- `/settings`.

What you earn in one realm leaves your account in the other as it was: nothing moves from one realm to another by itself. Leaving a realm's server costs 250 experience there and takes you off its boards, and only there; your account stays and is yours again when you come back (<span class="gi-m">👋&nbsp;[Joining and leaving](joining-and-leaving.md)</span>).

## What every realm has of its own

- **Its own <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/crown-of-chaos.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Crown of Chaos](../games/crown-of-chaos/index.md)</span>:** one Crown, its pool and its weekly ladder.
- **Its own <span class="gi-m">🗿&nbsp;[Jotnar](../games/hack-and-slash/jotnar.md)</span> giants**, posted in its Hack & Slash channel and fought by that server's players.
- **Its own <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](store.md)</span>**, with its own stock: what you pay goes to that realm's store, and the 5% on top to that realm's Crown pool.
- **Every kind of pile** (<span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[Piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md)</span>): treasure keys, scrap, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#rope)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#nail)</span> and credit piles, on the same schedule in every realm, in channels its verified members can see. What you collect goes to your account there, with that realm's key hunt, 4-hour key windows and treasure hunter role.
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/bronzekey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md)</span>**, opened and dealt with the keys and <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span> of that realm's pouch, in its own chest and scratch card channels (a realm whose admins haven't set them opens and deals none).
- **Its own RUNES wallet**, kept by the tipbot (Hermes): a [RUNES prize](credits-and-runes.md#runes-prizes) (<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/diceroll.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[dice](../games/dice.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/bronzekey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[chests](../games/treasure-chests.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md)</span>, the Crown's reward) is a share of the wallet of the realm you play in, so a realm whose wallet is empty (1 RUNES or less) pays no RUNES: everything else of those games is paid as usual. RUNES are paid only where the realm's tipbot is connected: today the Runebase server; the other realms are connected after the update, and until then they pay no RUNES the same way (the screens say "RUNES prizes aren't connected in this server yet").
- **Its own <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/hodl.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[hodler roles](hodlers.md)</span>**, with its own balances; the same linked address can hold a role in every server you play in.
- **Its own channels and roles** for each game, set by its admins: a dice roll outside the dice channel is posted in that realm's dice channel, rank changes in its ranks channel.
- **Its own welcome:** a realm whose admins set a welcome channel welcomes its newly verified members there, by the server's own name.
- **Its own admins:** a server's admins (Discord's Administrator permission) set its roles and channels with `/config`; that's all an admin of a server can do with the bot. Spawning piles and the maintenance and test commands belong to the bot's own operator.

A server that adds the bot or is switched on as a realm plays at once: its commands appear, and chatting and joining count from then on.

## Partner roles

A member of both the Runebase server and a partner server gets the partner's role in the Runebase server, and the Runebase server's role in the partner server where one is set up, each announced in that server's experience channel. Leaving the partner server takes them back.

## Only in the Runebase server

- <span class="gi-m">✉️&nbsp;[Invite](invites.md)</span> rewards are counted only there: joining a partner server through an invite counts for nobody.
- New members get the inactive-chatter role there only, and only its staff see who joined.
