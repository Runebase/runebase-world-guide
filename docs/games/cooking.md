# ![Cooking Fire](../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Cooking

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

The fire and the cooking table turn the fish and meat in your basket, with spices and sauces from the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../account/store.md)</span>, into dishes: buffs for fishing, hunting, expeditions, the heists, Crown of Chaos and Hack & Slash.

## The fire and cooking

1. `/fire` costs **one <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wood Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#wood-scrap)</span>** and starts a fire in the channel for **10 minutes**. Starting a fire pays **3 experience** and counts on the fire leaderboards. Without wood, or in a channel not every verified member can see and write in, no fire starts, nothing is spent and only you are told why (also from **Activities** → **Cooking Fire** → **Start Fire**).
2. **Add Fuel** costs another <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wood Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#wood-scrap)</span>, adds 10 minutes and pays 3 experience. Without wood, you're told so privately and nothing is spent.
3. **Cook** opens your private cooking table. Pick a recipe and an amount: it uses exactly the recipe's ingredients × the amount from your basket, gives you the dishes, pays **1 experience per dish** and moves your cooking <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/quest.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[quests](../account/quests.md#every-quest)</span>. Without the ingredients, or with an amount that isn't valid, nothing is spent, and the table says why.

The **<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/cuisine-cottage.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cuisine Cottage](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>** building gives each dish a 1% per level chance of one more; an extra dish pays its experience and counts for your cooking quests like the others. Cooking at two fires at the same moment works: both cooks are paid, and your quests count every dish. A fire that went out while the bot was offline is put out when the bot starts; its old buttons spend nothing.
