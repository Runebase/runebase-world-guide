# Attacking the Crown

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

**Attack The Crown**, pick an **offensive rune** (each option shows its value in this reign) and, if you like, an **attack skill**, then attack.

- **Each attack costs one <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relic](../../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#chaos-relic)</span>** (2 credits in the store; you can also **Buy <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relic](../../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#chaos-relic)</span>** from the Crown of Chaos menu, and 5% of what you pay goes into the pool). Without a relic the attack button is off. A double click spends one relic once.
- **Then you wait** for the cooldown before your next attack (the bot says "Attack Cooldown"; nothing is spent). The cooldown is the Crown's 15 s, plus the Crown's <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/fractured-time.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Fractured Time](skills/defensive/fractured-time.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/temporal-nexus.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Temporal Nexus](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>, minus your <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/blood-rush.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Blood Rush](skills/offensive/blood-rush.md)</span>, and **never under 3 seconds**.
- The Crown can't attack itself.

## Runes and damage

In a reign, each of the 24 runes has a value from 1 to 24 (the menus and the attack reports show it). **Every reign draws new values:** when a new Crown takes over, the 24 values are shuffled again, so a rune that was strong last reign may be weak now. The Crown has a **current defensive rune**, and every attack faces it:

- Your damage comes from how many steps your rune's value is above the Crown's rune's value (a value of 24 against 14 is 10 steps; 24 against 22 is 2): **3 damage per step**. <!-- Production's damage per step; the tests run with 50. -->
- The counting goes on past 24 and starts again at 1, so **a rune valued below the Crown's still hits**: 9 against 10 is 23 steps, the most there is, and 3 against 10 is 17.
- **The same value misses:** "The crown defended without taking damage!" The relic is still spent.
- After every attack (a hit, a miss or a dodge) the Crown's rune moves on to the next one in its list. Each public attack report shows both runes with their values.
- On top of the steps come your extra damage (<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/brutal-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Brutal Strike](skills/offensive/brutal-strike.md)</span>, the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/white-tailed-deer-with-juniper-berry-demi-glace.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[White-tailed Deer](../../items/consumables/meat-dishes.md#white-tailed-deer-with-juniper-berry-demi-glace)</span> [buff](buffs.md), [pets](../../account/pets.md#perks)), less the Crown's damage reduction (the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/blackened-tilapia-with-cumin-slaw.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Blackened Tilapia](../../items/consumables/fish-dishes.md#blackened-tilapia-with-cumin-slaw)</span> [buff](buffs.md), pets), multiplied by the Crown's handicap. A pet's perks count only while it's awake and well cared for ([Pets](../../account/pets.md)): the challenge screen shows your pet's extra damage range and attack cooldown reduction (you wait the Crown's cooldown less it), and every pet's attacking perks are checked in play at those numbers.
- The Crown can **dodge** (<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/veil-of-shadows.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Veil of Shadows](skills/defensive/veil-of-shadows.md)</span>, pets): a dodged attack deals nothing but still costs the relic.

## Mana and attack skills

You have **1,000 mana**, and you get 10 back every round (more with <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/abyssal-mana-tide.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Abyssal Mana Tide](skills/offensive/abyssal-mana-tide.md)</span>), up to 1,000. Only the attack skill you pick costs mana, once. Without enough mana, the attack goes on without the skill and costs no mana.

- **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/brutal-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Brutal Strike](skills/offensive/brutal-strike.md)</span>:** extra damage on the hit.
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/bleed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bleed](skills/offensive/bleed.md)</span>:** on a hit, the Crown bleeds for some rounds; each round's bleed damage counts as your damage. A new bleed of yours replaces your old one.
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/doomseed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Doomseed](skills/offensive/doomseed.md)</span>:** on a hit, plants a seed that detonates after its rounds (fewer with <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/premature-bloom.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Premature Bloom](skills/offensive/premature-bloom.md)</span>); **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/thornburst.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Thornburst](skills/offensive/thornburst.md)</span>** gives a detonation a chance to make the Crown bleed too (not a detonation the Crown's Bubble absorbs). A new seed of yours replaces your old one, and its rounds start again.
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/robin-hood.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Robin Hood](skills/offensive/robin-hood.md)</span>:** on a damaging hit, steals a share of the pool and splits it evenly among the reign's attackers still in the server (everyone who attacked this reign, a miss too, but not players who have left the server; nobody gets anything when the share would be under one unit each). **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/black-hood.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Black Hood](skills/offensive/black-hood.md)</span>:** steals a share for you alone. Each steals exactly its share; the rounding remainder stays in the pool.
- **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/silence.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Silence](skills/offensive/silence.md)</span>:** on a hit, the Crown can't use Resurgence, Blood Purge, Defuse or Mana Drain for the silence's duration (Bubble still works).
