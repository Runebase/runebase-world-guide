# Crown of Chaos buffs

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

Eat them in **Crown Buffs**; each one you eat is a charge (at most 500 held). **One charge is used on every attack, whether it hits, misses or is dodged**; none when the attack bounces off a bubble or waits for a cooldown. The Crown Buffs screen shows the charges you hold.

| Buff | Effect, per charge | Cooked from |
|---|---|---|
| [Blackened Tilapia with Cumin Slaw](../../items/dishes.md#blackened-tilapia-with-cumin-slaw) | 15 less damage from an attack on you as the Crown | 2 [Tilapia](../../items/ingredients.md#tilapia) + 6 [Cumin](../../items/ingredients.md#cumin) |
| [White-tailed Deer with Juniper Berry Demi-Glace](../../items/dishes.md#white-tailed-deer-with-juniper-berry-demi-glace) | +15 damage on your attack | 2 [White-tailed Deer](../../items/ingredients.md#white-tailed-deer) + 2 [Juniper Berry Demi-Glace](../../items/ingredients.md#juniper-berry-demi-glace) |
