# Holding the Crown

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

## The Crown's skills

Only the Crown has these buttons. They cost nothing but their cooldown; clicking one again while it cools down changes nothing. When one can't be used (it cools down, there's nothing to remove, the Crown is silenced), the reason goes to you alone; the menu stays as it is.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| Resurgence | heals 3% of the maximum health each of the next 10 rounds, never above the maximum; then waits an hour |
| Blood Purge | removes every bleed on the Crown, whoever cast it (seeds stay). With no bleed on the Crown it's refused (only you see why) and stays ready |
| Defuse | removes every seed waiting to detonate. With none, it's refused ("No detonations to defuse", only you see it) and stays ready |
| Mana Drain | while it lasts, every hit on the Crown (not a miss) drains 10% of the attacker's mana, never below 0 |
| Bubble | while it lasts, attacks bounce off for free, bleeds on the Crown deal 0 but still run down, and a seed that detonates deals nothing and starts no <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/thornburst.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Thornburst](skills/offensive/thornburst.md)</span> bleed (it's used up, and nothing of it hits after the Bubble). Remove Bubble ends it early. Then it waits an hour |

Their base cooldowns and durations are on the [overview](index.md#how-a-reign-works); what the defensive skills change, on their <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-tree/defensive.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[pages](skills/defensive/index.md)</span>.

### Checked in play

- **Resurgence:** Heals 3% of the maximum health each of the next 10 rounds (30 at 1,000; 33 with <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/throne-of-endurance.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Throne of Endurance](skills/defensive/throne-of-endurance.md)</span> 1; 90 with Throne 20), never above it. Ready again in an hour, and the answer says when; +20 on the weekly ladder.
- **Blood Purge:** Removes every bleed, whoever cast it, and leaves the seeds; +5 a bleed it removed. Then it waits 1,200 s, less <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/urgent-embind.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Urgent Embind](skills/defensive/urgent-embind.md)</span>'s 45 s a rank, and its answer says until when. With no bleed on the Crown it's refused, to the Crown alone, and stays ready. A bleed that has ticked its last round is gone at once, and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/hemostasis.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Hemostasis](skills/defensive/hemostasis.md)</span> keeps one with no rounds from being put on; one with no rounds left from an older reign counts for nothing (refused when it's the only one).
- **Defuse:** Removes every seed, whoever planted it, and leaves the bleeds; +5 a seed it removed. Then it waits 900 s, less <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/swift-disarmament.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Swift Disarmament](skills/defensive/swift-disarmament.md)</span>'s 30 s a rank, and its answer says until when. With no seed it's refused, to the Crown alone, and stays ready. A seed is gone once it detonates; one left from an older reign with no rounds counts for nothing (refused when it's the only one).
- **Mana Drain:** For 300 s (plus <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/siphons-grasp.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Siphon's Grasp](skills/defensive/siphons-grasp.md)</span>) each hit drains 10% of the attacker's 1,000 mana after the skill's own mana, never more than is left (the message says what it took); a miss or a dodge drains nothing. +2 a drained hit, also one that found no mana left. Ready again after 45 minutes.
- **Bubble:** For 300 s every attack bounces off, whatever its skill: no relic, mana, buff charge or cooldown spent, nothing applied or stolen, nothing on the weekly ladder. The round goes on under it: bleeds and seeds deal 0 and run down (a seed is spent, no Thornburst), regeneration, a Resurgence, mana, points and tax piles go on. +15; ready again in an hour; Remove Bubble ends it.
- **Silenced:** When an attacker's <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/silence.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Silence](skills/offensive/silence.md)</span> lands, Resurgence, Blood Purge, Defuse and Mana Drain are refused (to the Crown alone), even from an old button, and stay ready; a Resurgence or Mana Drain already running goes on; the Bubble works.

## Defensive runes

In **Defensive Runes**, pick up to 24 runes (the first becomes your current rune), move them up or down, and set the current one. Each line shows the rune's value in the running reign. While you hold the Crown, each attack faces your current rune and then moves it to the next in your list. A change you make while an attack is under way waits for that attack and is kept. Each attack report shows the Crown's defense streak (the attacks it has survived this reign), and your best streak is kept.

## Pets

Your active pet's holding perks count while you hold the Crown, it's awake and its stats meet the perks' conditions ([Pets](../../account/pets.md#perks)): extra maximum health (flat or %), more health regenerated every 5th point (or every 4th with the Shadow Hound), a dodge chance, less damage from each hit, a longer wait for each attacker, a stronger [Resurgence](#the-crowns-skills) (the Phoenix Chick: 4.25% a round). The Crown menu shows each with its number, and a new reign starts at your maximum health with your pet's bonus. Asleep, they add nothing; your pet's attacking perks don't help you hold the Crown. Checked in play for every pet.
