# 📊 The round and the reign

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

## The round, every minute

- The Crown gets **1 point**: on the reign, on the Crown of Chaos leaderboards (all-time and monthly, with their top-10 roles) and on the weekly ladder. Every 5th point it regenerates health.
- Every attacker's points shrink by 0.35%, and every attacker gets mana back.
- <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/bleed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bleeds](skills/offensive/bleed.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/doomseed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[seeds](skills/offensive/doomseed.md)</span> on the Crown run; their damage counts for whoever cast them. A bleed or a detonation can end the reign.
- Sometimes the round drops a **tax pile** (3% of the pool) in the Crown of Chaos channel, collected like a credit pile. A hit can drop one too (1% of the pool). Each tax pile is for **3, 4 or 5 players** (drawn for each pile, the pile says how many), who get equal shares, rounded down.
- **The handicap:** once the first 30 minutes of a reign are over, every round adds 1000% ÷ 10,080 to the Crown's handicap (up to 1,000% after 7 days), and every attack's damage is multiplied by (1 + handicap). A former Crown's handicap shrinks at a quarter of that speed. The attack report (and a bleed's or seed's message) shows the extra damage: none when nothing was dealt (a dodge, a miss, or a hit the Bubble absorbed). The handicaps are reset once a week.

## Points

Your **points** in a reign are the damage you dealt (bleeds and detonations included), shrinking a little every round. **The final blow adds 200 points.** The **Attack Score Board** ranks the attackers by points (and shows their damage): that's the order of the draw for the next Crown and of the top-three RUNES shares.

## The end of a reign

When an attack or a round's bleed brings the Crown to 0:

1. **The next Crown is drawn** among everyone with points in this reign who is still in the server: each player's chance is exactly their share of all the points. The draw is posted with its roll and each player's range (300 points of 750 is "0.00-299.99", the next player's starts at 300.00, and so on); a player with 0 points has no chance. The final blow's 200 points help, but don't crown you by themselves. When nobody still here has points, the realm's default Crown takes it.
2. **The old Crown gets the whole pool** in credits, once (the one prize not capped at 25,000 credits). The pool starts again at 0.
3. **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/currency/runes.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[RUNES](../../account/credits-and-runes.md#runes-prizes)</span>** (a share of the realm's RUNES wallet, tipped): the old Crown gets **0.000032407% per point**, counting at most **19,999 points**; the final blow gets 3.5% of that, and the top three by points still in the server 5%, 3.5% and 1.5% of it (someone who left the server gets no share, and the next one moves up).
4. **Experience:** the old Crown gets 0.85 × its points, at most **8,999**; a <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/quest.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[quest](../../account/quests.md)</span> completed in the same round (Points Earned) pays its experience on top.
5. The Crown role moves to the new Crown, who starts with a seizure bubble.

- Two final blows at the same moment (a round and an attack, two attackers, a double click) end the reign once and pay the pool once.
- If the tipbot is unreachable, the reign still ends and the pool is paid; no RUNES are tipped or promised. Without the realm's tipbot session, the RUNES are recorded as not paid.

## A Crown who leaves the server

A Crown who leaves the server **loses the reign without any reward**: until the next round, attacks on them are refused (nothing is spent); then the round ends the reign, the next Crown is drawn from the attackers still in the server, and the pool stays for the next reign.

## The pool

The pool grows with **5% of every store purchase** in the realm and the **unclaimed shares of expired credit piles**. It shrinks by <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/robin-hood.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Robin Hood](skills/offensive/robin-hood.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/crown-skill/black-hood.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Black Hood](skills/offensive/black-hood.md)</span> steals and the tax piles (a tax pile takes its credits from the pool only when it appears: one that can't be posted, or a restart before it appears, leaves them in the pool). The old Crown gets what's left when the reign ends.
