- **Learn it from:** your rank 12

- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Reduced chance of dropping tax CreditPiles when getting hit.

## What it does

Lowers the chance that a hit on you drops a tax pile from the pool (5% without it, 0.2% at rank 20).

The Crown's own skills and runes: [holding the Crown](../../holding-the-crown.md); the minute round: [the round and the reign](../../round-and-reign.md).

## Checked in play

- A damaging hit drops a tax pile (1% of the pool) on a roll under 4.76% at rank 1 and under 0.2% at rank 20, not above.
