**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Defensive skills](index.md) (for the Crown: they work while you hold it)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 12
- **Needs first:** [Iron Veins](iron-veins.md)
- **Opens:** [Urgent Embind](urgent-embind.md)

> In the game: Reduce Bleeding rounds

## What it does

Takes its rounds off every bleed on you as the Crown (a bleed of 5 rounds keeps 4 against rank 1).

The Crown's own skills and runes: [holding the Crown](../../holding-the-crown.md); the minute round: [the round and the reign](../../round-and-reign.md).
