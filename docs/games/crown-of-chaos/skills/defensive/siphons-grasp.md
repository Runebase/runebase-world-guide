# ![Siphon's Grasp](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/siphons-grasp.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Siphon's Grasp

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-tree/defensive.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Defensive skills](index.md)</span> (for the Crown: they work while you hold it)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 24
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Extends the duration of Mana Drain, prolonging its sapping effect on attackers.

## What it does

Your Mana Drain lasts 300 seconds plus 15 a rank.

The Crown's own skills and runes: [holding the Crown](../../holding-the-crown.md); the minute round: [the round and the reign](../../round-and-reign.md).

## Checked in play

- Mana Drain lasts 315 s at rank 1 and 600 s at rank 20 (300 without): a hit 2 s before its end is drained, one 1 s after it isn't.
