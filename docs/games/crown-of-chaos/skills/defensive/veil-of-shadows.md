**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Defensive skills](index.md) (for the Crown: they work while you hold it)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 1
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Fractured Time](fractured-time.md), [Grip of Stability](grip-of-stability.md)

> In the game: Chance to dodge the target.

## What it does

Gives you its % chance to dodge an attack as the Crown (11.5% at rank 20); a dodged attack deals nothing but still costs the attacker the relic.

The Crown's own skills and runes: [holding the Crown](../../holding-the-crown.md); the minute round: [the round and the reign](../../round-and-reign.md).
