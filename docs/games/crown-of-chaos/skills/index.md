# Crown of Chaos skills

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

## Skill points

Each skill tree (offensive and defensive) has **your rank + 30 points**. A skill holds at most 20. Add points with **Add 1 Skillpoint** or the amount box; a skill needs your rank to reach its level and its prerequisite skills (the menu says what's missing). **Reset Skills** takes back every skill of both trees for one **[Forgotten Throne Elixir](../../../items/store-consumables.md#forgotten-throne-elixir)** (8,000 credits in the store), once.

What each skill does at its rank is what its screen shows:
