# Abyssal Mana Tide

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Offensive skills](index.md) (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 1
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Channels a steady tide of mana from the abyss, increasing mana regeneration to fuel relentless attacks.

## What it does

You get 10 mana back each round, plus 1 a rank, up to 1,000.

Every attack costs one [Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic) and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).
