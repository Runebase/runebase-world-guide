**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Offensive skills](index.md) (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** an attack skill: you pick it for an attack ([mana and attack skills](../../attacking.md#mana-and-attack-skills))
- **Learn it from:** your rank 18
- **Needs first:** [Robin Hood](robin-hood.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Always steal from accumulated crown taxes and keeps them for yourself.

## What it does

On a damaging hit it steals its share of the pool for you alone; it steals exactly its share, the rounding remainder stays in the pool. A Black Hood final blow takes the thief's share out before the old Crown is paid the rest.

Every attack costs one [Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic) and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).
