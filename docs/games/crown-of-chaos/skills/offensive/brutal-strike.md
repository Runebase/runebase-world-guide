# ![Brutal Strike](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/brutal-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Brutal Strike

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-tree/offensive.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Offensive skills](index.md)</span> (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** an attack skill: you pick it for an attack ([mana and attack skills](../../attacking.md#mana-and-attack-skills))
- **Learn it from:** your rank 1
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/blood-rush.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Blood Rush](blood-rush.md)</span>

> In the game: Increases Physical Damage

## What it does

Adds its shown range to your hit (1-4 at rank 1, 20-23 at rank 20) and costs its shown mana once. Without enough mana the attack lands without it and costs no mana.

Every attack costs one <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic)</span> and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 adds 1-4 to your hit (the lowest roll 1, the highest 4) for 1 mana; rank 20 adds 20-23 for 29 mana, once a hit. The attack screen shows it as "Brutal Strike [💙29]" with "Increase Damage 20-23". With 28 mana at rank 20 the hit lands without it and costs no mana.
- With the White-tailed Deer and the Crown's Blackened Tilapia in the same hit, each counts once.
