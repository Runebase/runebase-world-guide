# Offensive skills

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

The Crown of Chaos skills for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown. Each name leads to the skill's page, with every rank.

| Skill | Kind | Needs | Rank 1 | Rank 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Brutal Strike](brutal-strike.md) | attack skill | your rank 1 | +1-4 extra damage; 1 mana | +20-23 extra damage; 29 mana |
| [Abyssal Mana Tide](abyssal-mana-tide.md) | passive | your rank 1 | +1 mana a round | +20 mana a round |
| [Blood Rush](blood-rush.md) | passive | your rank 6, [Brutal Strike](brutal-strike.md) | -1 s attack cooldown | -20 s attack cooldown |
| [Bleed](bleed.md) | attack skill | your rank 12, [Blood Rush](blood-rush.md) | 1-3 bleed damage a round; 3 bleed rounds; 10 mana | 20-41 bleed damage a round; 12 bleed rounds; 48 mana |
| [Doomseed](doomseed.md) | attack skill | your rank 12 | 5-25 detonation damage; 15 rounds to detonation; 10 mana | 100-215 detonation damage; 15 rounds to detonation; 95 mana |
| [Premature Bloom](premature-bloom.md) | passive | your rank 18, [Doomseed](doomseed.md) | -1 rounds to detonation | -11 rounds to detonation |
| [Thornburst](thornburst.md) | passive | your rank 24, [Doomseed](doomseed.md), [Bleed](bleed.md) | 5% chance to bleed on detonation | 100% chance to bleed on detonation |
| [Robin Hood](robin-hood.md) | attack skill | your rank 12 | 0.025% of the pool, shared among the attackers; 5 mana | 0.5% of the pool, shared among the attackers; 24 mana |
| [Black Hood](black-hood.md) | attack skill | your rank 18, [Robin Hood](robin-hood.md) | 0.0035% of the pool, to you; 5 mana | 0.07% of the pool, to you; 24 mana |
| [Silence](silence.md) | attack skill | your rank 24 | 35 s silence; 25 mana | 415 s silence; 500 mana |
