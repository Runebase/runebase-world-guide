# ![Premature Bloom](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/premature-bloom.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Premature Bloom

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Offensive skills](index.md) (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 18
- **Needs first:** [Doomseed](doomseed.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Manipulate the unstable energy of Doomseed, reducing its detonation timer. The bloom comes sooner… and deadlier.

## What it does

Takes its rounds off the 15 rounds your seeds wait before they detonate.

Every attack costs one [Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic) and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).
