# ![Premature Bloom](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/premature-bloom.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Premature Bloom

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-tree/offensive.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Offensive skills](index.md)</span> (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 18
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/doomseed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Doomseed](doomseed.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Manipulate the unstable energy of Doomseed, reducing its detonation timer. The bloom comes sooner.

## What it does

Takes its rounds off the 15 rounds your seeds wait before they detonate.

Every attack costs one <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#chaos-relic)</span> and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).

## Checked in play

- Your seeds detonate after 14 rounds at rank 1 and 4 at rank 20, never sooner, with the seed's own damage: <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/doomseed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Doomseed](doomseed.md)</span> 20's highest roll is still 215. Learned alone it plants nothing.
