# Thornburst

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** [Offensive skills](index.md) (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 24
- **Needs first:** [Doomseed](doomseed.md), [Bleed](bleed.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Enhances Doomseed by adding a chance to trigger a Bleed effect upon detonation. Enemies struck by the seed suffer damage over time as their wounds fester and bleed uncontrollably.

## What it does

Gives each detonation of your seeds its chance to make the Crown bleed too (5% at rank 1). A seed the Bubble absorbs starts no bleed.

Every attack costs one [Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic) and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).
