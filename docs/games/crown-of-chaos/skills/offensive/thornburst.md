# ![Thornburst](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/thornburst.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Thornburst

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-tree/offensive.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Offensive skills](index.md)</span> (for attackers: they work when you attack the Crown)
- **Kind:** passive: always on
- **Learn it from:** your rank 24
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/doomseed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Doomseed](doomseed.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/bleed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bleed](bleed.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill

> In the game: Enhances Doomseed by adding a chance to trigger a Bleed effect upon detonation. Enemies struck by the seed suffer damage over time as their wounds fester and bleed uncontrollably.

## What it does

Gives each detonation of your seeds its chance to make the Crown bleed too (5% at rank 1). A seed the Bubble absorbs starts no bleed.

Every attack costs one <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relic](../../../../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic)</span> and faces the Crown's rune ([runes and damage](../../attacking.md#runes-and-damage)).

## Checked in play

- At rank 1 a detonation bleeds on a roll under 5% (0.0499 did, 0.0501 didn't); at rank 20 always. The bleed is your own <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../assets/icons/crown-skill/bleed.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bleed](bleed.md)</span>'s (1-3 for 3 rounds at rank 1, 20-41 for 12 at rank 20), costs no mana even with none left, and first ticks the round after. A seed without Thornburst rolls nothing.
- A seed the Crown's Bubble absorbs starts no bleed.
