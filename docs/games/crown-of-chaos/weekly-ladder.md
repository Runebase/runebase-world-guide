# The weekly ladder

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

Every ISO week (Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday) has its own Crown of Chaos ladder. It scores what you did that week (the total is rounded down):

| Each … this week | Scores |
|---|---|
| Crowns Taken | 50 |
| Points Earned | 1 |
| Resurgences | 20 |
| Bleeds Purged | 5 |
| Defuses | 5 |
| Mana Drains | 2 |
| Silences | 10 |
| Bubbles | 15 |
| Attacks | 1 per 4 |
| Defends | 1 per 4 |
| Final Blows | 50 |

A [Silence](skills/offensive/silence.md) counts only when it lands: one that misses or is dodged scores nothing. Points Earned are the points you earn as the Crown; the Crown's skills count when used (Resurgences, Bubbles), per bleed purged or seed defused, and per hit its Mana Drain drained.

- On Monday (UTC), last week's **top 3** get the CoC Winners role.
- A tie goes to whoever reached the score first; players who left the server are left out.
- When the staff turn the weekly payouts on (on the Runebase server), last week's positive scores share **1% of the realm's RUNES wallet** by rank, once:

| Rank | Shares of the pool, each |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 5 |
| 5 | 4 |
| 6 to 10 | 3 |
| 11 to 20 | 2 |
| 21 to 50 | 1 |
| 51 to 100 | 0.5 |
