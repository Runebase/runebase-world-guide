# Classes

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

There are 7 classes. **Each class is its own character**, with its own attributes, skills, gear, inventory and condition; switching class and back keeps everything. Your **level is your rank** in the realm, the same for every class.

A new character gets its class's attributes and its full health, mana and stamina (what the class screen shows), and its class's weapon: a Short Sword for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/warrior.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Warrior](warrior.md)</span> and the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/paladin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Paladin](paladin.md)</span>, a Javelin for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/hunter.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Hunter](hunter.md)</span>, a Short Staff for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/wizard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wizard](wizard.md)</span>, a Katar for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/assassin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Assassin](assassin.md)</span>, a Wand for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/necromancer.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Necromancer](necromancer.md)</span> and a Club for the <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/class/druid.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Druid](druid.md)</span>. All but the Wizard and the Necromancer also get a Buckler.
