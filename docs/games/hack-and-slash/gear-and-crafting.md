# 👕 Gear, items and crafting

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

## Condition, repairs and gear

- **Condition:** at 00:00 UTC the class you have selected refills its health, mana and stamina; your other classes keep what they had. **<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/pheasant-with-blackberry-balsamic-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Pheasant with Blackberry Balsamic Glaze](../../items/consumables/meat-dishes.md#pheasant-with-blackberry-balsamic-glaze)</span>** fills your stamina to its maximum.
- **Wear:** every round there's a 5% chance one of your equipped items loses 1 durability, and 1.5% with every monster attack, whether it hits you or not (leggings too).
- **Repairs:** 1 credit per missing durability point on a Normal item, 2 on an Exceptional one, 3 on an Elite one (a Circlet 1.11). The cost shown, item by item or all at once, is exactly what you pay; durability goes back to the maximum. Without the credits, nothing changes.
- **Equipment:** equipping puts the item in its slot and the replaced one back in your inventory (a two-handed weapon also takes off the shield). Gear you're not strong, nimble or experienced enough for stays in your inventory; the strength and dexterity from your other gear and its active inscriptions count toward a requirement (not the item's own). Gear you wear but no longer meet the requirements of (after an attribute reset, taking off a +strength item, or losing levels) gives nothing and shows red on the equipment screen until you meet them again: after a reset, spend your points again. Every bonus an item shows counts while you wear it ([item bonuses](#item-bonuses)).
- **Inventory:** destroy an item, disassemble it into one crafting material of its quality and tier, or keep it in your stash. The inventory's and the stash's item menus jump straight to the item you pick.
- **Dropping an item:** **Drop** asks first, privately or publicly. A public drop is posted in the channel with a **Loot** button: the first other player who clicks it gets the item in their inventory (the post then says who), and anyone after them is told it's gone.
- **Stash upgrades:** each stash level holds one more item. **Upgrade Stash** (on the stash screen) shows the next level's cost in <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wood Scrap](../../items/materials/scrap.md#wood-scrap)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nails](../../items/materials/building-supplies.md#nail)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope](../../items/materials/building-supplies.md#rope)</span>, and **Preview Stash** shows any level's cost and size, level by level or by typing a level ("..."); the upgrade takes exactly the cost shown.
- **Item Info** (Equipment → Item Info): the catalog of every item base (with its picture), every magic and rare modifier and every inscription, written as an item's own screen writes it ("Strength Requirement: 25", "Armor: 3 - 5", "+10-20% Enhanced Defense"), with the range each number rolls in, and where it can appear ("Appears on"), its drop level, tier, crafting scrap and repair cost. Page with Previous and Next, or jump ("...") to a page number or a name.
- The <span class="gi-m">🏗️&nbsp;[buildings](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/training-grounds.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Training Grounds](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/arcane-library.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Arcane Library](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/healing-spring.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Healing Spring](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/mana-well.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mana Well](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/fitness-foundry.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Fitness Foundry](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/travelers-camp.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Traveler's Camp](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> make your characters stronger; the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/arcane-reliquary.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Arcane Reliquary](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span> makes Magic and Rare drops likelier.

## Crafting

`/craft` (or Equipment → Craft):

- **Craft Items:** pick the type, family, item and quality: an item costs **3 crafting materials and 1 scrap**, goes into your inventory and pays 1 experience. Without the materials, the button is off.
- **Convert Crafting Materials:** 3 of a material make 1 of the next tier, for a small credit fee (3 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/standard-essence.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Standard Essence](../../items/materials/hack-and-slash-crafting-materials.md#standard-essence)</span> → 1 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/elemental-core.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Elemental Core](../../items/materials/hack-and-slash-crafting-materials.md#elemental-core)</span> for 0.3 credits).
- **Inscriptions:** a Magic inscription costs 1 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/engraving-tool.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Engraving Tool](../../items/materials/hack-and-slash-crafting-materials.md#engraving-tool)</span>, a Rare one 3. It's drawn from the inscriptions your item's type and your level can get, each stat rolled in its range, and added inactive; activate it to make it the item's inscription (one at a time). Pick an inscription in the item's inscription menu to activate or **Remove** it; removing asks first ("No, go back!" keeps it) and leaves the item's other inscriptions as they are. Its bonuses then count like the item's own, its armor even on a ring or an amulet, its block on a shield.

## Item bonuses

What an item's screen says is what it does: each number below was checked in play, made from the game's own data, worn, and in a battle round. The item's screen shows the number it rolled; your stats screen moves by exactly that number while you wear it, and back when you take it off. A broken item (durability 0) gives nothing until it's repaired.

### An item's own numbers

| Field | What it does | Checked in play |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | A weapon's damage is your regular attack's physical damage (two katars add up for an Assassin); boots' is your kick's, a shield's your smite's. | yes (baseStats, inBattle) |
| Throw damage | A javelin's or throwing weapon's throw damage is what your throws deal. | yes (baseStats) |
| Armor | Adds to your armor: monsters hit you less often and armor takes armor ÷ (armor + damage) × 95% off their physical hits (at most 85%). | yes (baseStats, inBattle) |
| Block | A shield's (and a Necromancer's shrunken head's) block becomes block × (dexterity − 15) ÷ (2 × level), at most 50%: a monster's physical hit is blocked under it. | yes (baseStats, inBattle) |
| Durability | Wears 1 at a time in battle; at 0 the item gives nothing until repaired. Bows, crossbows, javelins, throwing weapons, rings and amulets never wear. | yes (baseStats, battleFormulas) |
| Strength requirement | You can't equip it with less strength (your other gear and its inscriptions count, not the item's own bonus). Worn gear whose requirement you no longer meet (after an attribute reset, or taking off a +strength item) gives nothing and shows red on the equipment screen until you meet it again. | yes (hackSlashItemBonuses, baseStats) |
| Dexterity requirement | The same with dexterity. | yes (hackSlashItemBonuses, baseStats) |
| Level requirement | You can't equip it below that level (magic and rare modifiers can raise it); worn below it (levels can drop: heists, leaving the server), it gives nothing and shows red. | yes (hackSlashItemBonuses, baseStats) |
| Two handed | Equipping it takes off your shield; a shield takes it off. | yes (hackSlashInventory) |
| Stack | Shown on javelins and throwing weapons; nothing uses it up. | yes (baseStats) |
| Repair cost | A repair costs the missing durability × the item's tier (Normal 1, Exceptional 2, Elite 3) × this (only the Circlet: 1.11). | yes (battleFormulas) |
| Tier | Normal 1, Exceptional 2, Elite 3: the repair cost per point and the auction fee. | yes (battleFormulas) |
| Tier name | Normal, Exceptional or Elite. | yes (baseStats) |
| Drop level | Drops from monsters of this level and up. | yes (battleLoot) |
| Crafting scrap | The scrap crafting the item costs. | yes (hackSlashCrafting) |
| Sockets | Not used: some bases list 1–6 sockets in the data, but no item gets them and no screen shows them. | yes (baseStats) |
| Slots | Not used: belts list slots in the data, but no item gets them and no screen shows them. | yes (baseStats) |
| Name | The item's name. | yes (baseStats) |
| Family | The item's family (the crafting menu's second step). | yes (baseStats) |
| Type | Its slot, the class that can wear it, and the modifiers and inscriptions it can get. | yes (baseStats) |
