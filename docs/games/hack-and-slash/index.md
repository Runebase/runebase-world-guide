# Hack & Slash

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

The bot's role-playing game: pick a [class](classes/index.md), fight [**quick battles**](quick-battles.md) against monsters, bring down the [**Jotnar**](jotnar.md) giants together with the server, and duel other players in the [**Arena**](arena.md). You learn [skills](skills/index.md), collect [gear, craft items](gear-and-crafting.md) and trade them at the [auction house](auction-house.md).

**Where:** `/hackslash` opens the Hack & Slash menu (quick battles, stats, skills, the Arena), `/equipment` your gear and inventory, `/craft` crafting.

## Tips

- Dexterity and attack-rating gear make you hit more often; a pure strength build misses more.
- Pick monsters near your level: those more than 5 levels below you give no experience.
- Use your free resets after the release to rebuild your characters for the new hit chance.
- Keep an [Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../../items/dishes.md#anchovy-with-anchovy-powder) for when a battle goes wrong.
