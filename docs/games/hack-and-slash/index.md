# 🗡️ Hack & Slash

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

The bot's role-playing game: pick a <span class="gi-m">🎭&nbsp;[class](classes/index.md)</span>, fight <span class="gi-m">⚔️&nbsp;[**quick battles**](quick-battles.md)</span> against monsters, bring down the <span class="gi-m">🗿&nbsp;[**Jotnar**](jotnar.md)</span> giants together with the server, and duel other players in the <span class="gi-m">🥊&nbsp;[**Arena**](arena.md)</span>. You learn <span class="gi-m">💥&nbsp;[skills](skills/index.md)</span>, collect <span class="gi-m">👕&nbsp;[gear, craft items](gear-and-crafting.md)</span> and trade them at the <span class="gi-m">🔨&nbsp;[auction house](auction-house.md)</span>.

**Where:** `/hackslash` opens the Hack & Slash menu (quick battles, stats, skills, the Arena), `/equipment` your gear and inventory, `/craft` crafting.

## Tips

- Dexterity and attack-rating gear make you hit more often; a pure strength build misses more.
- Pick monsters near your level: those more than 5 levels below you give no experience.
- Use your free resets after the release to rebuild your characters for the new hit chance.
- Keep an <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/anchovy-with-anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../../items/consumables/fish-dishes.md#anchovy-with-anchovy-powder)</span> for when a battle goes wrong.
