- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: A charged attack that drains life and mana from the victim every 3rd charge.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Life and mana steal give you their % of the damage dealt.
- Every third use releases its charged effect, then it starts over.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A charged strike, 2 mana each: the third steals 40% of its damage as life and as mana (135% at rank 20), then the count starts over.
