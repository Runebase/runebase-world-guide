# Dragon Flight

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)
- **Tree:** [Martial Arts](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Dragon Tail](dragon-tail.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Makes Your Dragon Tail kicks explosive and adds fire damage

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
