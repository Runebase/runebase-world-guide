- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill

- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: A fire attack that puts the target on fire.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Your hit plus its fire (6-10 at rank 1, 101-105 at rank 20), and a burn that ticks the round it lands and once a round after: 9-15 for 3 rounds at rank 1, 161-167 for 7 rounds at rank 20. 2 mana.
