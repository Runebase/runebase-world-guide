# ![Phoenix Strike](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/assassin/phoenix-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Phoenix Strike

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)
- **Tree:** [Martial Arts](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Cobra Strike](cobra-strike.md), [Blades of Ice](blades-of-ice.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: A finishing move that deals fire, lightning and cold damage all at once on the 3rd charge

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Every third use releases its charged effect, then it starts over.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
