**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/assassin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)</span>

- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/assassin/cobra-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cobra Strike](cobra-strike.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Attacks an enemy and deal increased damage on 3th charge.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Every third use releases its charged effect, then it starts over.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every third strike adds its damage bonus to the weapon damage, then it starts over.
