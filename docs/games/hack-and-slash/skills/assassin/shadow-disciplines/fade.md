- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Partially phases the Assassin to another plane increasing all magical resistances (at most 70% in total).

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It raises the five magical resistances (fire, cold, lightning, poison, magic), as its screen says, not physical damage; it can't take a resistance past 70%: the limit counts after the buffs.
