**Where to play:** Discord

- **Kind:** a debuff
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/assassin/cloak-of-shadows.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cloak of Shadows](cloak-of-shadows.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Stuns the enemy and lowers their armor

## How it works

- It stays on the monster for its rounds and keeps the monster's other debuffs. Cast again (or the same debuff from another source), it never stacks: the stronger of the two stays and its rounds refresh, a weaker cast never lowers it.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.

## Checked in play

- No damage: the monster you selected is stunned at once and for its rounds (2 at rank 1, the round it's cast included; 4 at rank 20) and meanwhile loses 20% of its armor (77%). It costs no mana.
