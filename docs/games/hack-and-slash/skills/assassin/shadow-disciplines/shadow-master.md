# Shadow Master

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)
- **Tree:** [Shadow Disciplines](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Shadow Warrior](shadow-warrior.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: increases the shadow warriors damage by % and adds rounds

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
