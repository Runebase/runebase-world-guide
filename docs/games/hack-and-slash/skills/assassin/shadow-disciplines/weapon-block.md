- **Weapon:** [a claw](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)

> In the game: Allows an Assassin to Block when using two claw-type weapons.

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).

## Checked in play

- It adds to the block from your gear, up to 75% (62.5% at rank 20 with claws).
