# ![Blade Sentinel](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/assassin/blade-sentinel.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Blade Sentinel

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)
- **Tree:** [Traps](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Blade Fury](blade-fury.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Throws a razor sharp blade that flies back and forth between the Assassin and her enemies.

## How it works

- It shoots a volley: "volley N" is N hits, the first on the monster you selected, the others on random living monsters.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- 3 blades of 6-10 physical (9 of 82-86 at rank 20), with your strength bonus but not your weapon's damage; the first on the monster you selected, the others on random living monsters, each rolling to hit. 2 mana.
