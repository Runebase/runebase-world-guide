> In the game: Drops a trap that shoots lightning at nearby foes.

## How it works

- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A trap that strikes one monster with 1-24 lightning (1-309 at rank 20) each round for 4 rounds (42), the round it's set included. 2 mana.
