- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Drops a small web that deals lightning damage to anything nearby.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its lightning damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes its lightning: 1-10 at rank 1, 1-143 at rank 20. 2 mana.
