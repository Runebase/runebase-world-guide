**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Assassin](../../../classes/assassin.md)
- **Tree:** [Traps](index.md)
- **Kind:** a summon, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Fire Blast](fire-blast.md)
- **Opens:** [Blade Fury](blade-fury.md), [Wake of Inferno](wake-of-inferno.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: The Assassin drops a trap at her location that throws exploding firebombs, dealing explosive fire damage.

## How it works

- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A trap that throws a fire bomb of 5-10 (81-105 at rank 20) at a monster each round, exploding on the monsters next to it, for 4 rounds (42), the round it's set included. 2 mana.
