- **Kind:** an exploding attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/cyclone-armor.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cyclone Armor](cyclone-armor.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 3 at rank 20

> In the game: A spout of ice that has a chance to freeze enemies.

## How it works

- It explodes: the monster it hits takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster you selected takes its cold twice (the blast and its explosion), each monster next to it once: 5-10 at rank 1, 119-162 at rank 20. Each of them may be frozen, at its own roll (15%, 63%), for 2 rounds (4), the round it lands included. 3 mana.
