# ![Armageddon](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/armageddon.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Armageddon

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Elemental Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Volcano](volcano.md), [Hurricane](hurricane.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: Small meteors fall from the sky around the Druid.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- Its fire damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes 88-110 fire (563-623 at rank 20) and burns 25-48 a round (310-333) for 2 rounds (6), the round it lands included; each may be stunned, at its own roll (10%, 48%). 2 mana, 40 at rank 20.
