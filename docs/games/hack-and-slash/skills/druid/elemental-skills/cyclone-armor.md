# Cyclone Armor

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Elemental Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Arctic Blast](arctic-blast.md)
- **Opens:** [Twister](twister.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Covers the druid in protective forces that absorb magical damage and damages melee attackers

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
