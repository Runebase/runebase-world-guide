- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/molten-boulder.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Molten Boulder](molten-boulder.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: A Firestorm that damages all the enemies

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its fire damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes its fire: 3-6 at rank 1, 60-120 at rank 20 (a spell: not your weapon's damage). 2 mana.
