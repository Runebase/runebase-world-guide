# ![Hurricane](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/hurricane.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Hurricane

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Elemental Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Tornado](tornado.md)
- **Opens:** [Armageddon](armageddon.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Manipulates a strong force of wind to surround your character damaging the enemies each round.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- For 15 rounds (72 at rank 20), from the round after you cast it, every monster takes 23-30 physical (308-391) each round, each may be stunned at its own roll (3%, 18%). 2 mana.
