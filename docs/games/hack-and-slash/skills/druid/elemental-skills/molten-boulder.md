- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Lunges a molten boulder at a foe that explodes in impact

## How it works

- It explodes: the monster it hits takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion.
- Its fire damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster you selected takes the boulder and its explosion, each monster next to it the explosion: 6-15 fire each at rank 1, 101-243 at rank 20. 2 mana.
