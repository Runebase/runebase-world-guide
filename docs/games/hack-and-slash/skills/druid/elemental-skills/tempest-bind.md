- **Kind:** a debuff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 3 at rank 20

> In the game: Binds a monster in a swirling tempest, stunning it for the rounds shown.

## How it works

- It stays on the monster for its rounds and keeps the monster's other debuffs. Cast again (or the same debuff from another source), it never stacks: the stronger of the two stays and its rounds refresh, a weaker cast never lowers it.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The selected monster doesn't attack for the rounds shown (1 at rank 1).
