# ![Feral Rage](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/feral-rage.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Feral Rage

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Shape Shifting Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Werewolf](werewolf.md)
- **Opens:** [Rabies](rabies.md), [Fire Claws](fire-claws.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Leeches life from enemies and adds damage %

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Life and mana steal give you their % of the damage dealt.
- It needs the Werewolf or Werebear shape: without it, the button makes the regular attack.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Without the Werewolf shape it makes the regular attack, for free.
