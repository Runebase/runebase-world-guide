# ![Fury](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/fury.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Fury

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Shape Shifting Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a chain attack
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Rabies](rabies.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 9 at rank 20

> In the game: Viciously attack enemies with chained attacks.

## How it works

- "targets N" hits N times, hopping between the monsters.
- It needs the Werewolf or Werebear shape: without it, the button makes the regular attack.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- In Werewolf shape: your hit, chained to 2 monsters (8 at rank 20), at +0% (+285%). 3 mana, 9 at rank 20.
