# ![Lycanthropy](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/lycanthropy.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Lycanthropy

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Shape Shifting Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Werewolf](werewolf.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Increases the Life of the Druid while Shapeshifted, as well as the rounds of the transformation.

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
