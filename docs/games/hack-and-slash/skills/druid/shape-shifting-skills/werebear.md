**Where to play:** Discord

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/druid.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)</span>

- **Kind:** a shapeshift, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/maul.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Maul](maul.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Transforms the Druid into a Werebear.

## How it works

- It turns you into its shape for its rounds; the Druid skills that need the shape work only then.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The Werebear shape for 10 rounds (105 at rank 20): +20% defense (210%), +3% damage (60%) and +10% health (105%: a hit takes that much less of your health). 2 mana.
