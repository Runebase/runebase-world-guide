# ![Werewolf](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/druid/werewolf.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Werewolf

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Shape Shifting Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a shapeshift, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Lycanthropy](lycanthropy.md), [Feral Rage](feral-rage.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Transforms the Druid into a Werewolf.

## How it works

- It turns you into its shape for its rounds; the Druid skills that need the shape work only then.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Cast first, it lets the shape skills (Feral Rage, Hunger, …) work.
