**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Summoning Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a summon, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Carrion Vine](carrion-vine.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Summons a poisonous vine that attacks enemies and adds a damage over time poison debuff.

## How it works

- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A vine with 50 health (1,000 at rank 20) for 15 rounds (72); each round it poisons a monster: 2-4 a round (40-80) for 3 rounds. 2 mana.
