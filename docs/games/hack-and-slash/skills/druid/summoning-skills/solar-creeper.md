# Solar Creeper

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Druid](../../../classes/druid.md)
- **Tree:** [Summoning Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Carrion Vine](carrion-vine.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Adds life, rounds and chance for an explosive attack to your Poison Creeper

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
