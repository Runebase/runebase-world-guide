- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)

- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Summons a grizzly bear whose blows can stun enemies.

## How it works

- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A grizzly with 1,500 health (4,350 at rank 20) whose blows of 25-40 physical (215-458), with your strength bonus, stun at 5% (24%), for 15 rounds (72). 2 mana.
