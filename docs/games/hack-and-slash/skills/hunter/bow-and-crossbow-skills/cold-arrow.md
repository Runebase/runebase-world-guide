# ![Cold Arrow](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/cold-arrow.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Cold Arrow

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)
- **Tree:** [Bow and Crossbow Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Ice Arrow](ice-arrow.md), [Guided Arrow](guided-arrow.md)
- **Weapon:** [a bow](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 3 at rank 20

> In the game: Enchants an arrow, converts damage to cold and adds chance to freeze your enemy.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
