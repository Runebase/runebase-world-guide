# ![Freezing Arrow](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/freezing-arrow.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Freezing Arrow

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)
- **Tree:** [Bow and Crossbow Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an exploding attack
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Guided Arrow](guided-arrow.md), [Ice Arrow](ice-arrow.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** [a bow](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 3 at rank 20

> In the game: Enchants an arrow to deliver cold damage that has a chance to freeze any monsters near the point of impact.

## How it works

- It explodes: the monster it hits takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Your arrow with its cold on the monster you selected, the cold on each monster next to it (40-50 at rank 1, 230-240 at rank 20); each of them may be frozen, at its own roll of the freeze chance (40%, 78%). 3 mana.
