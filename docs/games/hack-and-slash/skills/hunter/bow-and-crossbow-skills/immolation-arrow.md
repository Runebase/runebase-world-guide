- **Weapon:** [a bow](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Enchants an arrow with fire that puts the target on fire.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- Its fire damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Your arrow plus its fire (20, 115 at rank 20), and a burn that ticks the round it lands and then once a round: 13-25 for 2 rounds at rank 1, 260-272 for 8 rounds at rank 20. 2 mana.
