# ![Fend](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/fend.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Fend

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/hunter.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/javelin-and-spear-skills.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Javelin and Spear Skills](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** an area attack
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/impale.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Impale](impale.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** [a javelin and a spear or javelin](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: strikes all enemy targets.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes your thrust at its damage bonus: +70% at rank 1, +260% at rank 20. 2 mana, 40 at rank 20.
