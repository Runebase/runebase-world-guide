# ![Jab](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/jab.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Jab

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)
- **Tree:** [Javelin and Spear Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Power Strike](power-strike.md), [Impale](impale.md)
- **Weapon:** [a javelin and a spear or javelin](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: Attack multiple times in succession, releasing a flurry of jabs.

## How it works

- It shoots a volley: "volley N" is N hits, the first on the monster you selected, the others on random living monsters.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Three thrusts, the first on the monster you selected, the others on random living monsters: each at −15% at rank 1, +42% at rank 20. 2 mana at rank 1, 40 at rank 20.
