# Decoy

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)
- **Tree:** [Passive and Magic Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a summon
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Slow Missiles](slow-missiles.md)
- **Opens:** [Valkyrie](valkyrie.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates a double, confusing foes.

## How it works

- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
