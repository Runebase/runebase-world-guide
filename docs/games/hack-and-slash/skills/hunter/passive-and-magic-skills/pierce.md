# ![Pierce](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/pierce.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Pierce

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/hunter.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/passive-and-magic-skills.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Passive and Magic Skills](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/penetrate.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Penetrate](penetrate.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Grants a chance for your missile attacks to continue through its victim.

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
