# ![Slow Missiles](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/hunter/slow-missiles.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Slow Missiles

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Hunter](../../../classes/hunter.md)
- **Tree:** [Passive and Magic Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Inner Sight](inner-sight.md)
- **Opens:** [Decoy](decoy.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: reduces the damage of incoming missile attacks.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
