# Skills

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

Each class has three skill trees.

- **Skill points:** one per level, and the default Throw skill holds one, so a level-3 character has 2 to spend. You can't spend more than you have, and a skill takes at most 20 points.
- A skill needs its level and the skills listed before it (each skill's page says which).
- **Reset:** 5 [Grilled Sardines with Sumac Yogurt Sauce](../../../items/dishes.md#grilled-sardines-with-sumac-yogurt-sauce) in total, whatever the points; it leaves only the base skills.
- **Using skills:** on the battle screen you pick a main and a secondary skill; each is a button with its mana cost ("[Bonk](warrior/combat-skills/bonk.md) [💙2]"), charged once when used. A skill you can't use (not enough mana, not in the right shape, or without the weapon it needs, like [Magic Arrow](hunter/bow-and-crossbow-skills/magic-arrow.md) without a bow) shows as **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
- **What a skill does at each rank is what its skill screen shows**: the battle uses the same numbers.

## Free resets at the release

Every character that existed before this release gets **one free attribute reset and one free skill reset**: the reset screen says **free**, nothing is spent, and it's used once (also on a double click). After it, resets cost their steaks and sardines again. New characters don't get them.

## How skills work in battle

| Kind | What happens (examples the tests play) |
|---|---|
| attack | hits the selected monster ([Bonk](warrior/combat-skills/bonk.md) adds its flat damage, then its %) |
| area | hits every monster ([Cleave](warrior/combat-skills/cleave.md)) |
| volley | several hits, after the first on random living monsters ([Strafe](hunter/bow-and-crossbow-skills/strafe.md) rank 1: 3 arrows) |
| chain | "targets N" hits N times, hopping between monsters ([Guided Arrow](hunter/bow-and-crossbow-skills/guided-arrow.md) rank 1: 4 hits) |
| explodes | the target takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion ([Fire Ball](wizard/fire-spells/fire-ball.md)) |
| burn, poison | ticks once a round for its rounds ([Inferno](wizard/fire-spells/inferno.md) "rounds 4" ticks 4 times; [Fire Wall](wizard/fire-spells/fire-wall.md) burns every monster) |
| buff | on you for its rounds ([Battle Cry](warrior/warcries/battle-cry.md) +35% damage, [Battle Orders](warrior/warcries/battle-orders.md) +40% health, [Strategic Shout](warrior/warcries/strategic-shout.md) +40% attack rating, [Cloak of Shadows](assassin/shadow-disciplines/cloak-of-shadows.md) dodge and avoid, [War Cry](warrior/warcries/war-cry.md) parry) |
| debuff | on the monster ([Amplify Damage](necromancer/curses/amplify-damage.md): +15% physical damage taken; [Inner Sight](hunter/passive-and-magic-skills/inner-sight.md): −40 armor on every monster; [Howl](warrior/warcries/howl.md): −10% armor). Casting it again replaces it |
| aura | works while it's your main or secondary skill ([Might](paladin/auras/might.md) +20% physical damage, [Holy Fire](paladin/auras/holy-fire.md) burns every monster, [Concentration](paladin/auras/concentration.md) mana steal) |
| heal | Paladin [Heal](paladin/sanctified-protector/heal.md): 10% of your maximum health |
| stun | a stunned monster skips its attack ([Tornado](druid/elemental-skills/tornado.md); a [Screech](warrior/warcries/screech.md) stun lasts the round; [Tempest Bind](druid/elemental-skills/tempest-bind.md) for its rounds) |
| shapeshift | [Werewolf](druid/shape-shifting-skills/werewolf.md) and [Werebear](druid/shape-shifting-skills/werebear.md): the Druid skills that need the shape fall back to the regular attack without it |
| summon | a minion fights at your side for its rounds ([Clay Golem](necromancer/summoning-spells/clay-golem.md) rank 1: 275 health, 10 rounds; [Raise Skeleton](necromancer/summoning-spells/raise-skeleton.md) needs a corpse) |
| charges | every third use releases the charged effect ([Tiger Strike](assassin/martial-arts/tiger-strike.md)) |
| passive | always on (some only with their weapon) |

[Mirror](warrior/combat-skills/mirror.md) (Warrior) copies your attack at its own damage bonus (−50% at rank 1). Paladin [Cleansing](paladin/sanctified-protector/cleansing.md) removes your debuffs and heals 5%.
