- **Kind:** a debuff on every monster, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/life-tap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Life Tap](life-tap.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/decrepify.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Decrepify](decrepify.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Lower all magical resistances of the foe

## How it works

- It stays on every monster for its rounds; casting it again replaces it and keeps the monster's other debuffs.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It lowers the five magical resistances (fire, cold, lightning, poison, magic), never physical. On you (in the Arena) it lowers your whole resistance before the 70% limit, and nothing stops it below 0.
- In a quick battle, at rank 20, your magic hits deal 48% more and your weapon hits as hard as without it.
