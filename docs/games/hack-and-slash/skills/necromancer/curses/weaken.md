# Weaken

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Necromancer](../../../classes/necromancer.md)
- **Tree:** [Curses](index.md)
- **Kind:** a debuff on every monster, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Amplify Damage](amplify-damage.md)
- **Opens:** [Terror](terror.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Decrease Damage the damage dealth by your enemies

## How it works

- It stays on every monster for its rounds; casting it again replaces it and keeps the monster's other debuffs.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
