- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/bone-wall.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bone Wall](bone-wall.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates a protective shield of bone that absorbs damage from physical damage.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- For its rounds (5 at rank 1, 43 at rank 20) a shield takes the physical damage of monster hits before your health, up to 30 points (315 at rank 20); used up, it's gone.
