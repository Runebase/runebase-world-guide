- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Summons a bone missile that damages the enemy

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its magic damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster you selected takes its magic damage: 20-40 at rank 1, 210-420 at rank 20.
