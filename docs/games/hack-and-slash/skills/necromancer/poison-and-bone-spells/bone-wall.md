# Bone Wall

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Necromancer](../../../classes/necromancer.md)
- **Tree:** [Poison and Bone Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Bone Armor](bone-armor.md)
- **Opens:** [Bone Prison](bone-prison.md)
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Adds magical damage absorption to Bone Armor

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
