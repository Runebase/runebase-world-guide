# ![Poison Nova](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/poison-nova.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Poison Nova

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Necromancer](../../../classes/necromancer.md)
- **Tree:** [Poison and Bone Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Poison Explosion](poison-explosion.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: A ring of poison explodes from the Necromancer.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- Its poison damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster is poisoned from that round: its poison damage every round for its rounds (35-62 for 3 rounds at rank 1, 377-632 for 5 at rank 20).
