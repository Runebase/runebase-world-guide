# ![Teeth](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/teeth.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Teeth

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/necromancer.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Necromancer](../../../classes/necromancer.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/poison-and-bone-spells.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Poison and Bone Spells](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/necromancer/corpse-explosion.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Corpse Explosion](corpse-explosion.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Summons multiple projectiles that damage enemies.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its magic damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes its magic damage: 3-5 at rank 1, 41-62 at rank 20, for 2 mana at every rank.
