- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: Raises an Undead Mage to fight for you.

## How it works

- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It needs a corpse: a monster that fell in the battle.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- At rank 10 the skeleton mage has 100 armor, as its screen shows.
