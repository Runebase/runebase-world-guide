**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Auras](index.md)
- **Kind:** an aura
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Might](might.md)
- **Opens:** [Concentration](concentration.md)
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Increase attack rating %

## How it works

- It works while it's your main or secondary skill, and counts once a round (in the Arena, once a move).

## Checked in play

- Your attacks get its attack rating bonus, in the menus and in battle: +25% at rank 1 (+215% at rank 20); a hit that would have missed lands.
