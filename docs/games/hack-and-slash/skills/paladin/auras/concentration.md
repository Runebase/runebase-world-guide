# Concentration

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Auras](index.md)
- **Kind:** an aura
- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Blessed Aim](blessed-aim.md)
- **Opens:** [Fanaticism](fanaticism.md)
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Adds manaSteal % to your physical attacks

## How it works

- It works while it's your main or secondary skill, and counts once a round (in the Arena, once a move).
- Life and mana steal give you their % of the damage dealt.

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 selected: 3% mana steal.
