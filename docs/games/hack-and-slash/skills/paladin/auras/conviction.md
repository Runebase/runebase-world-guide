# Conviction

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Auras](index.md)
- **Kind:** an aura, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Sanctuary](sanctuary.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Lower all enemy resistances

## How it works

- It works while it's your main or secondary skill, and counts once a round (in the Arena, once a move).

## Checked in play

- In the Arena it lowers only your opponent's resistances (53% at rank 20), on your move and theirs.
