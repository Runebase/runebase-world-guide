# ![Conviction](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/conviction.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Conviction

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Auras](index.md)
- **Kind:** an aura, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Sanctuary](sanctuary.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Lower all magical resistances of enemies

## How it works

- It works while it's your main or secondary skill, and counts once a round (in the Arena, once a move).

## Checked in play

- It lowers your enemies' five magical resistances (fire, cold, lightning, poison, magic), never physical: 53% at rank 20. In the Arena it lowers only your opponent's, on your move and theirs.
