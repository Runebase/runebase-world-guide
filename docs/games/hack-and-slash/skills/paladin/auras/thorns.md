# ![Thorns](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/thorns.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Thorns

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Auras](index.md)
- **Kind:** an aura
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Reflect Physical melee damage back to the enemy

## How it works

- It works while it's your main or secondary skill, and counts once a round (in the Arena, once a move).

## Checked in play

- A monster's physical hit that lands on you comes back at its % (25% at rank 1, 215% at rank 20), through the monster's armor. Only melee hits: a ranged hit (an arrow, a bolt) isn't answered (your gear's Reflect Physical still answers it).
