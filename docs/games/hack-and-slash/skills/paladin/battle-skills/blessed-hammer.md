# Blessed Hammer

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Battle Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a chain attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Holy Bolt](holy-bolt.md)
- **Opens:** [Holy Shield](holy-shield.md), [Fist of the Heavens](fist-of-the-heavens.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: A hammer that shoots through enemies with a chance to stun

## How it works

- "targets N" hits N times, hopping between the monsters.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- Its magic damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
