# ![Conversion](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/conversion.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Conversion

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Battle Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Vengeance](vengeance.md)
- **Opens:** [Fist of the Heavens](fist-of-the-heavens.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Adds a small chance to convert the target to fight for the paladin

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- A hit that lands has its chance (1% at rank 1, 4.8% at rank 20) to turn the monster into a minion that fights for you with the health it had left, for its rounds (10, 48); at most 1 at once (3 at rank 20). A monster the hit kills isn't converted.
