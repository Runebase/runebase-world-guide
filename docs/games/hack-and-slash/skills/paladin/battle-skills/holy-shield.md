# ![Holy Shield](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/holy-shield.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Holy Shield

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/paladin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/battle-skills.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Battle Skills](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/charge.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Charge](charge.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/blessed-hammer.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Blessed Hammer](blessed-hammer.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** [a shield](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: Magically enhances shield to give defense bonuses, increased block and increased smite damage.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- For its rounds (10 at rank 1, 105 at rank 20): +25% armor (+500%), +15% block (+34%) and its smite damage on Smite (+3-6, +41-101). Without a shield it falls away.
