- **Kind:** an exploding attack
- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/zeal.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Zeal](zeal.md)</span>

- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Adds explosive Elemental (Fire, Lightning and Cold) damage to all melee attacks.

## How it works

- It explodes: the monster it hits takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster it hits takes your weapon's hit and its fire, lightning and cold; its neighbours the fire, lightning and cold (6-12, 1-15 and 6-12 at rank 1; 82-183, 1-148 and 82-183 at rank 20).
