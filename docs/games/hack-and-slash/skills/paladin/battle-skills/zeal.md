# ![Zeal](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/zeal.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Zeal

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Battle Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md)
- **Opens:** [Vengeance](vengeance.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: attacks multiple adjacent enemies.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It hits "targets" adjacent monsters, each with its damage and attack rating bonus: 1 hit at +6% damage and +10% attack rating at rank 1, 4 hits at +120% and +200% at rank 20.
