# ![Prayer](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/prayer.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Prayer

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)
- **Tree:** [Sanctified Protector](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** [Heal](heal.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Heals the character after each round.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It heals its % of your maximum health at the end of the round you cast it and of each of its rounds: 1% for 2 rounds at rank 1 (3 heals), 5.75% for 13 at rank 20 (14).
