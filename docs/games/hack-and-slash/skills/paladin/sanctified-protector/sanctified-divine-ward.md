# ![Sanctified Divine Ward](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/sanctified-divine-ward.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Sanctified Divine Ward

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/paladin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Paladin](../../../classes/paladin.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/sanctified-protector.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Sanctified Protector](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/paladin/summon-divine-protector.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Summon Divine Protector](summon-divine-protector.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Improves the sanctified protector rounds, hp and chance to stun enemies

## How it works

- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.

## Checked in play

- Your Divine Protectors get its health (+150 at rank 1, +3,000 at rank 20), its rounds (+4, +10) and its stun chance on their hits (3%, 7%).
