**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/warrior.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/combat-masteries.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Combat Masteries](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/though-skin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Though Skin](though-skin.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Increase all resistance (at most 70% in total)

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).

## Checked in play

- It adds its own rank's resistance to fire, cold, lightning, poison and magic, whatever Though Skin's rank; resistances stop at 70%.
