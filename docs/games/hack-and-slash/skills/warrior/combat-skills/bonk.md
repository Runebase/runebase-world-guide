**Where to play:** Discord

- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/double-swing.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Double Swing](double-swing.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/stun.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Stun](stun.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Bonk releases a powerful blow that increases damage. Damage and Attack Rating are also increased compared to normal blow; damage is added both as flat amount and percentage (after flat increase).

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- At rank 1 it adds 1 flat damage and +50% damage, at rank 20 20 and +145%, and costs its 2 mana once; picked on the battle screen it hits for that damage.
