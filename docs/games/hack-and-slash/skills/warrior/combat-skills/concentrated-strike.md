**Where to play:** Discord

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/warrior.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)</span>

- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 30 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/wound.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wound](wound.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 50 at rank 20

> In the game: Increases damage, critical chance and unable to miss

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It never misses and can't be blocked. Rank 1: +35% damage and a 2% critical chance, for 2 mana; rank 20: +415% and 21%, for 50 mana.
