# Critical Throw

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)
- **Tree:** [Combat Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Double Throw](double-throw.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** [a throwing weapon](../../../gear-and-crafting.md#weapons-that-skills-need)

> In the game: Chance to critically hit enemies with throw weapons and attack rating increase

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
