# ![Mirror](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/mirror.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Mirror

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)
- **Tree:** [Combat Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a summon
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

> In the game: Summon a reflection of the warrior that duplicates the warrior's attacks

## How it works

- A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The reflection repeats your attack with its damage bonus: −50% at rank 1.
- It has your critical chance: a critical reflection deals 75% more, as yours does.
