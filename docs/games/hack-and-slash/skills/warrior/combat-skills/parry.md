**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/warrior.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/combat-skills.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Combat Skills](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a passive skill
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/strategic-strike.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Strategic Strike](strategic-strike.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Gives chance to parry incoming monster attacks

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).
