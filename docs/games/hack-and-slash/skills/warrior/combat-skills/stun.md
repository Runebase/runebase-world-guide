**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)
- **Tree:** [Combat Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** an attack
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Bonk](bonk.md)
- **Opens:** [Wound](wound.md)
- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Chance to Stun enemies & increased attack damage

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- Its physical damage rolls to hit ([your hit chance](../../../attributes-and-combat.md#hitting-and-missing)), and monsters can block it.
