# ![Wound](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/warrior/wound.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Wound

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)
- **Tree:** [Combat Skills](index.md)
- **Kind:** a debuff
- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Stun](stun.md)
- **Opens:** [Cleave](cleave.md), [Concentrated Strike](concentrated-strike.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Put a bleeding effect on the enemy

## How it works

- It stays on the monster for its rounds; casting it again replaces it and keeps the monster's other debuffs.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster bleeds from the next round: 50% of your weapon's damage (with your strength bonus) a round for 4 rounds at rank 1, 145% for 10 rounds at rank 20. 2 mana.
