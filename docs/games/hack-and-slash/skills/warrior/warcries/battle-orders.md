# Battle Orders

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)
- **Tree:** [Warcries](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Screech](screech.md)
- **Opens:** [War Cry](war-cry.md), [Battle Command](battle-command.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 2 at rank 20

> In the game: Increases life next rounds

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 gives +40% health for 7 rounds, for 2 mana.
