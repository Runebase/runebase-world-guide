- **Class:** [Warrior](../../../classes/warrior.md)

- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Howl](howl.md)

> In the game: Increases attack rating next rounds

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 adds +40% attack rating, in the menus and in battle (a 62.3% hit chance becomes 69.8%).
