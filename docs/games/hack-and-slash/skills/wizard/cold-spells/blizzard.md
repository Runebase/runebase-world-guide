# ![Blizzard](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/blizzard.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Blizzard

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)
- **Tree:** [Cold Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Frost Nova](frost-nova.md), [Glacial Spike](glacial-spike.md)
- **Opens:** [Frozen Orb](frozen-orb.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 22 at rank 20

> In the game: Summons an ice storm to rain cold death onto your enemies.

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes its cold damage (51-86 at rank 1, 621-694 at rank 20), each with its freeze chance (20%, 96%) to miss its attacks for its rounds (2, 4).
