# Chilling Armor

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)
- **Tree:** [Cold Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Shiver Armor](shiver-armor.md)
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Confers a defense bonus and launches an ice blast against attackers.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It strikes back with your Ice Blast, at Ice Blast's own rank and freeze chance.
