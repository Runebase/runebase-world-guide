# ![Frost Nova](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/frost-nova.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Frost Nova

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/wizard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/cold-spells.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cold Spells](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 6 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/blizzard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Blizzard](blizzard.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 22 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates an expanding ring of ice and frost that has a chance to freeze the enemy

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Every monster takes its cold damage (1-2 at rank 1, 39-59 at rank 20) and has its freeze chance (20%, 77%): a frozen monster misses its attacks for 2 rounds, this one included.
