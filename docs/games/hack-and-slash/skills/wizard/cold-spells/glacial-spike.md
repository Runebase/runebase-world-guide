- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 22 at rank 20

> In the game: A shard of ice that inflicts massive cold damage and explodes with a chance freeze nearby enemies.

## How it works

- It explodes: the monster it hits takes the hit and the explosion, its neighbours the explosion.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The monster it hits takes its cold damage twice (the hit and the explosion), its neighbours once (17-26 at rank 1, 264-349 at rank 20); each has its freeze chance (20%, 96%) to miss its attacks for its rounds (2, 4). A critical spike adds +75% to the hit and to the explosion.
