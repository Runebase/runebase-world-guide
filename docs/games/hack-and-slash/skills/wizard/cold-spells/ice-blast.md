- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 22 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates a bolt of ice that has a chance to freeze the target.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its cold damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- The selected monster takes its cold damage (8-12 at rank 1, 198-278 at rank 20) and has its freeze chance (20%, 96%): a frozen monster misses its attacks for its rounds (2 at rank 1, 4 at rank 20), this one included.
