# ![Fire Bolt](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/fire-bolt.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Fire Bolt

**Where to play:** Discord

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/wizard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)</span>

- **Kind:** an attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 1 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** no other skill
- **Opens:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/fire-ball.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Fire Ball](fire-ball.md)</span>
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates a bolt of fire.

## How it works

- It hits the monster you selected.
- Its fire damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 deals its fire damage; armor doesn't reduce fire.
