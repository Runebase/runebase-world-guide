# Fire Wall

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)
- **Tree:** [Fire Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** a debuff on every monster, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Blaze](blaze.md)
- **Opens:** [Meteor](meteor.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates a wall of fire.

## How it works

- Its burn or poison ticks once a round for its rounds.
- It stays on every monster for its rounds; casting it again replaces it and keeps the monster's other debuffs.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- Rank 1 burns every monster 10-12 a round.
