- **Learn it from:** level 18 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/lightning.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Lightning](lightning.md)</span>

- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 3 at rank 1, 22 at rank 20

> In the game: Casts a lightning bolt that jumps through multiple targets.

## How it works

- "targets N" hits N times, hopping between the monsters.
- Its lightning damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- It strikes "targets" times with its lightning damage, hopping between the monsters: 5 strikes at rank 1, 24 at rank 20.
