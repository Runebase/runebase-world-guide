# ![Energy Shield](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/energy-shield.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Energy Shield

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/class/wizard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)</span>
- **Tree:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill-tree/lightning-spells.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Lightning Spells](index.md)</span>
- **Kind:** a buff, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 24 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/chain-lightning.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chain Lightning](chain-lightning.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../../../assets/icons/skill/wizard/teleport.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Teleport](teleport.md)</span>
- **Opens:** no other skill
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 5 at rank 1, 24 at rank 20

> In the game: Absorbs damage to Mana instead of Life.

## How it works

- It's on you for its rounds.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

## Checked in play

- For its rounds, its share of every monster hit on you comes off your mana instead of your health: 20% for 10 rounds at rank 1, 100% for 48 at rank 20.
