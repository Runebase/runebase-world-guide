# Nova

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Class:** [Wizard](../../../classes/wizard.md)
- **Tree:** [Lightning Spells](index.md)
- **Kind:** an area attack, a spell
- **Learn it from:** level 12 (your rank)
- **Needs first:** [Static Field](static-field.md)
- **Opens:** [Thunder Storm](thunder-storm.md)
- **Weapon:** any
- **Mana:** 2 at rank 1, 21 at rank 20

> In the game: Creates an expanding ring of electricity that damages the foe and has a chance to stun

## How it works

- It hits every monster.
- A monster it stuns skips its attack that round.
- Its lightning damage never misses: only physical attacks roll to hit.
- It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says **Attack** and makes the regular attack instead, for free.
