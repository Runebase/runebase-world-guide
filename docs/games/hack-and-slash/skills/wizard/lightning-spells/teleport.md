- **Weapon:** any

> In the game: Decrease Stamina used for going to a Quickbattle

## How it works

- It's always on: nothing to select, no mana.
- What it adds to your character is what its screen shows (checked at ranks 1 and 20).

## Checked in play

- A quick battle costs 20 stamina minus its screen value (returning to a battle 5).
