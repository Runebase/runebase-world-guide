# Increased Experience Gain

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

An attack type you pick with `/heist`: **+1 Increased Experience Gain**.

You gain 1 more experience: 11 instead of 10.

Every heist steals 10 experience plus the attack type; then the chances roll (recovery, block, counter-attack, deflect): [one heist](index.md#one-heist).
