# ![Increased Experience Gain](../../../assets/icons/heist-attack/increased-experience-gain.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Increased Experience Gain

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

An attack type you pick with `/heist`: **+1 Increased Experience Gain**.

You gain 1 more experience: 11 instead of 10.

## Checked in play

- You steal 11 and the defender loses 10; the heist menu's Attack button plays it unless you pick another.

Every heist steals 10 experience plus the attack type; then the chances roll (recovery, block, counter-attack, deflect): [one heist](index.md#one-heist).
