# ![Increased Opponent Experience Loss](../../../assets/icons/heist-attack/increased-opponent-experience-loss.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Increased Opponent Experience Loss

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

An attack type you pick with `/heist`: **+1 Increased Opponent Experience Loss**.

The defender loses 1 more experience: 11 instead of 10.

## Checked in play

- Picked in the heist menu's attack picker: you steal 10 and the defender loses 11.

Every heist steals 10 experience plus the attack type; then the chances roll (recovery, block, counter-attack, deflect): [one heist](index.md#one-heist).
