# ![Recovery Chance](../../../assets/icons/heist-attack/recovery-chance.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Recovery Chance

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

An attack type you pick with `/heist`: **+5% Recovery Chance**.

Adds 5% to your chance to keep the energy of the attack; it steals no extra experience.

Every heist steals 10 experience plus the attack type; then the chances roll (recovery, block, counter-attack, deflect): [one heist](index.md#one-heist).
