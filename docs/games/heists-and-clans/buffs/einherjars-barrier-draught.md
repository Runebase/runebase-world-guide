# ![Einherjar's Barrier Draught](../../../assets/icons/item/einherjars-barrier-draught.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Einherjar's Barrier Draught

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a super buff (at most 5 a month)
- **Each charge:** +100% Block Chance
- **Used:** when you defend; as the attacker it waits for a counter-attack, blocks it, and only then is used

## Where you get it

Bought in the [store](../../../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Einherjar's Barrier Draught](../../../items/store-consumables.md#einherjars-barrier-draught).

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
