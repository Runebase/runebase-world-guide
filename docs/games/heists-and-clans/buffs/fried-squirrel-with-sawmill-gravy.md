# ![Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy](../../../assets/icons/item/fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** +1 Increased Opponent Exp Loss
- **Used:** when you attack (on top of the attack type), and in your counter-attack when you defend

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Squirrel](../../../items/ingredients.md#squirrel) + 1 [Sawmill Gravy](../../../items/ingredients.md#sawmill-gravy); swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy](../../../items/dishes.md#fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy).

## Checked in play

- The defender loses 11 instead of 10 (12 with the +1 loss attack); in your counter-attack when you defend, the raider loses 11; a plain defence keeps it.
- The heist menu lists it with its charges and its number and adds it to your stats; the log shows it among your triggered buffs when it's used.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
