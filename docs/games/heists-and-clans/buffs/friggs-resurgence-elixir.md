# ![Frigg's Resurgence Elixir](../../../assets/icons/item/friggs-resurgence-elixir.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Frigg's Resurgence Elixir

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a super buff (at most 5 a month)
- **Each charge:** +100% Recovery Chance
- **Used:** when you attack: one heist per charge keeps its energy

## Where you get it

Bought in the [store](../../../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Frigg's Resurgence Elixir](../../../items/store-consumables.md#friggs-resurgence-elixir).

## Checked in play

- Each charge keeps one attack's energy (3/3 left), and that attack still scores its energy on the ladder. Held while you defend, it stays.
- The heist menu lists it with its charges and its number and adds it to your stats; the log shows it among your triggered buffs when it's used.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
