# ![Grilled Eel with Sichuan Peppercorns](../../../assets/icons/item/grilled-eel-with-sichuan-peppercorns.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Grilled Eel with Sichuan Peppercorns

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** +5% Recovery Chance
- **Used:** when you attack; a charge is used even when the recovery fails. On defence it does nothing and stays

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/eel.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Eel](../../../items/ingredients.md#eel)</span> + 4 <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/sichuan-peppercorns.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Sichuan Peppercorns](../../../items/ingredients.md#sichuan-peppercorns)</span>; swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/convert-market.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/grilled-eel-with-sichuan-peppercorns.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Grilled Eel with Sichuan Peppercorns](../../../items/dishes.md#grilled-eel-with-sichuan-peppercorns)</span>.

## Checked in play

- Exactly 5% for your attack to keep its energy (10% with the Recovery Chance attack); the charge is used even when it doesn't. Held while you defend, it stays: a defender never recovers energy.
- The heist menu lists it with its charges and its number and adds it to your stats; the log shows it among your triggered buffs when it's used.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
