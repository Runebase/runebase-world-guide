# 💪 Heist buffs

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

Eat a buff from the heist menu's **Heist Buffs** (every buff of every game: <span class="gi-m">🍲&nbsp;[Buffs by game](../../../items/buffs.md)</span>); each one you eat is one **charge**. **Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each**, whether it changed the outcome or not.

- A buff used "only when you're counter-attacked" (<span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/moose-with-blueberry-sauce.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Moose](moose-with-blueberry-sauce.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mackerel](mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/einherjars-barrier-draught.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Barrier Draught](einherjars-barrier-draught.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/heist-buff/assault-fortification.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Assault Fortification](assault-fortification.md)</span>) stays when you attack and nobody strikes back. A defending-only buff (<span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/seared-tuna-with-wasabi-butter.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Seared Tuna](seared-tuna-with-wasabi-butter.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/skadis-retribution-brew.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Retribution Brew](skadis-retribution-brew.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/heist-buff/retribution-fury.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Retribution Fury](retribution-fury.md)</span>) stays when you attack, and an attacking-only one (<span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/grilled-eel-with-sichuan-peppercorns.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Grilled Eel](grilled-eel-with-sichuan-peppercorns.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/friggs-resurgence-elixir.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Frigg's Elixir](friggs-resurgence-elixir.md)</span>) when you defend; <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wild Turkey](wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Fried Squirrel](fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.md)</span> wait for your attack or your counter-attack.
- **A deflected heist** uses none of the defender's charges (they weren't hit); the raider's are used once, on the heist that lands, with the new defender's.
- **Super buffs:** at most **5** a month. You must be on this month's heist ladder: you are once you joined, attacked or were attacked this month (in the heist since an earlier month, you're refused until then, and nothing is taken). For a clan member, the clan must be on this month's clan ladder too (opening the heist menu puts it there); both count them. A 6th is refused and nothing is taken.
- **Super buff charges you haven't used are gone at the month change:** the new month's first run resets them to 0. Your other buffs stay.
- **Earned buffs** come from the [attack types](../attacks-and-defence/index.md#one-heist), and from gear and pets that add a chance to earn them: each win adds one charge.
