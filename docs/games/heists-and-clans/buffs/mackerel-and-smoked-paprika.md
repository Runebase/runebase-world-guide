**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** -1 Lessen Exp Loss
- **Used:** when you're hit (also a blocked hit uses the charge), and when you're counter-attacked as the attacker

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Mackerel](../../../items/ingredients.md#mackerel) + 4 [Smoked Paprika](../../../items/ingredients.md#smoked-paprika); swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Mackerel and Smoked Paprika](../../../items/dishes.md#mackerel-and-smoked-paprika).

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
