# Moose with Blueberry Sauce

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** +10% Block Chance
- **Used:** when you defend; as the attacker, only when you're counter-attacked (it waits for one, then blocks it at its chance)

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Moose](../../../items/ingredients.md#moose) + 2 [Blueberry Sauce](../../../items/ingredients.md#blueberry-sauce); swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Moose with Blueberry Sauce](../../../items/dishes.md#moose-with-blueberry-sauce).

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
