# ![Retribution Fury](../../../assets/icons/heist-buff/retribution-fury.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Retribution Fury

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** earned in heists
- **Each charge:** +100% Counter-Attack Chance
- **Used:** when you defend: the next hit is counter-attacked. As the attacker it does nothing and is kept

## Where you get it

The <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/heist-attack/gain-retribution-fury.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gain Retribution Fury (100% Counter-Attack Buff) attack](../attacks-and-defence/gain-retribution-fury.md)</span> and the gear <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/heist-gear/skadis-rejuvenating-shroud.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Skadi's Rejuvenating Shroud](../gear/skadis-rejuvenating-shroud.md)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/heist-gear/skadis-frostbite-ward.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Skadi's Frostbite Ward](../gear/skadis-frostbite-ward.md)</span> give a chance to earn one charge each heist; pets can too.

## Checked in play

- Won on an attack (the Post-Heist Effects say so), one charge a win; it counter-attacks the next hit. Held while you attack, it stays.
- The heist menu lists it with its charges and its number and adds it to your stats; the log shows it among your triggered buffs when it's used.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: <span class="gi-m">💪&nbsp;[heist buffs](index.md)</span>.
