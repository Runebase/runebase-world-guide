# ![Retribution Fury](../../../assets/icons/heist-buff/retribution-fury.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Retribution Fury

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** earned in heists
- **Each charge:** +100% Counter-Attack Chance
- **Used:** when you defend: the next hit is counter-attacked. As the attacker it does nothing and is kept

## Where you get it

The [Gain Retribution Fury (100% Counter-Attack Buff) attack](../attacks-and-defence/gain-retribution-fury.md) and the gear [Skadi's Rejuvenating Shroud](../gear/skadis-rejuvenating-shroud.md), [Skadi's Frostbite Ward](../gear/skadis-frostbite-ward.md) give a chance to earn one charge each heist; pets can too.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
