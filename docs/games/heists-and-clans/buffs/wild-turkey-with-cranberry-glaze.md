# ![Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze](../../../assets/icons/item/wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** +2 Increased Exp Gain
- **Used:** when you attack, and in your counter-attack when you defend; never on a plain defence

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Wild Turkey](../../../items/ingredients.md#wild-turkey) + 2 [Cranberry Glaze](../../../items/ingredients.md#cranberry-glaze); swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: [Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze](../../../items/dishes.md#wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze).

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
