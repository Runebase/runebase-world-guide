# ![Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze](../../../assets/icons/item/wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Kind:** a dish you eat
- **Each charge:** +2 Increased Exp Gain
- **Used:** when you attack, and in your counter-attack when you defend; never on a plain defence

## Where you get it

Cooked at the [cooking table](../../cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/wild-turkey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wild Turkey](../../../items/ingredients.md#wild-turkey)</span> + 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/cranberry-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Cranberry Glaze](../../../items/ingredients.md#cranberry-glaze)</span>; swapped for at the [convert market](../../../account/convert-market.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../loot-reels.md). Everything about the item: <span class="gi-m">![](../../../assets/icons/item/wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze](../../../items/dishes.md#wild-turkey-with-cranberry-glaze)</span>.

## Checked in play

- Each charge adds 2 to what you steal: 13 instead of 11 with the +1 attack, for as many attacks as you have charges, then back to 11.
- A charge is used on every attack, also one that keeps its energy, and in your counter-attack when you defend (you win back 12); a plain defence keeps it. In a deflected heist it's used once, on the heist that lands.
- The heist menu lists it with its charges and its number and adds it to your stats; the log shows it among your triggered buffs when it's used.

## How buffs work

Each one you eat (or earn) is one charge. Every charge you hold that applies to your side is used on every heist you're in, one charge each, whether it changed the outcome or not; no charge goes below 0. More: [heist buffs](index.md).
