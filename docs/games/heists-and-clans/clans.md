# Clans

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **Register** a clan from the clan menu: it costs **15,000 credits** (the only clan action that costs credits). With fewer credits, a name already taken, or a name under 3 letters, nothing is spent, and you're told privately; if something goes wrong while registering, nothing is spent either.
- **Join** by request: find a clan with `/browseclans` (every open clan, page by page) and ask to join; the leader accepts or declines. A full clan can't accept. If you founded your own clan meanwhile, the leader is told you're already in a clan.
- The leader can pass the leadership and kick members. When the last member leaves, the clan closes.
- A clan accepts at most 10 joins a month: a member who leaves and comes back uses another. Once the 10 are used, the clan takes no new requests that month, and waiting ones can only be declined.
- Leave a clan and you lose its protection at once: your former clanmates can heist you.
- Clanmates can't heist each other, and a deflection never lands in the attacker's clan.
- A heist between members of two clans also counts for both clans' ladders.
