# ⚡ Clans

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

- **A clan belongs to the server it was registered in** (<span class="gi-m">🌍&nbsp;[realm](../../account/realms.md)</span>): it's listed, joined and led only there, and it plays for that server's clan ladder, monthly clan prize and clan winners role. Your account in another server can't join it, and a leader whose account is in another server can't lead it from there.
- **Register** a clan from the clan menu: it costs **15,000 credits** (the only clan action that costs credits). With fewer credits, a name already taken, or a name under 3 letters, nothing is spent, and the clan menu says why; if something goes wrong while registering, nothing is spent either. Spaces around the name don't count, when you register or edit it.
- **Join** by request: find a clan with `/browseclans` (every open clan of the server you're in, page by page) and ask to join; the leader accepts or declines. A full clan can't accept. If you founded your own clan meanwhile, the leader is told you're already in a clan.
- The leader can pass the leadership and kick members, but not themselves: to step down, pass the leadership or leave. When the last member leaves, the clan closes.
- Whatever a clan screen refuses (a taken name, a full clan, a member who already left, a button from before the leadership passed on) is said on that screen, which shows the clan as it is now.
- A clan accepts at most 10 joins a month: a member who leaves and comes back uses another. Once the 10 are used, the clan takes no new requests that month, and waiting ones can only be declined.
- Leave a clan and you lose its protection at once: your former clanmates can heist you.
- Clanmates can't heist each other, and a deflection never lands in the attacker's clan.
- A heist between members of two clans also counts for both clans' ladders.
