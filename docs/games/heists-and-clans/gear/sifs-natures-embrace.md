## Checked in play

- Exactly 2% for your attack to keep its energy; a counter-attack never gives energy back.
- Its numbers are the ones on the gear screen, the crafting screen and the heist menu (Heist - Modifiers and Heist - Stats). Worn on the other side of a heist, or taken off, it does nothing; every slot adds up.

## How gear works

You craft it with scrap from the heist gear menu (exactly the scrap listed) and wear one item per slot. An attacking bonus works when you attack, a defending bonus when you defend. A counter-attack is the defender's attack and the raider's defence for experience: an attacking experience bonus also adds to your counter-attack when you defend, and a defending block or experience bonus also protects you when you're counter-attacked as the raider; a counter-attack never gives energy back or earns a buff. Without Tactical Shift your neutral set counts both ways; with it, the offensive set attacks and the defensive set defends, and in a counter-attack each side keeps the set it's wearing (the raider their offensive set, the defender their defensive set). Every slot adds up. More: <span class="gi-m">🛡️&nbsp;[heist gear](index.md)</span>.
