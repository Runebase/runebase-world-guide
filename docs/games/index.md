# Games

Every game of Runebase World, each on its own page; each page says where you can play it. How they connect: [the guide's first page](../index.md#how-the-games-connect).

| Game | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Hack & Slash](hack-and-slash/index.md) | the role-playing game: quick battles, the Jotnar world boss, the Arena, gear and the auction house |
| <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/crown-of-chaos.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Crown of Chaos](crown-of-chaos/index.md)</span> | one player holds the Crown, everyone else attacks it with <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Chaos Relics](../items/keys-and-cards.md#chaos-relic)</span>; a reign pays the Crown pool |
| <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/game/heists.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Heists and clans](heists-and-clans/index.md)</span> | steal experience from other players, join a clan, climb the monthly ladder |
| [Fishing](fishing.md) | trips on energy that catch fish, with rods to craft and enhance |
| [Hunting](hunting.md) | trips on energy that catch meat, with rifles to craft and enhance |
| [Expeditions](expeditions.md) | trips on energy that bring back scrap, crafting materials and credits |
| [Cooking](cooking.md) | the fire and the cooking table: fish and meat become dishes that buff the other games |
| [Loot Reels](loot-reels.md) | free spins every day |
| [WordWeaver](wordweaver.md) | guess a hidden word in six tries |
| [Chess](chess.md) | chess against the bot or another player |
| [Hnefatafl](hnefatafl.md) | the Viking board game, against the bot or ranked against other players |
| [Dice](dice.md) | `/roll` two dice for experience, a RUNES prize and a prize draw |
| [Treasure chests](treasure-chests.md) | open a chest with a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/bronze-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bronze](../items/keys-and-cards.md#bronze-key)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/silver-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Silver](../items/keys-and-cards.md#silver-key)</span> or <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gold Key](../items/keys-and-cards.md#gold-key)</span> |
| [Piles and the key hunt](piles-and-key-hunt.md) | grab the piles that appear in the channels; the keys you find score on a monthly ladder |
| [Scratch cards](scratch-cards.md) | scratch a card with a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#mystery-card)</span> for RUNES, experience, keys and more |
| [Runes Pattern](runes-pattern.md) | the whole server's game: mark the words on your card as they appear in the channel |
