# 🗝️ Piles and the key hunt

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

Be first to grab the piles that appear in the server's channels, and climb the monthly key hunt with the treasure keys you find. Keys open <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/emoji/bronzekey.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[treasure chests](treasure-chests.md)</span>.

## Piles

Piles of credits, treasure keys, scrap and building supplies appear in the server's channels for a short time, in every <span class="gi-m">🌍&nbsp;[realm](../account/realms.md)</span>: each server gets every kind of pile on the same schedule, in channels its verified members can see. What you collect goes to your account in that server (its pouch, its quests and boards, its key hunt and its 4-hour key windows). To collect one, press its button and answer the **captcha**: the first players to answer correctly take the pile's slots. A pile is collected only in the server it appeared in: its button pressed anywhere else is refused privately, and nothing is collected.

- **Credit piles** are shared: each collector gets their share and **2 experience**. Answering the captcha wrong, or collecting twice, pays nothing. The pile's post says what it holds, each share and how many shares are left, to the last 0.00000001 credits, and after each collect it shows what's left and who collected (until 2026-11-04 it kept showing the whole pile as left); your collect posts your share.
- A wrong answer shows the captcha again, marked incorrect, with the tries you have left: press **Answer** again for another try.
- **Three tries per pile:** after your third wrong answer on a pile you can't collect that pile any more. The captcha says so (it closes, with no picture and no **Answer** button), and so does the pile's button if you press it again. Every kind of pile counts this way (credits, treasure keys, scrap, building supplies), and every player has their own three tries: your wrong answers don't count against anyone else. The count lasts until the pile ends, a restart of the bot included.
- Discord shows you one captcha at a time: a captcha you closed without answering doesn't count, and your next answer goes to the pile you answered, once.
- A pile still open when the bot restarts stays collectable until its time is up.
- A pile has a limited number of slots: when more players answer than there are slots, only the first ones are paid, and the others are told.
- When a credit pile expires, its unclaimed share goes to the **Crown of Chaos** pool.
- A key or supply pile taken to its last slot is replaced by a post naming who collected it, with a **Pouch** (keys, scrap) or **Basket** (building supplies) button: whoever clicks it sees their own pouch or basket, privately; the post stays as it is.
- **Nail piles** come every hour: each of their players gets the same number of <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nails](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#nail)</span> (3 to 9, set when the pile appears) and 2 experience. A Rope pile works the same way with <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/rope.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Rope](../items/materials/building-supplies.md#rope)</span>. The pile's post says how many each player gets, the total and how many are left.
- **Scrap piles**: each collector gets one <span class="gi-m">🔩&nbsp;[scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md)</span> of the pile's kind (<span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Wood Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#wood-scrap)</span>, <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/metal-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Metal Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#metal-scrap)</span> or <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/leather-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Leather Scrap](../items/materials/scrap.md#leather-scrap)</span>) and 1 experience (the post's number of scraps is one for each of its slots).
- In a server without an experience channel, where collects are posted, a collect pays all the same and shows what you got on your own screen, where you answered the captcha.
- **What your collect posts is what you got:** the summary's first lines are the pile's own loot (the scrap, the Nails or Rope, the experience), exactly what went into your pouch or basket; a quest the collect completes lists its rewards under its own heading.
- **Treasure keys**: the first key of each type you find in **4 hours** pays **10 experience**. Within those 4 hours, your **6th <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/bronze-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bronze Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#bronze-key)</span>, 4th <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/silver-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Silver Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#silver-key)</span> or 2nd <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gold Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#gold-key)</span>** also gives a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Card](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span>. Each type counts on its own, and its 4 hours start with its first key; the message for each key you find says when they end and how many more you need for the card. A restart of the bot, even in the middle of your find, never pays those 10 experience twice and never loses them.

**Pile boards** (`/leaderboard`): the Scrap, Credits Pile and BuildingSupply Pile Collector boards, all-time and monthly, count **1 for each pile you collect**, whatever it held. The TreasureKey Finder board counts 1 for each key.

## Key hunt

The **Monthly TreasureKey Hunt Ladder** (`/leaderboard`, monthly boards) ranks the treasure key hunters of the calendar month, each server its own (a key found in a server counts toward that server's ladder): each key you find scores **1 point for a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/bronze-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Bronze Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#bronze-key)</span>, 2 for a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/silver-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Silver Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#silver-key)</span>, 3 for a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gold Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#gold-key)</span>**. When the staff pay the monthly prizes (on the Runebase server), last month's best hunters share **0.75% of the realm's RUNES wallet** by rank:

| Rank | Shares of the pool, each |
|---|---|
| 1 | 12 |
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 8 |
| 4 to 5 | 5 |
| 6 to 10 | 3 |
| 11 to 25 | 1.5 |
| 26 to 50 | 0.75 |

A tie goes to whoever found their keys first, and players who left the server or are banned from the bot are left out (of the ladder, its prizes and the winners role). Each server's winners are announced in that server's own experience channel.

## Tips

- Piles go fast: answer the captcha quickly.
