# Runes Pattern

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

A game for the whole server in the Runes Pattern channel. A word appears in the channel every 20 seconds; click the ones on your card to mark them.

- A word that isn't on your card is a penalty: it costs you one marking.
- The word that completes **the day's current challenge** (a pattern on the card) wins the game: the winner uses **one [Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#runes-pattern-card)** and is paid once (experience and a prize roll), and a new game starts. When two players click the winning word at the same moment, there is one winner.
- A win pays **50 experience**; the **day's 3rd challenge pays 150** and counts the day toward the weekly challenge.
- The challenges follow the UTC day.
- Words disappear from the channel when their time is up.

Runes Pattern Cards cost 50 credits in the [store](../account/store.md); they also drop from many prize tables.
