# Runes Pattern

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

A game for the whole server in the Runes Pattern channel. A word appears in the channel every 20 seconds; click the ones on your card to mark them.

- A word that isn't on your card is a penalty: it costs you one marking.
- The word that completes **the day's current challenge** (a pattern on the card) wins the game: the winner uses **one <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#runes-pattern-card)</span>** and is paid once (experience and a prize roll), and a new game starts. When two players click the winning word at the same moment, there is one winner.
- A win pays **50 experience**; the **day's 3rd challenge pays 150** and counts the day toward the weekly challenge.
- The challenges follow the UTC day.
- Words disappear from the channel when their time is up. No word comes twice until all 75 have come; then the channel says the word pool starts over.
- **Show My Runes Pattern Card** (under every word) or **Activities → Runes Pattern** shows your own card, only to you: your words, what you've marked, today's challenges and the week's. Your card is dealt the first time you look at it or click a word.
- Every click on a word is answered only to you: marked, already marked, not on your card, or expired. A word clicked twice at once is marked once. Without a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#runes-pattern-card)</span> a click marks nothing and offers the store.
- **The wild card:** the word **Runes** is a wild card. Collect it (once per word) and your card shows **Apply Wild Card**: pick any unmarked word of your card and it's marked. A wild card that completes the challenge wins like a word, with one <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#runes-pattern-card)</span>. Your wild card stays yours when the word you picked was marked meanwhile, or when you have no Runes Pattern Card left (the bot tells you); picked on two devices at once, it's used once.
- **Users Playing** lists who plays (20 a page); players who chose to play anonymously show as "Anonymous Player". A player who hasn't clicked for 15 minutes leaves the list.
- If something goes wrong with a click, only you are told and nothing is marked or used: click again.
