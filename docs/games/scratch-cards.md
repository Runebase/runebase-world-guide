# ![Scratch Card](../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Scratch cards

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

`/scratchcard` in the scratch card channel. A card costs **one <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Card](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span>** (a scratch credit, from your pouch). Scratch all three fields, then **Collect**: the card pays its combination once.

- <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/currency/runes.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[RUNES](../account/credits-and-runes.md#runes-prizes)</span> prizes are a share of the realm's RUNES wallet, sent to you as a tip. Other prizes go to your pouch, your basket or your experience (a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Gold Key](../items/keys-and-cards/treasure-keys.md#gold-key)</span>, a <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#runes-pattern-card)</span>, a new Mystery Card…).
- No card is dealt without a Mystery Card, outside the scratch card channel, outside the Runebase server, or when you already have 100 cards open (the bot says so, and your Mystery Card is kept). Every refusal is told only to you.
- **Activities → Scratch Card** shows your Mystery Cards and the odds; **New Scratch Card** deals a card right there, to scratch and collect on the menu. Under a card you collected in the channel, **Scratch again** deals your next one.
- A card's buttons are its owner's: anyone else pressing them is told, only to them, that it isn't their card.
- A card waits for you when the bot restarts: scratch the rest and collect it afterwards; it pays once.
- If the tipbot is offline, your Mystery Card is kept. If it goes offline before you press **Collect**, only you are told, the card waits, and it pays once when the tipbot is back.
- A card pays once, also when it's shown twice (in the channel and in **Activities → Scratch Card**) or a field is clicked twice: a field scratches once, and a collected card pays nothing more.
- **Collect** before all three fields are scratched tells you, privately, to scratch them first.
- The result shows the prize with the combination's chance per card, as in the table below. Every combination is played in the tests and pays exactly what it shows.
- A prize your pouch has no room for isn't paid, and the result says so. FRE and AES give one of each key: a key whose pouch is full isn't paid, the others are, and the result names each key.
- With the tipbot online but unable to send for this server, a RUNES prize is shown as **not paid: Tipbot offline** and the card counts as collected.
- If something goes wrong while you collect, scratch or get a card, only you are told, nothing is spent or paid, and the card waits for you: collect or scratch again.
