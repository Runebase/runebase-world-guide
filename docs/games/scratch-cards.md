# Scratch cards

**Where to play:** Discord
{ .where-to-play }

`/scratchcard` in the scratch card channel. A card costs **one [Mystery Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#mystery-card)** (a scratch credit, from your pouch). Scratch all three fields, then **Collect**: the card pays its combination once.

- RUNES prizes are a share of the realm's RUNES wallet, sent to you as a tip. Other prizes go to your pouch, your basket or your experience (a [Gold Key](../items/keys-and-cards.md#gold-key), a [Runes Pattern Card](../items/keys-and-cards.md#runes-pattern-card), a new Mystery Card…).
- No card is dealt without a Mystery Card, outside the scratch card channel, or when you already have 100 cards open.
- If the tipbot is offline, your Mystery Card is kept.

Mystery Cards come from the Party Store ([Buildings](../account/buildings.md)), [heists](heists-and-clans/index.md), [chests](treasure-chests.md), [Loot Reels](loot-reels.md) and other prize tables. The HUG combination also gives 1 experience.
