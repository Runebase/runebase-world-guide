# ![Baked Cod with Old Bay Butter](../../assets/icons/item/baked-cod-with-old-bay-butter.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Fish dishes

Fish dishes are cooked at the fire's <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/cod.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[fish](../ingredients/fish.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/spices.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[spices](../ingredients/spices.md)</span>. Each entry says where you get the dish and what it does; <span class="gi-m">🍲&nbsp;[Buffs by game](../buffs.md)</span> lists the buffs by the game they help.

## ![Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../../assets/icons/item/anchovy-with-anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Anchovy with Anchovy Powder

> In the game: Restores HP and Mana to full when consumed.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/anchovy.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy](../ingredients/fish.md#anchovy)</span> + 10 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy Powder](../ingredients/spices.md#anchovy-powder)</span>; swapped for at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Hack & Slash:** heals a character back to full health and mana (a dead one can then return to its battle) ([more](../../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>, as a group 1 dish.

## ![Mackerel and Smoked Paprika](../../assets/icons/item/mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Mackerel and Smoked Paprika

> In the game: Reduces experience loss by 1 in the heist mini-game when defending. Each Mackerel consumed grants a buff for the upcoming hit, bolstering your resilience against your opponents.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/mackerel.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mackerel](../ingredients/fish.md#mackerel)</span> + 4 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/smoked-paprika.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Smoked Paprika](../ingredients/spices.md#smoked-paprika)</span>; swapped for at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Heists:** 1 less experience lost when you're hit, and when you're counter-attacked as the attacker ([more](../../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.md)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>, as a group 1 dish.
