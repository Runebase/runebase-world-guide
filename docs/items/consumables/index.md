# ![Consumables](../../assets/icons/emoji/consumables.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Consumables

What you eat or drink: the dishes cooked at the fire's <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from <span class="gi-m">🧺&nbsp;[ingredients](../ingredients/index.md)</span>, and the drinks, brews and elixirs sold in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>. Each entry says where you get it and what it does in the game it's for; <span class="gi-m">🍲&nbsp;[Buffs by game](../buffs.md)</span> lists them by the game they help. Dishes wait in your **basket** (`/basket`), where <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/swordfish-kebabs-with-lemon-and-rosemary.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Swordfish Kebabs](fish-dishes.md#swordfish-kebabs-with-lemon-and-rosemary)</span> are eaten for experience.
