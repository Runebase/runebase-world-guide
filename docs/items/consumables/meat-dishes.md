# ![Elk with Huckleberry Sauce](../../assets/icons/item/elk-with-huckleberry-sauce.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Meat dishes

Meat dishes are cooked at the fire's <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/elk.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[meat](../ingredients/meat.md)</span> and <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/sauces.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[sauces](../ingredients/sauces.md)</span>. Each entry says where you get the dish and what it does; <span class="gi-m">🍲&nbsp;[Buffs by game](../buffs.md)</span> lists the buffs by the game they help.

## ![Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy](../../assets/icons/item/fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy

> In the game: Increases opponent experience loss by 1 in the heist mini-game when attacking. Each Squirrel consumed strengthens your attacks, inflicting greater losses upon your adversaries.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/unstartedcampfire.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)</span> from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/squirrel.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Squirrel](../ingredients/meat.md#squirrel)</span> + 1 <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Sawmill Gravy](../ingredients/sauces.md#sawmill-gravy)</span>; swapped for at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Heists:** the defender loses 1 more experience when you attack (and in your counter-attack) ([more](../../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.md)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>, as a group 1 dish.
