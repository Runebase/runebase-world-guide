# Store consumables

Drinks, brews and elixirs sold in the [store](../../account/store.md), each for one game ([Buffs by game](../buffs.md)).

## ![Fishing Beer](../../assets/icons/item/fishing-beer.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Fishing Beer

> In the game: Adds 2 seconds of fishing for every energy a trip uses: one Fishing Beer per energy.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/party-store.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Party Store](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Fishing:** +2 seconds of fishing per energy, one Fishing Beer per energy ([more](../../games/fishing.md#buffs)).
- **Store:** 3 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 2.25 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Hunting Weed](../../assets/icons/item/hunting-weed.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Hunting Weed

> In the game: Adds 2 seconds of hunting for every energy a trip uses: one Hunting Weed per energy.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/party-store.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Party Store](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hunting:** +2 seconds of hunting per energy, one Hunting Weed per energy ([more](../../games/hunting.md#buffs)).
- **Store:** 3 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 2.25 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Einherjar's Barrier Draught](../../assets/icons/item/einherjars-barrier-draught.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Einherjar's Barrier Draught

> In the game: 100% Chance to block Heist Hits. **This Consumable is a SuperBuff, Only 5 Super Buffs can be consumed each month**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Heists:** a super buff: blocks the next hit on you (as the attacker, a counter-attack) (<span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/einherjars-barrier-draught.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[more](../../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/einherjars-barrier-draught.md)</span>).
- **Store:** 850 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 637.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Forgotten Throne Elixir](../../assets/icons/item/forgotten-throne-elixir.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Forgotten Throne Elixir

> In the game: Resets Crown of Chaos Skills

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Crown of Chaos:** resets every Crown of Chaos skill of both trees, once ([more](../../games/crown-of-chaos/skills/index.md#skill-points)).
- **Store:** 8,000 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 200 credits (2.5%, more with a Trade Post).
