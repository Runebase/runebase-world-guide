# ![Consumables](../../assets/icons/emoji/consumables.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Store consumables

Drinks, brews and elixirs sold in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>, each for one game (<span class="gi-m">🍲&nbsp;[Buffs by game](../buffs.md)</span>).

## ![Fishing Beer](../../assets/icons/item/fishing-beer.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Fishing Beer

> In the game: Adds 2 seconds of fishing for every energy a trip uses: one Fishing Beer per energy.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/party-store.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Party Store](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Fishing:** +2 seconds of fishing per energy, one Fishing Beer per energy ([more](../../games/fishing.md#buffs)).
- **Store:** 3 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 2.25 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Einherjar's Barrier Draught](../../assets/icons/item/einherjars-barrier-draught.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Einherjar's Barrier Draught

> In the game: 100% Chance to block Heist Hits. **This Consumable is a SuperBuff, Only 5 Super Buffs can be consumed each month**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.

- **Store:** 850 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 637.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
