# Dishes

Dishes are cooked at the fire's cooking table from fish or meat and a spice or sauce. Each one is a buff, a heal or a reset for one game.

## Fish dishes

### ![Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../assets/icons/item/anchovy-with-anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Anchovy with Anchovy Powder

> In the game: Restores HP and Mana to full when consumed.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the [cooking table](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Anchovy](ingredients.md#anchovy) + 10 [Anchovy Powder](ingredients.md#anchovy-powder); swapped for at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** heals a character back to full health and mana (a dead one can then return to its battle) ([more](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market), as a group 1 dish.

### ![Mackerel and Smoked Paprika](../assets/icons/item/mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Mackerel and Smoked Paprika

> In the game: Reduces experience loss by 1 in the heist mini-game when defending. Each Mackerel consumed grants a buff for the upcoming hit, bolstering your resilience against your opponents.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the [cooking table](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Mackerel](ingredients.md#mackerel) + 4 [Smoked Paprika](ingredients.md#smoked-paprika); swapped for at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Heists:** 1 less experience lost when you're hit, and when you're counter-attacked as the attacker ([more](../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/mackerel-and-smoked-paprika.md)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market), as a group 1 dish.

### ![Grilled Sardines with Sumac Yogurt Sauce](../assets/icons/item/grilled-sardines-with-sumac-yogurt-sauce.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Grilled Sardines with Sumac Yogurt Sauce

> In the game: Used in Hack'n'Slash RPG-Game for Resetting Skills

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the [cooking table](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Sardines](ingredients.md#sardines) + 3 [Sumac](ingredients.md#sumac); swapped for at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** pays a skill reset (5 in total, whatever the points) ([more](../games/hack-and-slash/skills/index.md)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market), as a group 1 dish.

### ![Baked Cod with Old Bay Butter](../assets/icons/item/baked-cod-with-old-bay-butter.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Baked Cod with Old Bay Butter

> In the game: 25% additional experience on your next QuickBattle

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the [cooking table](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 [Cod](ingredients.md#cod) + 6 [Old Bay Seasoning](ingredients.md#old-bay-seasoning); swapped for at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).

- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market), as a group 2 dish.

### ![Blackened Tilapia with Cumin Slaw](../assets/icons/item/blackened-tilapia-with-cumin-slaw.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Blackened Tilapia with Cumin Slaw
