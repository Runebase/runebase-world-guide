# Dishes

Dishes are cooked at the fire's cooking table from fish or meat and a spice or sauce. Each one is a buff, a heal or a reset for one game.

## Fish dishes

### ![Anchovy with Anchovy Powder](../assets/icons/item/anchovy-with-anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Anchovy with Anchovy Powder

> In the game: Restores HP and Mana to full when consumed.

- **Where you get it:** cooked at the [cooking table](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking) from 2 <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/anchovy.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy](ingredients.md#anchovy)</span> + 10 <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/anchovy-powder.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Anchovy Powder](ingredients.md#anchovy-powder)</span>; swapped for at the [convert market](../account/convert-market.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** heals a character back to full health and mana (a dead one can then return to its battle) ([more](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/convert-market.md), as a group 1 dish.
