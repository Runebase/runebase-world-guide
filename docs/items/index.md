# 🎒 Items and buffs

Everything you can hold, eat or spend across the games: what it is, where you get it, and what it does in each game that uses it. Fish, meat, spices, sauces and dishes wait in your **basket** (`/basket`); scrap, keys and <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Mystery Cards](keys-and-cards/cards-and-relics.md#mystery-card)</span> sit in your **pouch** (`/pouch`).

The catalog comes in five categories, each with a page per kind of item (the table below). Every entry links the games it comes from and the games it's used in, and every game page links back to the entries of the items it names.
