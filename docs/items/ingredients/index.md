# Ingredients

What dishes are cooked from at the fire's [cooking table](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking): fish and spices make the [fish dishes](../consumables/fish-dishes.md), meat and a sauce the [meat dishes](../consumables/meat-dishes.md). Fish come from [fishing](../../games/fishing.md), meat from [hunting](../../games/hunting.md), spices and sauces from the [store](../../account/store.md); they all wait in your **basket** (`/basket`).
