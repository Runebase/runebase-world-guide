# Sauces

Sauces are sold in the [store](../../account/store.md) and cooked with [meat](meat.md) into [meat dishes](../consumables/meat-dishes.md).

## ![Sawmill Gravy](../../assets/icons/item/sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Sawmill Gravy

> In the game: Made with hearty ingredients like sausage and flour, giving it a thick and creamy texture.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md), [quest rewards](../../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Cooking:** cooked into <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/item/fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy](../consumables/meat-dishes.md#fried-squirrel-with-sawmill-gravy)</span>.
- **Store:** 1 credit in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 0.75 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
