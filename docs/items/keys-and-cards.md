# Keys, cards and relics

Treasure keys open chests, Mystery Cards scratch cards, Runes Pattern Cards play Runes Pattern, and Chaos Relics attack the Crown.

## Treasure keys

### Bronze Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the [Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).
- **Key hunt:** each one found scores 1 on the monthly key hunt ladder ([more](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#key-hunt)).

### Silver Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the [Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).
- **Key hunt:** each one found scores 2 on the monthly key hunt ladder ([more](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#key-hunt)).

### Gold Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the [Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).
- **Key hunt:** each one found scores 3 on the monthly key hunt ladder ([more](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#key-hunt)).

## Cards and relics

### Mystery Card

- **Where you get it:** made by the [Party Store](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Scratch cards:** scratches one scratch card ([more](../games/scratch-cards.md)).

### Runes Pattern Card

> In the game: A card for the Runes Pattern Game

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [dice](../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [chat points](../account/community.md#chat-points), [memes](../account/community.md#memes).
- **Runes Pattern:** a card to play Runes Pattern with ([more](../games/runes-pattern.md)).
- **Store:** 50 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 37.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Chaos Relic

> In the game: Mysterious relic imbued with chaotic power. Spend these to challenge the reigning crown holder and prove your worth.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [hunting (extra prizes)](../games/hunting.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../games/wordweaver.md), [dice](../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [Runes Pattern](../games/runes-pattern.md), [chat points](../account/community.md#chat-points), [bumps](../account/community.md#bumps), [votes](../account/community.md#votes), [memes](../account/community.md#memes), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Crown of Chaos:** pays one attack on the Crown, hit or miss ([more](../games/crown-of-chaos/attacking.md)).
- **Store:** 2 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 1.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
