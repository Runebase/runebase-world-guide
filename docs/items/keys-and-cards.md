# Keys, cards and relics

Treasure keys open chests, Mystery Cards scratch cards, Runes Pattern Cards play Runes Pattern, and Chaos Relics attack the Crown.

## Treasure keys

### ![Bronze Key](../assets/icons/item/bronze-key.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Bronze Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/treasure-forge.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).
- **Key hunt:** each one found scores 1 on the monthly key hunt ladder ([more](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#key-hunt)).

### ![Silver Key](../assets/icons/item/silver-key.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Silver Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/treasure-forge.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).
- **Key hunt:** each one found scores 2 on the monthly key hunt ladder ([more](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#key-hunt)).

### ![Gold Key](../assets/icons/item/gold-key.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Gold Key

- **Where you get it:** made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/treasure-forge.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Treasure Forge](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Treasure chests:** opens a treasure chest ([more](../games/treasure-chests.md)).

## Cards and relics

### ![Mystery Card](../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Mystery Card

- **Where you get it:** made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/party-store.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Party Store](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Scratch cards:** scratches one scratch card ([more](../games/scratch-cards.md)).

### ![Runes Pattern Card](../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Runes Pattern Card

> In the game: A card for the Runes Pattern Game

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md); a prize of [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [dice](../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [chat points](../account/chat-points.md), [memes](../account/memes.md).
- **Runes Pattern:** a card to play Runes Pattern with ([more](../games/runes-pattern.md)).
- **Store:** 50 credits in the [store](../account/store.md); sells back for 37.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### ![Chaos Relic](../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Chaos Relic

> In the game: Mysterious relic imbued with chaotic power. Spend these to challenge the reigning crown holder and prove your worth.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [hunting (extra prizes)](../games/hunting.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../games/wordweaver.md), [dice](../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [scratch cards](../games/scratch-cards.md), [Runes Pattern](../games/runes-pattern.md), [chat points](../account/chat-points.md), [bumps](../account/bumps.md), [votes](../account/votes.md), [memes](../account/memes.md), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Crown of Chaos:** pays one attack on the Crown, hit or miss ([more](../games/crown-of-chaos/attacking.md)).
- **Store:** 2 credits in the [store](../account/store.md); sells back for 1.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
