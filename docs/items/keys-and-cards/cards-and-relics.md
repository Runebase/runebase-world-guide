# 🧸 Cards and relics

Mystery Cards scratch <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/goldcard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[scratch cards](../../games/scratch-cards.md)</span>, Runes Pattern Cards play <span class="gi-m">🃏&nbsp;[Runes Pattern](../../games/runes-pattern.md)</span>, and Chaos Relics pay for <span class="gi-m">🤺&nbsp;[attacks on the Crown](../../games/crown-of-chaos/attacking.md)</span>.

## ![Mystery Card](../../assets/icons/item/mystery-card.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Mystery Card

- **Where you get it:** made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/party-store.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Party Store](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; for the 6th Bronze, 4th Silver or 2nd Gold Key you find in <span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[piles](../../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles)</span> within 4 hours; a prize in 5 prize tables (below).
- **Scratch cards:** scratches one scratch card ([more](../../games/scratch-cards.md)).

## ![Runes Pattern Card](../../assets/icons/item/runes-pattern-card.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Runes Pattern Card

> In the game: A card for the Runes Pattern Game

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize in 8 prize tables (below).

## ![Chaos Relic](../../assets/icons/item/chaos-relic.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Chaos Relic

> In the game: Mysterious relic imbued with chaotic power. Spend these to challenge the reigning crown holder and prove your worth.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize in 16 prize tables (below).
- **Crown of Chaos:** pays one attack on the Crown, hit or miss ([more](../../games/crown-of-chaos/attacking.md)).
- **Store:** 2 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 1.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
