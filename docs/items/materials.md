# Scrap, supplies and crafting materials

Scrap and building supplies build and upgrade buildings and craft gear; crafting materials and Engraving Tools are for Hack & Slash.

## Scrap

### ![Wood Scrap](../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Wood Scrap

> In the game: Wood Scrap Resource

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../assets/icons/building/lumber-yard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Lumber Yard](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; swapped for at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../games/wordweaver.md), [chess](../games/chess.md#against-the-bot-singleplayer), [Hnefatafl](../games/hnefatafl.md#against-the-bot-singleplayer), [dice](../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../games/treasure-chests.md), [Runes Pattern](../games/runes-pattern.md), [chat points](../account/community.md#chat-points), [bumps](../account/community.md#bumps), [votes](../account/community.md#votes), [memes](../account/community.md#memes), [quest rewards](../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Cooking:** starts a fire, or adds fuel to one: one each ([more](../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading every building ([costs](../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Crafting:** crafts [fishing rods](../games/fishing.md#rods), [hunting rifles](../games/hunting.md#rifles), [heist gear](../games/heists-and-clans/gear/index.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** crafting a Hack & Slash item takes 1 scrap ([crafting](../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../account/gifts-and-convert-market.md#the-convert-market).
- **Store:** 100 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); can't be sold.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store) (no fixed price: the fewer it has, the dearer); from [disassembling](../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#condition-repairs-and-gear) a Hack & Slash item, or [converting](../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting) 3 of the tier below; a prize of [expeditions](../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
