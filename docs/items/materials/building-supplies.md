# ![Building Supplies](../../assets/icons/emoji/buildingsupplies_emoji.webp){ .gi .gi-title width="64" height="64" loading="lazy" } Building supplies

Building supplies pay for [building and upgrading](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading) buildings.

## ![Toolbox](../../assets/icons/item/toolbox.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Toolbox

> In the game: Toolbox for constructing buildings.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>, [quest rewards](../../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading 12 buildings ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Store:** 1,250 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 937.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Nail](../../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Nail

> In the game: Nail for constructing buildings.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/nail-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail Factory](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from <span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[piles](../../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles)</span> in the channels; a prize in 12 prize tables (below).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading every building ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Store:** 2.5 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 1.875 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
