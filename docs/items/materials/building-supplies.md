# Building supplies

Building supplies pay for [building and upgrading](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading) buildings.

## ![Toolbox](../../assets/icons/item/toolbox.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Toolbox

> In the game: Toolbox for constructing buildings.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); a prize of [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md), [quest rewards](../../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading 12 buildings ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Store:** 1,250 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 937.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

## ![Nail](../../assets/icons/item/nail.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Nail

> In the game: Nail for constructing buildings.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/nail-factory.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Nail Factory](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; a prize of [heists](../../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../../games/wordweaver.md), [dice](../../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../../games/treasure-chests.md), [Runes Pattern](../../games/runes-pattern.md), [chat points](../../account/chat-points.md), [bumps](../../account/bumps.md), [votes](../../account/votes.md), [memes](../../account/memes.md), [quest rewards](../../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading every building ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Store:** 2.5 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 1.875 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
