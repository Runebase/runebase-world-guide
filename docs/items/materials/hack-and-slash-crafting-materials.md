# 🧵 Hack & Slash crafting materials

Crafting materials [craft](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting) Hack & Slash items, and Engraving Tools pay for their inscriptions.

## ![Standard Essence](../../assets/icons/item/standard-essence.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Standard Essence

> In the game: Hack & Slash Crafting Material for crafting **Normal items** of **Normal quality**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span> (no fixed price: the fewer it has, the dearer); from [disassembling](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#condition-repairs-and-gear) a Hack & Slash item, or [converting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting) 3 of the tier below; a prize of <span class="gi-m">🧭&nbsp;[expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Hack & Slash:** crafting a Hack & Slash item takes 3 crafting materials ([crafting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Store:** its price follows the store's stock (<span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[prices by stock](../../account/store.md)</span>); a sale pays 75% of the price at one more.
