# Hack & Slash crafting materials

Crafting materials [craft](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting) Hack & Slash items, and Engraving Tools pay for their inscriptions.

## ![Standard Essence](../../assets/icons/item/standard-essence.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Standard Essence

> In the game: Hack & Slash Crafting Material for crafting **Normal items** of **Normal quality**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md) (no fixed price: the fewer it has, the dearer); from [disassembling](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#condition-repairs-and-gear) a Hack & Slash item, or [converting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting) 3 of the tier below; a prize of [expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** crafting a Hack & Slash item takes 3 crafting materials ([crafting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Store:** its price follows the store's stock ([prices by stock](../../account/store.md)); a sale pays 75% of the price at one more.

## ![Engraving Tool](../../assets/icons/item/engraving-tool.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Engraving Tool

> In the game: Tool for engraving **Runic Inscriptions** onto items to bestow additonal attributes.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md) (no fixed price: the fewer it has, the dearer); a prize of [expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md), [dice](../../games/dice.md), [bumps](../../account/bumps.md), [votes](../../account/votes.md), [memes](../../account/memes.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** pays an inscription: 1 for a Magic one, 3 for a Rare one ([more](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Store:** its price follows the store's stock ([prices by stock](../../account/store.md)); a sale pays 75% of the price at one more.
