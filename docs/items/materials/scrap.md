# Scrap

Wood, leather and metal scrap pay for [building and upgrading](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading) and for crafting; each entry says where.

## ![Wood Scrap](../../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Wood Scrap

> In the game: Wood Scrap Resource

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/lumber-yard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Lumber Yard](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from [piles](../../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles) in the channels; swapped for at the [convert market](../../account/convert-market.md); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Hack & Slash quick battles](../../games/hack-and-slash/quick-battles.md#quick-battles), [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../../games/wordweaver.md), [chess](../../games/chess.md#against-the-bot-singleplayer), [Hnefatafl](../../games/hnefatafl.md#against-the-bot-singleplayer), [dice](../../games/dice.md), [treasure chests](../../games/treasure-chests.md), [Runes Pattern](../../games/runes-pattern.md), [chat points](../../account/chat-points.md), [bumps](../../account/bumps.md), [votes](../../account/votes.md), [memes](../../account/memes.md), [quest rewards](../../account/quests.md#every-quest).
- **Cooking:** starts a fire, or adds fuel to one: one each ([more](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading every building ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Crafting:** crafts [fishing rods](../../games/fishing.md#rods), [hunting rifles](../../games/hunting.md#rifles), [heist gear](../../games/heists-and-clans/gear/index.md).
- **Hack & Slash:** crafting a Hack & Slash item takes 1 scrap ([crafting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the [convert market](../../account/convert-market.md).
- **Store:** 100 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); can't be sold.
