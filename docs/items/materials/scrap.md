# 🔩 Scrap

Wood, leather and metal scrap pay for [building and upgrading](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading) and for crafting; each entry says where.

## ![Wood Scrap](../../assets/icons/item/wood-scrap.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Wood Scrap

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; made by the <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/building/lumber-yard.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[Lumber Yard](../../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does)</span>; collected from <span class="gi-m">🗝️&nbsp;[piles](../../games/piles-and-key-hunt.md#piles)</span> in the channels; swapped for at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>; a prize in 16 prize tables (below).
- **Cooking:** starts a fire, or adds fuel to one: one each ([more](../../games/cooking.md#the-fire-and-cooking)).
- **Buildings:** pays for building and upgrading every building ([costs](../../account/buildings.md#building-and-upgrading)).
- **Crafting:** crafts <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/fishin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[fishing rods](../../games/fishing.md#rods)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🏹&nbsp;[hunting rifles](../../games/hunting.md#rifles)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🛡️&nbsp;[heist gear](../../games/heists-and-clans/gear/index.md)</span>.
- **Hack & Slash:** crafting a Hack & Slash item takes 1 scrap ([crafting](../../games/hack-and-slash/gear-and-crafting.md#crafting)).
- **Convert market:** can pay at the <span class="gi-m">♻️&nbsp;[convert market](../../account/convert-market.md)</span>.
- **Store:** 100 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; can't be sold.
