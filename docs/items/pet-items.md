# Pet items

Pet tokens find pets; food, cures and wash kits care for them. Each comes in six rarities, for the pets of that rarity.

## Pet tokens

### ![Common Pet Token](../assets/icons/item/common-pet-token.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Common Pet Token

> In the game: A token for common pets.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [hunting (extra prizes)](../games/hunting.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [heists](../games/heists-and-clans/attacks-and-defence/index.md#the-heists-prize), [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md), [WordWeaver](../games/wordweaver.md), [dice](../games/dice.md), [chat points](../account/chat-points.md), [bumps](../account/bumps.md), [votes](../account/votes.md), [memes](../account/memes.md).
- **Pets:** consumed to find a pet of its rarity ([getting a pet](../account/pets.md#getting-a-pet)).
- **Store:** 5 credits in the [store](../account/store.md); sells back for 3.75 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
