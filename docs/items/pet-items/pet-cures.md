# Pet cures

A pet cure cures a pet of its rarity ([caring for your pet](../../account/pets.md#caring-for-your-pet)); there's one for each of the six rarities.

## ![Common Pet Cure](../../assets/icons/item/common-pet-cure.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Common Pet Cure

> In the game: Cure for common pets.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../../account/store.md); a prize of [fishing (extra prizes)](../../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [hunting (extra prizes)](../../games/hunting.md#extra-prizes-every-second), [expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions), [Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Pets:** cares for a pet of its rarity ([caring for your pet](../../account/pets.md#caring-for-your-pet)).
- **Store:** 10 credits in the [store](../../account/store.md); sells back for 7.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
