# 📦 Pet tokens

A pet token is spent to find a pet of its rarity ([getting a pet](../../account/pets.md#getting-a-pet)); there's one for each of the six rarities.

## ![Common Pet Token](../../assets/icons/item/common-pet-token.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Common Pet Token

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize in 11 prize tables (below).
- **Pets:** consumed to find a pet of its rarity ([getting a pet](../../account/pets.md#getting-a-pet)).
- **Store:** 5 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 3.75 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
