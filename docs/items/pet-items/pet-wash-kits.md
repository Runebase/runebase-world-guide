# 🛁 Pet wash kits

A pet wash kit washes a pet of its rarity ([caring for your pet](../../account/pets.md#caring-for-your-pet)); there's one for each of the six rarities.

## ![Common Pet Wash Kit](../../assets/icons/item/common-pet-wash-kit.webp){ .gi .gi-entry width="32" height="32" loading="lazy" } Common Pet Wash Kit

- **Where you get it:** bought in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; a prize of <span class="gi-m">![](../../assets/icons/emoji/fishin.webp){ .gi .gi-mention width="20" height="20" loading="lazy" }&nbsp;[fishing (extra prizes)](../../games/fishing.md#extra-prizes-every-second)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🏹&nbsp;[hunting (extra prizes)](../../games/hunting.md#extra-prizes-every-second)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🧭&nbsp;[expeditions](../../games/expeditions.md#the-three-expeditions)</span>, <span class="gi-m">🎰&nbsp;[Loot Reels](../../games/loot-reels.md)</span>.
- **Pets:** cares for a pet of its rarity ([caring for your pet](../../account/pets.md#caring-for-your-pet)).
- **Store:** 5 credits in the <span class="gi-m">🏪&nbsp;[store](../../account/store.md)</span>; sells back for 3.75 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).
