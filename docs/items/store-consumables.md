# Store consumables

Drinks, brews and elixirs sold in the store, each for one game.

### Fishing Beer

> In the game: Adds 2 seconds of fishing for every energy a trip uses: one Fishing Beer per energy.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); made by the [Party Store](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Fishing:** +2 seconds of fishing per energy, one Fishing Beer per energy ([more](../games/fishing.md#buffs)).
- **Store:** 3 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 2.25 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Hunting Weed

> In the game: Adds 2 seconds of hunting for every energy a trip uses: one Hunting Weed per energy.

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); made by the [Party Store](../account/buildings.md#what-each-building-does); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Hunting:** +2 seconds of hunting per energy, one Hunting Weed per energy ([more](../games/hunting.md#buffs)).
- **Store:** 3 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 2.25 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Einherjar's Barrier Draught

> In the game: 100% Chance to block Heist Hits. **This Consumable is a SuperBuff, Only 5 Super Buffs can be consumed each month**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Heists:** a super buff: blocks the next hit on you (as the attacker, a counter-attack) ([more](../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/einherjars-barrier-draught.md)).
- **Store:** 850 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 637.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Skadi's Retribution Brew

> In the game: 100% Chance to Counter Attack Heist Hits. **This Consumable is a SuperBuff, Only 5 Super Buffs can be consumed each month**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Heists:** a super buff: counter-attacks the next hit on you ([more](../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/skadis-retribution-brew.md)).
- **Store:** 850 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 637.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Frigg's Resurgence Elixir

> In the game: 100% Chance to recover Heist Energy. **This Consumable is a SuperBuff, Only 5 Super Buffs can be consumed each month**

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Heists:** a super buff: your next attack keeps its energy ([more](../games/heists-and-clans/buffs/friggs-resurgence-elixir.md)).
- **Store:** 850 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 637.5 credits (75%, more with a Trade Post).

### Forgotten Throne Elixir

> In the game: Resets Crown of Chaos Skills

- **Where you get it:** bought in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); a prize of [Loot Reels](../games/loot-reels.md).
- **Crown of Chaos:** resets every Crown of Chaos skill of both trees, once ([more](../games/crown-of-chaos/skills/index.md#skill-points)).
- **Store:** 8,000 credits in the [store](../account/store.md#the-store); sells back for 200 credits (2.5%, more with a Trade Post).
