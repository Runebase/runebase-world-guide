# Store, trading and cooking

Where credits and items change hands: the store, gifts between players, scratch cards, Runes Pattern, the convert market, and the basket with the fire and the cooking table that turn your catch into dishes. How credits get in and out of the game is in [Credits and RUNES]({{ '/credits-and-runes/' | relative_url }}).

## The store

**Where:** `/store`. Pick a category and an item, then **Buy** or **Sell** and type the amount.

- A purchase costs exactly the price × the amount, and on top of that the game adds **5% of what you pay to the Crown of Chaos pool** (you don't pay it).
- **Selling** pays back a share of the price (most items 75%), from the store's own balance. The **Trade Post** building adds 0.15% of the price per level (75% becomes 90% at level 100).
- An amount that isn't valid, or more than your wallet holds, buys nothing and the store tells you why. Two devices buying with credits for only one purchase: one buys, the other is refused.
- **Scrap:** every day your first 5 scrap cost a quarter of the price (75% off); after that, full price. The store never sells more scrap than it has in stock.
- **Crafting materials** (for Hack & Slash) have no fixed price: deliveries restock the store, and buying lowers the stock and raises the price.

## Gifts

`/gift` sends credits to another member. The receiver gets exactly what you send, it's recorded on both sides, and nothing is created or lost. A gift reaches a member who never used the bot too.

Nothing moves for an amount that isn't valid, a gift to yourself or to a bot, a member who can't be found, or more than you have, and only you see why (the receiver doesn't see it). A gift that goes through is shown in the channel. Two gifts at the same time can never spend the same credits twice.

## Scratch cards

`/scratchcard` in the scratch card channel. A card costs **one Mystery Card** (a scratch credit, from your pouch). Scratch all three fields, then **Collect**: the card pays its combination once.

- RUNES prizes are a share of the realm's RUNES wallet, sent to you as a tip. Other prizes go to your pouch, your basket or your experience (a Gold Key, a Runes Pattern Card, a new Mystery Card…).
- No card is dealt without a Mystery Card, outside the scratch card channel, or when you already have 100 cards open.
- If the tipbot is offline, your Mystery Card is kept.

Mystery Cards come from the Party Store, heists, chests, Loot Reels and other prize tables. The HUG combination also gives 1 experience.

## Runes Pattern

A game for the whole server in the Runes Pattern channel. A word appears in the channel every 20 seconds; click the ones on your card to mark them.

- A word that isn't on your card is a penalty: it costs you one marking.
- The word that completes **the day's current challenge** (a pattern on the card) wins the game: the winner uses **one Runes Pattern Card** and is paid once (experience and a prize roll), and a new game starts. When two players click the winning word at the same moment, there is one winner.
- A win pays **50 experience**; the **day's 3rd challenge pays 150** and counts the day toward the weekly challenge.
- The challenges follow the UTC day.
- Words disappear from the channel when their time is up.

Runes Pattern Cards cost 50 credits in the store; they also drop from many prize tables.

## The basket

`/basket` shows what you gathered and cooked. **Swordfish Kebabs with Lemon and Rosemary** can be eaten from the basket: each one gives **15 experience**. More than you have, or an amount that isn't a whole number, eats nothing.

## The fire and cooking

1. `/fire` costs **one Wood Scrap** and starts a fire in the channel for **10 minutes**. Starting a fire pays **3 experience** and counts on the fire leaderboards. Without wood, or in a channel not every verified member can see and write in, no fire starts, nothing is spent and only you are told why (also from **Activities** → **Cooking Fire** → **Start Fire**).
2. **Add Fuel** costs another Wood Scrap, adds 10 minutes and pays 3 experience. Without wood, you're told so privately and nothing is spent.
3. **Cook** opens your private cooking table. Pick a recipe and an amount: it uses exactly the recipe's ingredients × the amount from your basket, gives you the dishes, pays **1 experience per dish** and moves your cooking quests. Without the ingredients, or with an amount that isn't valid, nothing is spent, and the table says why.

The **Cuisine Cottage** building gives each dish a 1% per level chance of one more; an extra dish pays its experience and counts for your cooking quests like the others. A fire that went out while the bot was offline is put out when the bot starts; its old buttons spend nothing.

## Tips

- Sell what you don't need: most items sell for 75% of their price, more with a Trade Post.
- Buy up to 5 scrap a day: those cost a quarter of the price.
- The convert market turns spare dishes into scrap for buildings.
