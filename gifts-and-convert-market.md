# Gifts and the convert market

Two ways to trade: send credits to another member, or swap scrap and dishes at fixed rates. How credits get in and out of the game is in [Credits and RUNES]({{ '/credits-and-runes/' | relative_url }}).

## Gifts

`/gift` sends credits to another member. The receiver gets exactly what you send, it's recorded on both sides, and nothing is created or lost. A gift reaches a member who never used the bot too.

Nothing moves for an amount that isn't valid, a gift to yourself or to a bot, a member who can't be found, or more than you have, and only you see why (the receiver doesn't see it). A gift that goes through is shown in the channel. Two gifts at the same time can never spend the same credits twice.

## The convert market

`/convert` swaps scrap and dishes at fixed rates. Pick what you want and what you pay with: the price is in the column of what you pay with. For example, **2 Wood Scrap buy 10 Anchovy with Anchovy Powder**, and **20 Anchovy with Anchovy Powder buy 1 Wood Scrap**. Each conversion moves exactly the listed amounts; without enough to pay, nothing moves. A double click converts once.
