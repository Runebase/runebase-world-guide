# The Runebase bot games

The Runebase bot runs a set of games in the Runebase Discord server and its partner servers ("realms"). They're all connected: what you catch, find and build in one game helps you in the others, and almost everything pays **experience** (your rank) and **credits** (the bot's money, which you can withdraw as Credits tokens on the Runebase blockchain).

> **Coming soon:** this guide describes the bot's next update (v0.173.0). Until it's live, some rules and numbers on the bot are still the old ones.

**Start here:** chat in the server (your first message creates your account), then type `/start`. The main menu shows your rank card; **Activities** lists the games.

## How the games connect

- **Every day at 00:00 UTC** your energy refills (fishing, hunting, expeditions; heists: 3 energy, and you can be hit 6 times again), and the buildings' nightly production waits for you in **Collect**. Through the day: 15 Loot Reels spins, a WordWeaver word every 4 hours, a single-player chess game every 3 hours (5 paid wins a day) and Hnefatafl (5 paid wins a day), the dice on a cooldown, a chat payout every 12 hours, the bump and vote windows. Piles and keys appear in the channels, and Runes Pattern words keep coming.
- **Materials:** fishing, hunting and expeditions fill your basket and pouch (fish, meat, scrap). The fire and the cooking table turn fish, meat, spices and sauces into dishes: buffs for the activities, the heists, Crown of Chaos and Hack & Slash. Scrap (wood, metal, leather) from piles and activities pays for buildings, fire fuel and crafting; building supplies (Toolbox, Nail, Rope) come from piles, prizes and the factories.
- **Buildings** (each level needs a rank) give lasting bonuses: more energy, longer expeditions, better store prices, stronger heists and battles, and nightly production.
- **Credits** come from the games' prizes, quests, piles, chests and minigame wins, and from the Iron Bank's interest. You spend them in the store, on gifts, scratch cards, Runes Pattern cards and clan registration. Credits come in from RUNES (or Credits token) deposits and Hermes purchases, and go out as Credits tokens through withdrawals; some prizes (dice, chests, scratch cards, Crown of Chaos) pay RUNES directly.
- **Experience** comes from nearly everything and decides your **rank**. Heists take experience from the defender and give experience to the attacker. Most games also have all-time and monthly **leaderboards** with roles for the top players, and the heist and key hunt ladders can pay monthly RUNES prizes.
- **Pets** help in fishing, hunting, expeditions and heists while they're awake and cared for.

## What you can count on

- What a screen promises is what you're paid.
- A double click, or two devices at once, never pays twice or spends twice.
- If the bot restarts, nothing you earned is lost: trips, the nightly production and your games pick up where they were. While the bot is starting up, it answers "The bot is starting up…" and does nothing.
- When something is refused (not enough energy, credits or items), you get a clear message and nothing is spent.
- Refusals only you see, with the reason: a button or menu that isn't yours, a command used too often, the dice on cooldown, a chest without a key, a scratch card outside its channel, a refused collect or craft, the bot starting up, an old button, `/fish` and `/hunt` without energy (with your fishing or hunting menu) or while you're busy, `/fire` without wood or in a channel where fires can't burn, a `/gift` you can't make (the receiver doesn't see it). What succeeds stays where it was: a trip, a fire and a gift still show in the channel.
- Buttons on old messages from an earlier version of the bot only show a note.
