# Minigames

Four games in the Activities menu: **Loot Reels**, **WordWeaver**, **Chess** and **Hnefatafl**. Each has its own leaderboards (all-time and monthly).

**Where:** `/start` → **Activities**.

## Loot Reels

A spin pays exactly what it shows, once. When the day's spins are used up, double clicks and old Spin buttons pay nothing more. Each spin counts on the Loot Reels leaderboards.

## WordWeaver

Guess a hidden word of 5 to 8 letters.

1. **Get new word** deals the word.
2. **Guess Word** opens a box to type your guess. You have **six guesses**: after the sixth wrong one the game is over and pays nothing, and a seventh is refused. A word that doesn't exist is refused and doesn't cost a guess.
3. Solving the word pays **20 experience**, a prize from the table below and one point on the WordWeaver leaderboards. It pays once; an old Guess button pays nothing.
4. Each solved word counts 1 toward the **WordWeaver - Solved** quest (a wrong guess counts nothing). A solve that completes it shows the quest's rewards with the word's and pays them once, the quest's experience on top of the 20.

A new word comes **4 hours** after your last one was dealt.

## Chess

### Against the bot (Singleplayer)

**Chess** → **Singleplayer**: pick a difficulty and play against the bot. Pick your move from the menus or type it (SAN Input); a move typed wrong is refused and the bot doesn't move.

- **Only a win pays:** experience, credits and maybe scrap (table below), and chess points on the leaderboards:

  | Difficulty | Experience | Chess points |
  |---|---|---|
  | easy | 15 | 2 |
  | medium | 20 | 3 |
  | hard | 35 | 5 |

- A loss pays nothing, and neither does a draw (a stalemate, for example).
- **Only the first 5 wins of a UTC day pay.** The next day pays again.
- A new game can start **3 hours** after your last one started.
- Wins also complete the Chess - Win quests, which pay their own experience and rewards.
- A win pays once, even when the winning move is clicked twice.



| Difficulty | Credits (always) | Also, each on its own chance |
|---|---|---|
| easy | 15 to 25 credits | 1 Leather Scrap 25%, 1 Metal Scrap 25%, 1 Wood Scrap 25% |
| medium | 25 to 50 credits | 2 Leather Scrap 25%, 2 Metal Scrap 25%, 2 Wood Scrap 25% |
| hard | 50 to 85 credits | 3 Leather Scrap 25%, 3 Metal Scrap 25%, 3 Wood Scrap 25% |



Credits always come; each scrap rolls on its own.

### Against other players (Multiplayer)

Challenge another player. Only your own moves count: when your move and the turn timer's random move land at the same moment, your move is played; a resignation and a move at the same moment end the game once, the way that came first.

**If the bot restarts during your game,** a restart never counts against you: the player to move gets a DM and **15 minutes** before any timer counts again (a blitz clock is paused through them).

## Hnefatafl

The Viking board game, new in this release. Two sides on an **11 × 11** board (the Copenhagen rules):

- **The attackers** (24 pieces) move first and win by **surrounding the King on all four sides**. A King on the edge of the board can't be captured.
- **The defenders** (12 pieces and the King, who starts on the throne in the middle) win when **the King reaches a corner**.
- Pieces move in straight lines, any number of free squares, and can't jump. Only the King may stop on the throne or the corners.
- A piece is captured when it's **sandwiched** between two enemies (a corner and the empty throne count as enemies). Moving into a sandwich yourself is safe. A row of defenders along the edge can be captured by a shieldwall.
- A side with no legal move loses.
- A move that breaks the rules is refused and the board stays as it was.

### Against the bot (Singleplayer)

A win pays credits and maybe scrap, by difficulty (table below). **Only the first 5 wins of a UTC day pay**; the 6th says so and pays nothing. Hnefatafl gives no experience: its Win quests pay their other rewards.



| Difficulty | Credits (always) | Also, each on its own chance |
|---|---|---|
| easy | 15 to 25 credits | 1 Leather Scrap 25%, 1 Metal Scrap 25%, 1 Wood Scrap 25% |
| medium | 25 to 50 credits | 2 Leather Scrap 25%, 2 Metal Scrap 25%, 2 Wood Scrap 25% |
| hard | 50 to 85 credits | 3 Leather Scrap 25%, 3 Metal Scrap 25%, 3 Wood Scrap 25% |



### Against other players (ranked)

Multiplayer games score **ranked points** on the Hnefatafl leaderboards: **25 for a win, 5 for a loss, 12 each for a draw**. To stop farming:

- a game only scores when both sides made at least 10 moves (a game resigned in its first moves scores nothing);
- the same two players score at most **3** ranked games a day (another opponent still scores);
- a player scores at most **5** ranked games a day, whoever the opponents are;
- you can't challenge yourself.

No game ends in a draw today, though: playing the same position over and over doesn't end the game (a repetition draw isn't counted), so a game goes on until someone wins, resigns or runs out of time.

Only the side to move may move, each player from their own screen. An abandoned game times out once, within the same limits. A restart never counts against you: as in chess, the player to move gets a DM and 15 minutes.

## Tips

- Loot Reels are free: spin your 15 every day.
- Chess and Hnefatafl pay only 5 wins a day each; a hard win pays the most.
- WordWeaver: a word that doesn't exist costs nothing, so try a real word that tests many letters.
