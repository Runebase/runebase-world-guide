    🏗️ Buildings¶

    Buildings are yours alone: 3 resource buildings and 23 facilities you build and upgrade with building supplies and scrap. Most work on their own, wherever their effect applies (more energy, longer expeditions, better store prices, stronger heists and battles). Some produce something every night or let you make something every day, which you pick up in the Collect menu.

    Where: /buildings to build and upgrade. /start → Activities → Collect for  The Inn,  Treasure Forge,  Party Store,  Nail Factory,  Rope Factory and the  Iron Bank.

    Building and upgrading¶

    Pick the type and the building, then Build the next level.

    • Cost:  Toolbox,  Nail and  Rope (building supplies) and  Wood,  Metal and  Leather Scrap. No credits. The screen shows exactly what the Build button takes.
    • Rank: each building has a "rank per level": level 3 of a building with 5 ranks per level needs rank 15.
    • Last level: 100 divided by the ranks per level, rounded down. A building at its last level can't go higher.
    • With one resource short, or your rank too low, the Build button is off and nothing is taken. A double click builds once and charges once; a Build from an older screen that no longer fits takes nothing, and the buildings screen says why (not enough, your rank, the last level).
    • The Preview screen shows what a level gives (its perks, the numbers the building then works with) and its rank, starting at the level you have; the arrows step one level, ... jumps to any level (a level that doesn't exist changes nothing: the preview keeps the level it showed and says which levels there are). It also shows the total cost from nothing to the last level.

    Every night: production¶

    At 00:00 UTC the resource buildings put their scrap in your pouch (up to its room),  The Inn adds experience to its pool, and the  Iron Bank adds interest to its pool. Each building produces once a day: if the bot restarts in the middle of the night's production, it finishes the day for exactly the buildings it hadn't reached, and pays nobody twice. If the bot is down at midnight, the day is produced once when it's back; a day the bot was down for entirely isn't made up. Resource buildings keep the fraction of a scrap they couldn't give (3.6 a day pays 3 and keeps 0.6 for the next night).

    Collecting¶

    In Collect, each building shows what's waiting and what a collect costs. When the pool is empty, the day's collects are used up, or you're short of the scrap a collect costs, the button is off and nothing happens. A double click, or two devices, collect once: a click that comes too late collects nothing, and the collect screen says why ("there's nothing to collect", today's collects used, the scrap it costs).

    • The day's collects start again at 00:00 UTC: a collect at 23:59:59 UTC uses that day's, and at 00:00 UTC the next day's are there.
    • A  Nail Factory or  Rope Factory collect that completes a Collector quest shows the quest's rewards on the collect screen.

    The Iron Bank¶

    The Iron Bank keeps credits for you and pays interest on them.

    1. Deposit credits, up to what your level can store.
    2. Every night it adds 0.25% of what's stored to its pool, up to the pool's size. Collect the pool for a few  Metal Scrap.
    3. To take your credits out, Unbound them first. After the unbounding period you Withdraw them. Nothing is earned while they unbound.

    Every credit the Iron Bank moves is in your wallet's history. A double click on Collect or Withdraw pays once, and a double click on Start Unbounding starts it once.

    When the bank says no, its screen says why (as public or private as it is), and nothing moves: a deposit that isn't a positive amount (the smallest is 0.00000001 credits; typed as a plain number, rounded down to 8 decimals: 1.000000009 deposits 1, and 0x10 or 1e3 is refused), more than your wallet holds or more than the bank can still store; and anything an older screen still offers that no longer fits: a deposit while your credits unbound, unbounding when nothing is stored, a withdrawal before the unbounding period ends or when nothing unbounds. A second Withdraw (a double click, another device) is told there's nothing left to withdraw.

    If something goes wrong while you deposit, start unbounding or withdraw, its screen says so ("Something went wrong, nothing was withdrawn."), nothing of it happened, and you can try again.

    Tips¶

    • Upgrading needs both the resources and the rank: check the Preview for what each level gives and the whole road to the last level.
    • Collect  The Inn and the  Iron Bank before their pools fill up: a full pool earns nothing more.
    • Most buildings work without you doing anything; the energy buildings add their energy at the daily refill (00:00 UTC).
