    ♻️ The convert market¶

    /convert swaps scrap and dishes at fixed rates.

    Pick what you want and what you pay with: the price is in the column of what you pay with. For example, 2  Wood Scrap buy 10  Anchovy with Anchovy Powder, and 20 Anchovy with Anchovy Powder buy 1 Wood Scrap. Each conversion moves exactly the listed amounts; without enough to pay, or without room in your pouch for scrap, nothing moves, and the convert screen says why (also when an older screen still shows the Convert button). A double click converts once.
