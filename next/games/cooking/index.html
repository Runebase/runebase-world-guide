    Cooking¶

    Where to play: Discord

    The fire and the cooking table turn the fish and meat in your basket, with spices and sauces from the 🏪 store, into dishes: buffs for fishing, hunting, expeditions, the heists, Crown of Chaos and Hack & Slash.

    The fire and cooking¶

    1. /fire costs one  Wood Scrap and starts a fire in the channel for 10 minutes. Starting a fire pays 3 experience and counts on the fire leaderboards. Without wood, or in a channel not every verified member can see and write in, no fire starts, nothing is spent and only you are told why (from Activities → Cooking Fire → Start Fire, the fire guide says it, as public or private as it is).
    2. Add Fuel costs another  Wood Scrap, adds 10 minutes and pays 3 experience. Without wood, you're told so privately and nothing is spent.
    3. Cook opens your private cooking table. Pick a recipe and an amount: it uses exactly the recipe's ingredients × the amount from your basket, gives you the dishes, pays 1 experience per dish and moves your cooking  quests. Without the ingredients, or with an amount that isn't valid (not a whole number, 0, or more than 5,000 at once), nothing is spent, and the table says why.

    The  Cuisine Cottage building gives each dish a 1% per level chance of one more; an extra dish pays its experience and counts for your cooking quests like the others. Cooking at two fires at the same moment works: both cooks are paid, and your quests count every dish. A fire that went out while the bot was offline is put out when the bot starts; one whose end the bot missed while running is put out within the hour; its old buttons spend nothing.

    What the dishes are for¶

    Every dish, the game it helps and what it does there (each dish's entry in Items and buffs, under  fish dishes or  meat dishes, says where else you get it):

    Dish Used in What it does
    Anchovy with Anchovy Powder 👹 Hack & Slash heals a character back to full health and mana (a dead one can then return to its battle)
    Mackerel and Smoked Paprika  Heists 1 less experience lost when you're hit, and when you're counter-attacked as the attacker
    Grilled Sardines with Sumac Yogurt Sauce 💥 Hack & Slash pays a skill reset (5 in total, whatever the points)
    Baked Cod with Old Bay Butter 👹 Hack & Slash +25% experience on a quick battle, picked before it or on the results screen and spent once
    Seared Tuna with Wasabi Butter  Heists 3% chance to counter-attack when you're hit
    Blackened Tilapia with Cumin Slaw 🧪 Crown of Chaos 15 less damage from each attack on you as the Crown, one charge per attack
    Grilled Eel with Sichuan Peppercorns  Heists 5% chance to keep your energy when you attack
    Pan-Fried Trout with Lemon and Dill  Fishing one more energy of fishing when the day's energy is gone
    Swordfish Kebabs with Lemon and Rosemary 🧺 Your basket eaten from the basket for experience
    Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy  Heists the defender loses 1 more experience when you attack (and in your counter-attack)
    Braised Rabbit with Mustard Cream Sauce 🧭 Expeditions +2 seconds per energy on an expedition, one charge per energy
    Grilled Coyote Steak With Lingonberry Sauce 👹 Hack & Slash pays a stats reset (half a steak per point spent, rounded up)
    Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze  Heists +2 experience when you attack (and in your counter-attack)
    Pheasant with Blackberry Balsamic Glaze 👹 Hack & Slash fills your stamina to its maximum (Restore Stamina now)
    White-tailed Deer with Juniper Berry Demi-Glace 🧪 Crown of Chaos +15 damage on your attack on the Crown, one charge per attack
    Elk with Huckleberry Sauce 👹 Hack & Slash double damage on a quick battle, picked before it or on the results screen and spent once
    Moose with Blueberry Sauce  Heists 10% chance to block when you're hit; as the attacker, it blocks a counter-attack
    Wild Boar with Cherry Sauce 🏹 Hunting one more energy of hunting when the day's energy is gone
