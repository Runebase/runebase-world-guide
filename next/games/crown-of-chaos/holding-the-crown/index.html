    👑 Holding the Crown¶

    Where to play: Discord

    The Crown's skills¶

    Only the Crown has these buttons. They cost nothing but their cooldown; clicking one again while it cools down changes nothing. When one can't be used (it cools down, there's nothing to remove, the Crown is silenced), your Crown of Chaos menu says why, and nothing changes.

    Skill What it does
    Resurgence heals 3% of the maximum health each of the next 10 rounds, never above the maximum; then waits an hour
    Blood Purge removes every bleed on the Crown, whoever cast it (seeds stay). With no bleed on the Crown it's refused (your menu says why) and stays ready
    Defuse removes every seed waiting to detonate. With none, it's refused ("No detonations to defuse", on your menu) and stays ready
    Mana Drain while it lasts, every hit on the Crown (not a miss) drains 10% of the attacker's mana, never below 0
    Bubble while it lasts, attacks bounce off for free, bleeds on the Crown deal 0 but still run down, and a seed that detonates deals nothing and starts no  Thornburst bleed (it's used up, and nothing of it hits after the Bubble). Remove Bubble ends it early. Then it waits an hour

    Their base cooldowns and durations are on the overview; what the defensive skills change, on their  pages.

    Checked in play¶

    • Resurgence: Heals 3% of the maximum health each of the next 10 rounds (30 at 1,000; 33 with  Throne of Endurance 1; 90 with Throne 20), never above it. Ready again in an hour, and the answer says when; +20 on the weekly ladder.
    • Blood Purge: Removes every bleed, whoever cast it, and leaves the seeds; +5 a bleed it removed. Then it waits 1,200 s, less  Urgent Embind's 45 s a rank, and its answer says until when. With no bleed on the Crown it's refused, on the Crown's menu, and stays ready. A bleed that has ticked its last round is gone at once, and  Hemostasis keeps one with no rounds from being put on; one with no rounds left from an older reign counts for nothing (refused when it's the only one).
    • Defuse: Removes every seed, whoever planted it, and leaves the bleeds; +5 a seed it removed. Then it waits 900 s, less  Swift Disarmament's 30 s a rank, and its answer says until when. With no seed it's refused, on the Crown's menu, and stays ready. A seed is gone once it detonates; one left from an older reign with no rounds counts for nothing (refused when it's the only one).
    • Mana Drain: For 300 s (plus  Siphon's Grasp) each hit drains 10% of the attacker's 1,000 mana after the skill's own mana, never more than is left (the message says what it took); a miss or a dodge drains nothing. +2 a drained hit, also one that found no mana left. Ready again after 45 minutes.
    • Bubble: For 300 s every attack bounces off, whatever its skill: no relic, mana, buff charge or cooldown spent, nothing applied or stolen, nothing on the weekly ladder. The round goes on under it: bleeds and seeds deal 0 and run down (a seed is spent, no Thornburst), regeneration, a Resurgence, mana, points and tax piles go on. +15; ready again in an hour; Remove Bubble ends it.
    • Silenced: When an attacker's  Silence lands, Resurgence, Blood Purge, Defuse and Mana Drain are refused (the Crown's menu says why), even from an old button, and stay ready; a Resurgence or Mana Drain already running goes on; the Bubble works.

    Defensive runes¶

    In Defensive Runes, pick up to 24 runes (the first becomes your current rune), move them up or down, and set the current one. Each line shows the rune's value in the running reign. While you hold the Crown, each attack faces your current rune and then moves it to the next in your list. A change you make while an attack is under way waits for that attack and is kept. Each attack report shows the Crown's defense streak (the attacks it has survived this reign), and your best streak is kept.

    Pets¶

    Your active pet's holding perks count while you hold the Crown, it's awake and its stats meet the perks' conditions (🐾 Pets): extra maximum health (flat or %), more health regenerated every 5th point (or every 4th with the Shadow Hound), a dodge chance, less damage from each hit, a longer wait for each attacker, a stronger Resurgence (the Phoenix Chick: 4.25% a round). The Crown menu shows each with its number, and a new reign starts at your maximum health with your pet's bonus. Asleep, they add nothing; your pet's attacking perks don't help you hold the Crown. Checked in play for every pet.

    Buildings¶

    🏗️ Buildings help the Crown:  Bastion of the Chosen (+15 maximum health per level),  Pillar of the Undying (every 5th point heals 1 + 3 per level) and  Temporal Nexus (+1 second per level on each attacker's cooldown). A new Crown with  Throne of Endurance starts with 1,000 × (1 + 10% per rank) health.
