    🧭 Expeditions¶

    Where to play: Discord

    An idle activity: you spend energy, wait while your expedition runs, and come back to what it found. Expeditions bring back scrap, crafting materials and credits.  Fishing and 🏹 hunting work the same way.

    Where: /start → Activities → Expeditions lets you pick how much energy to spend, and holds the Expedition Buffs menu.

    How a trip works¶

    1. You choose how much energy to spend. Each energy is 10 seconds of trip, longer with a resource building (see below).
    2. While the trip runs you're busy: you can't start another expedition, fishing trip or hunt until it's over (your expeditions screen tells you that you're already busy and until when, with the way back to the menu). That notice has the expedition's own button: press it once you're free to start it with 1 energy.
    3. Every second of the trip is one roll for its scrap, and one roll on the trip's extra prizes (crafting materials, pet items, tokens, credits and more; the table is at the end of this page).
    4. Each scrap found is its own prize, as in fishing and hunting: when your pouch is full, the scrap that still fits is paid and the rest isn't. The result lists what was paid and, apart, what your pouch had no room for (Unable to loot Pouch full); Today's loot on the menu counts only what was paid (with the trips' experience and quest rewards).
    5. At the end you get what it found, your experience and your quest progress, and the trip counts on the leaderboards.

    A trip pays exactly once. Clicking twice, or using two devices, starts one trip and spends the energy once. If the bot restarts during your trip, the trip is finished when the bot is back, and paid once.

    Energy¶

    Activity Energy each day Most energy Seconds per energy
    Expeditions 5 + Expedition Command Center level 25 (level 20, rank 100) 10 + the level of the expedition's resource building (+2 per Braised Rabbit charge)
    • Energy refills at 00:00 UTC every day. The refill sets your energy to the day's full amount; it doesn't add to what you had left.
    • A trip that runs over midnight still pays once.
    • Expeditions have no dish for more energy (fishing and hunting have one): without expedition energy, expeditions can't be started. If you click an expedition without the energy it needs (say, spent on another device), the menu says so (it shows your energy as it is now) and nothing is spent.

    Experience, quests and leaderboards¶

    An expedition pays 3 experience per energy.

    Expeditions also move your  quests: the expedition quests count the seconds of your expeditions. A quest that reaches its goal completes on its own and pays its reward once.

    The expedition board counts expedition seconds.

    The three expeditions¶

    Each looks for one kind of scrap:

    Expedition Finds, chance each second Longer with Better odds with
    Deep Excavation 1 Metal Scrap 8%  Iron Mine (+1 second per energy per level) Mining Pick
    Woodland Logging Expedition 1 Wood Scrap 8%  Lumber Yard (+1 second per energy per level) Wood Axe
    Nature Expedition 1 Leather Scrap 8%  Tannery (+1 second per energy per level) Skinning Knife
    • Each energy of an expedition lasts 10 seconds plus the level of its resource building ( Iron Mine for Deep Excavation,  Lumber Yard for Woodland Logging,  Tannery for Nature Expedition): with Iron Mine level 2, one energy of Deep Excavation is 12 seconds, 12 rolls.
    • The expedition menu shows each expedition's time for the energy you pick and its scrap chance (with your utility and pet), the way the trip then uses them. Expedition Secondary Loot Info lists the extra prizes.
    • Credits from an expedition go straight into your wallet.

    Utilities: Wood Axe, Skinning Knife, Mining Pick¶

    Crafted for credits in /start → Inventory → Utilities. Every level adds 0.05 percentage points to the chance of finding its scrap: on its expedition (⛏️ Mining Pick on Deep Excavation, 🪓 Wood Axe on Woodland Logging, 🔪 Skinning Knife on Nature Expedition), in fishing's extra prizes (Mining Pick for  Metal Scrap, Wood Axe for  Wood Scrap), in hunting's (Skinning Knife for  Leather Scrap), and in the other prize tables that have that scrap (votes, bumps, chat, dice, heists, treasure chests and more). With exactly the price in your wallet you can craft it. Level n needs rank n (the screen shows it as "User Level"): below it the craft button is off, and a click on an older one crafts nothing and tells you, privately, the rank you need.

    Buffs¶

    Buff Where it comes from Used by
    Braised Rabbit with Mustard Cream Sauce cooked: 2  Rabbit + 2  Mustard Cream Sauce expeditions
    •  Braised Rabbit with Mustard Cream Sauce: eat it in Expeditions → Expedition Buffs. Each one is a charge that adds 2 seconds to one energy of an expedition: an expedition uses one charge per energy. Fishing and hunting never use them.
    • The expedition menu shows the buffs you have and the trip time they give.

    Pets¶

    Your active pet helps while it's awake and cared for (see 🐾 Pets for every pet, its perks and when they work). Its expedition perk adds its points to one expedition's scrap. Asleep, or below the perk's conditions, the pet adds nothing. The pet counts if it's active and awake when the trip ends: a pet changed during the trip gives its perks, one put to sleep gives none.

    Buildings that help¶

    All in 🏗️ Buildings:

    •  Expedition Command Center: +1 expedition energy a day per level.
    • Lumber Yard, Iron Mine, Tannery: +1 second per energy on their expedition, per level.
    •  Arcane Reliquary: the expeditions' crafting materials are 0.25% (of their own chance) likelier per level.

    Everything adds up once: a utility, a pet perk, the resource building, the Command Center and  Braised Rabbit on one expedition each count exactly once.

    Tips¶

    • Spend the day's energy before 00:00 UTC: the refill doesn't carry leftovers over.
    •  Braised Rabbit is used one per energy: a 10-energy expedition uses 10.
