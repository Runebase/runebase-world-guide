    Shadow Warrior¶

    Where to play: Discord

    • Class:  Assassin
    • Tree:  Shadow Disciplines
    • Kind: a summon
    • Learn it from: level 18 (your rank)
    • Needs first:  Weapon Block,  Cloak of Shadows
    • Opens:  Shadow Master
    • Weapon: any
    • Mana: 2 at rank 1, 40 at rank 20

    In the game: Summons a shadow warrior that copies the Assasin's attacks.

    How it works¶

    • A minion fights at your side for its rounds.
    • It costs its mana once, when used. Without enough mana, or without the weapon or shape it needs, its button says Attack and makes the regular attack instead, for free.

    Checked in play¶

    • At rank 20 the Shadow Warrior has your armor +228%.
