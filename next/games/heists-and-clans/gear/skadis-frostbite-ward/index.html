    Skadi's Frostbite Ward¶

    Where to play: Discord

    • Slot: ring
    • When attacking: Gain Retribution Fury (Counter Attack Buff) +1%
    • When defending: Block Chance +0.25%
    • Crafting cost: 25  Wood Scrap, 200  Metal Scrap, 75  Leather Scrap

    Checked in play¶

    • 1% more to earn a Retribution Fury charge (3% + 1% with the Gain Retribution Fury attack).
    • Exactly 0.25% to block a hit, and 0.25% to block a counter-attack against you when you're the raider.
    • Its numbers are the ones on the gear screen, the crafting screen and the heist menu (Heist - Modifiers and Heist - Stats). Worn on the other side of a heist, or taken off, it does nothing; every slot adds up.

    How gear works¶

    You craft it with scrap from the heist gear menu (exactly the scrap listed) and wear one item per slot. An attacking bonus works when you attack, a defending bonus when you defend. A counter-attack is the defender's attack and the raider's defence for experience: an attacking experience bonus also adds to your counter-attack when you defend, and a defending block or experience bonus also protects you when you're counter-attacked as the raider; a counter-attack never gives energy back or earns a buff. Without Tactical Shift your neutral set counts both ways; with it, the offensive set attacks and the defensive set defends, and in a counter-attack each side keeps the set it's wearing (the raider their offensive set, the defender their defensive set). Every slot adds up. More: 🛡️ heist gear.

    Other items for the ring slot:  Baldur's Gleaming Band,  Einherjar's Wrathful Loop.
