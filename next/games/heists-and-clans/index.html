    Heists and clans¶

    Where to play: Discord

    In a heist you steal experience from another player, not credits. Buffs, gear, pets, a building and a clan change the odds. Every heist counts on a monthly ladder; its best players win a role, and the ladder can pay  RUNES prizes.

    Where: /heist (pick the player and the attack type), or /start → Activities → Heist for the heist menu (joining, buffs, gear, clans, the attack guide). /heistboard shows the ladders, /browseclans lists the clans.

    Joining and leaving¶

    • Join from the heist menu (Join → Confirm join). You need a minimum amount of experience, which the menu shows ("You need at least … experience to join the heist games!"). Only players who joined can heist and be heisted.
    • If your experience drops below that minimum after a heist, you lose your place in the heist game.
    • Leaving puts you in a protective bubble: nobody can heist you, and you can't heist. The bubble has limits:
    Rule Limit
    Leaving the heist (a bubble), per calendar month 3
    Earliest bubble after joining 2 days
    No bubble in the last days of the month 3 days
    Waiting time to rejoin after bubble 1 of the month 24 hours
    Waiting time to rejoin after bubble 2 of the month 48 hours
    Registering a clan 15,000 credits
    Members in a clan at most 5
    Accepted joins per clan per month at most 10
    • After your 3rd bubble of a month you can't rejoin until the next month begins: the heist menu's join button stays locked, and /rolepanel (the public panel) refuses privately.
    • Joining stays open in the month's last 3 days; only leaving is closed then.
    • Left in an earlier month, or lost your place to low experience? You can rejoin right away once you have enough experience again, also in a new month.
    • /rolepanel (the heist role's Earn or Remove button) does the same: leaving within 2 days of joining, or rejoining before the wait is over, is refused with the time you can: privately under the public role panel, on the heist menu from its join and leave screens.
    • Being heisted is no bubble: you stay in the heist, protected only by the 6 hits a day.
    • A week without an attack and you leave the heist: 7 days after you join, and 7 days after each of your attacks, you're out of the heist if you haven't attacked since (being heisted doesn't count). It isn't a bubble: you can rejoin at once, and from your rejoin you have a new week. The heist channel names the players who left.

    Energy and hits¶

    • You have 3 heist energy a day, and you can be heisted 6 times a day. Both refill at 00:00 UTC, when the bot's midnight refill runs (a few seconds after midnight it may still count the old day). A heist counts on the ladder of the month it's made in: 23:59:59 on the last day is that month, 00:00:00 on the 1st the new one.
    • An attack costs 1 energy (unless you recover it: one heist). Every heist on you uses one of your 6 daily hits, also a blocked one.
    • Mímisbrunnr (in the heist menu): sacrifice an eye, once a month, to refill your heist energy and your daily hits. It costs nothing else (experience, credits, buffs and your score stay as they are), counts for your clan too, and is told in the heist channel. It's closed in the last 3 days of the month. A second eye the same month, or one from outside the heist, is refused privately; a double click takes one eye.

    A heist is refused, and nothing changes, when you're out of energy, when you target yourself, a player in a bubble or who never joined, a player who left the server, a player already hit 6 times today, or a member of your own clan; also when the player bubbles or leaves the server while your heist is starting, and when the heist would pay you more than 15,000 experience at once (with a big backlog of heist quests: see  quests). If you haven't joined, /heist tells you heists aren't enabled for you; on a server not set up for heists (no heist role, or no heist channel for the heists' log), it says so. Every refusal is private, also from the heist menu's attack guide. A double click on the guide's Attack button attacks once: the second click is told the attack was already made.

    The buttons under every heist log (Heist, the monthly ladders, Legend) work for anyone and answer only the one who clicks.

    Tips¶

    • Every charge that applies to your side is used on every heist: eat buffs for the heists you're about to do.
    • A clan protects you (1 less experience lost) and keeps clanmates from heisting you.
    • Super buffs are limited to 5 a month.
