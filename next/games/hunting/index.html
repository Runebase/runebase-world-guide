    🏹 Hunting¶

    Where to play: Discord

    An idle activity: you spend energy, wait while your trip runs, and come back to a catch. Hunting fills your basket with meat for  cooking.  Fishing and 🧭 expeditions work the same way.

    Where: /hunt goes on a one-energy trip straight away. /start → Activities → Hunt lets you pick how much energy to spend, and holds the crafting, equipment and buff menus.

    How a trip works¶

    1. You choose how much energy to spend. Each energy is 10 seconds of trip.
    2. While the trip runs you're busy: you can't start another hunt, fishing trip or expedition until it's over (the bot tells you that you're already busy and until when: privately after /hunt, on the menu after its button).
    3. Every second of the trip is one roll for a catch, and one roll on the trip's extra prizes (scrap, pet items, tokens and more; the table is at the end of this page).
    4. Each scrap found is its own prize: when your pouch is full, the scrap that still fits is paid and the rest isn't. The result lists what was paid and, apart, what your pouch had no room for (Unable to loot Pouch full); Today's loot on the hunting menu counts only what was paid (with the trips' experience and quest rewards).
    5. At the end you get your catch, your experience and your quest progress, and the trip counts on the leaderboards.

    A trip pays exactly once. Clicking twice, or using two devices, starts one trip and spends the energy once. If the bot restarts during your trip, the trip is finished when the bot is back, and paid once.

    Energy¶

    Activity Energy each day Most energy Seconds per energy
    Hunting 10 + Hunting Cabin level 22 (level 12, rank 96) 10 (+2 per Hunting Weed)
    • Energy refills at 00:00 UTC every day. The refill sets your energy to the day's full amount; it doesn't add to what you had left.
    • A trip that runs over midnight still pays once.
    • Out of hunting energy? A  Wild Boar with Cherry Sauce pays for one more energy of hunting. It's only used for the energy your daily energy doesn't cover, never while you still have energy. Without one, the trip doesn't start and nothing is spent: /hunt tells you so privately, with your hunting menu (your energy, when it refills), and a Go Hunting button on a menu from earlier tells you privately and leaves the menu as it was.

    Experience, quests and leaderboards¶

    A hunt pays 1 experience per energy.

    Trips also move your  quests: the Hunting quest counts the energy you spend, and the quest for each animal counts what you catch of it (Hunting - Elk counts your  elk). A quest that reaches its goal completes on its own and pays its reward once.

    The hunting leaderboards count the seconds of your trips (weed seconds included).

    Rifles¶

    You craft them in the Hunt menu (Craft Hunting Rifle) for scrap from your pouch. You can own one of each. Without one, you only catch  Squirrel.

    Item Crafting cost (wood / metal / leather scrap) Catches, chance each second
    no rifle –  Squirrel 5%
    Savage Model 11 1 / 6 / 2  Squirrel 5%,  Rabbit 5%,  Coyote 5%
    Winchester Model 70 16 / 41 / 20  Wild Turkey 5%,  Pheasant 5%,  White-tailed Deer 5%
    Weatherby Mark V 50 / 200 / 60  Elk 5%,  Moose 5%,  Wild Boar 5%
    • Equip a rifle in the Hunt menu. The one equipped when the trip ends decides the catch, so a swap during a trip counts.
    • With more than one rifle you can turn on rotative hunting: each second of the trip rolls on the next one in turn. With only one, the switch isn't offered and a trip never rotates.
    • Crafting costs exactly the scrap shown. With a scrap short, or a rifle you already own, the Craft button is off.

    Enhancements¶

    Each rifle has its own enhancement, upgraded from the crafting screen for scrap. Every level adds 0.1 percentage points to each of that rifle's three catches (not to your other rifles). Each level needs a rank of twice the level (the crafting screen shows it as "User Level"): below it the upgrade button is off, and a click on an older one upgrades nothing and tells you, privately, the rank you need. The costs below are wood / metal / leather scrap.

    Utilities: Skinning Knife¶

    Crafted for credits in /start → Inventory → Utilities. Every level adds 0.05 percentage points to the chance of finding  Leather Scrap: in hunting's extra prizes, on the Nature Expedition (🧭 expeditions), and in the other prize tables that have  Leather Scrap (votes, bumps, chat, dice, heists, treasure chests and more). With exactly the price in your wallet you can craft it. Level n needs rank n (the screen shows it as "User Level"): below it the craft button is off, and a click on an older one crafts nothing and tells you, privately, the rank you need. The other two utilities, the 🪓 Wood Axe and the ⛏️ Mining Pick, raise Wood and  Metal Scrap ( fishing).

    Buffs¶

    Buff Where it comes from Used by
    Hunting Weed 3 credits in the store; the Party Store hunting
    Wild Boar with Cherry Sauce cooked: 2  Wild Boar + 6  Cherry Sauce hunting, when the energy is gone
    •  Hunting Weed (bought from the store, or collected at the  Party Store): each one adds 2 seconds to one energy of a trip, so a trip uses one per energy, at most. A 3-energy trip with 2 weeds lasts 34 seconds. The extra seconds roll for catches and extra prizes like any other second, and count on the leaderboard. Experience and the Hunting quest count energy, so the extra seconds add none.
    • The hunting menu shows the buffs you have and the trip time they give.

    Pets¶

    Your active pet helps while it's awake and cared for (see 🐾 Pets for every pet, its perks and when they work). Its hunting perk adds its points to one animal. A perk for an animal your rifle doesn't hunt does nothing: a  Moose perk needs the  Weatherby Mark V, and without a rifle only a  Squirrel perk helps. Asleep, or below the perk's conditions, the pet adds nothing. The pet counts if it's active and awake when the trip ends: a pet changed during the trip gives its perks, one put to sleep gives none.

    Buildings that help¶

    In 🏗️ Buildings: the  Hunting Cabin gives +1 hunting energy a day per level.

    Everything adds up once: a rifle enhancement, a pet perk, weed and the  Hunting Cabin on one trip each count exactly once.

    Tips¶

    • Spend the day's energy before 00:00 UTC: the refill doesn't carry leftovers over.
    • A rifle's enhancement only helps its own three animals.
    • Weed is used one per energy: a 10-energy trip uses 10.
