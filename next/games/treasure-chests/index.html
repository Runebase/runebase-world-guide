    Treasure chests¶

    Where to play: Discord

    A quick game of luck: open treasure chests with keys.

    /treasurechest in the treasure chest channel shows the chest. Loot treasure chest, then pick a key: the chest spends exactly that key and rolls its key's prize table three times ( credits,  RUNES, items, experience). A chest whose three rolls all miss pays 1 experience.

    • Without that key, or with the tipbot offline (also when the bot can't read the realm's RUNES wallet), nothing is spent: only you are told.
    • When the realm's RUNES wallet holds 1 RUNES or less, chests still open: the loot table shows its RUNES rows as none: the realm's RUNES wallet is empty, and a RUNES prize you roll is shown as not paid (0 RUNES); the key is spent and the rest of the chest is paid.
    • The same in a server whose RUNES prizes aren't connected yet (every server but the Runebase server, until after the update): the loot table shows its RUNES rows as none: RUNES prizes aren't connected in this server yet, and a RUNES prize is shown as not paid; the rest of the chest is paid.
    • One key clicked on two chest buttons at once opens one chest and spends the key once, also when one of them is in Activities → Treasure and the other under a chest in the channel.
    • Activities → Treasure opens the same chests from the menu: pick a key's table (View … Loot Table), then Use … key; the result shows on your menu and in the chest channel. Without that key, or with the tipbot offline, your menu says why and nothing is spent.
    • Chests open in every 🌍 realm (every server the bot plays in), with the keys of that server's pouch: with /treasurechest only in the server's chest channel, from Activities → Treasure in any channel. Their  RUNES prizes come from that server's own RUNES wallet (the loot table shows them from it), and none when it's empty. A server whose admins haven't set a chest channel opens no chests: the bot tells you so (only you, or on your menu from Activities → Treasure), and nothing is spent. Every refusal of /treasurechest or of a chest in the channel is told only to you: no chest channel on the server, the wrong channel (also on a chest posted before the chest channel moved), no key, or a key button pressed faster than the bot allows; from Activities → Treasure your menu says why.
    • A key button answers at once, even when the bot is busy: in the chest channel you see the bot thinking, then the chest; a refusal replaces that with a note only you see.
    • If something goes wrong while opening the chest, your key isn't spent and you're told: only you under a chest in the channel, on your menu from Activities → Treasure.

    Getting keys: key piles in the channels (🗝️ Piles and the key hunt), the  Treasure Forge building (a  Bronze,  Silver or  Gold Key for 1, 2 or 3  Metal Scrap; 🏗️ Buildings), heists, scratch cards and Loot Reels. Keys are kept in your pouch, which has room for a limited number per pouch level.

    Tips¶

    • A  Gold Key pays about twice what a  Bronze Key pays, at the same odds.
