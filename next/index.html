    The Runebase World Guide¶

    Runebase World is the world of the Runebase games. Today you play it through the Runebase bot, in the Runebase Discord server and its partner servers (🌍 realms); the Runebase World web app, with its MMORPG, comes later, and many games will be playable in both. Each game's page says where you can play it. The games are all connected: what you catch, find and build in one game helps you in the others, and almost everything pays experience (your rank) and credits (the bot's money, which you can withdraw as Credits tokens on the Runebase blockchain).

    Start here: chat in the server (your first message creates your account), then type /start. The main menu shows your rank card; Activities lists the games.

    How the games connect¶

    • Every day at 00:00 UTC your energy refills ( fishing, 🏹 hunting, 🧭 expeditions;  heists: 3 energy, and you can be hit 6 times again), and the buildings' nightly production waits for you in Collect. Through the day: 15 🎰 Loot Reels spins, a 🔤 WordWeaver word every 4 hours, a single-player ♟️ chess game every 3 hours (5 paid wins a day) and 🏰 Hnefatafl (5 paid wins a day), the  dice on a cooldown, a 🗣️ chat payout every 12 hours, the ⛰️ bump and 🗳️ vote windows. 🗝️ Piles and keys appear in the channels, and 🃏 Runes Pattern words keep coming.
    • Materials: fishing, hunting and expeditions fill your basket and pouch (fish, meat, scrap). The fire and the  cooking table turn 🧺 fish, meat, spices and sauces into  dishes: 🍲 buffs for the activities, the heists, Crown of Chaos and Hack & Slash. Scrap ( wood,  metal,  leather) from piles and activities pays for buildings, fire fuel and crafting; building supplies ( Toolbox,  Nail,  Rope) come from piles, prizes and the factories.
    • 🏗️ Buildings (each level needs a rank) give lasting bonuses: more energy, longer expeditions, better store prices, stronger heists and battles, and nightly production.
    •  Credits come from the games' prizes, quests, piles, chests and WordWeaver, chess and Hnefatafl wins, and from the  Iron Bank's interest. You spend them in the store, on gifts, scratch cards, Runes Pattern cards and clan registration. Credits come in from RUNES (or Credits token) deposits and Hermes purchases, and go out as Credits tokens through withdrawals; some prizes (dice, chests, scratch cards, Crown of Chaos) pay  RUNES directly.
    • Experience comes from nearly everything and decides your 🥇 rank. Heists take experience from the defender and give experience to the attacker. Most games also have all-time and monthly 🏅 leaderboards with 🏷️ roles for the top players, and the heist and key hunt ladders can pay monthly RUNES prizes.
    • 🐾 Pets help in fishing, hunting, expeditions and heists while they're awake and cared for.

    What you can count on¶

    • What a screen promises is what you're paid.
    • A double click, or two devices at once, never pays twice or spends twice.
    • If the bot restarts, nothing you earned is lost: trips, the nightly production and your games pick up where they were. While the bot is starting up, it answers "The bot is starting up…" and does nothing.
    • When something is refused (not enough energy, credits or items), you get a clear message and nothing is spent.
    • A refusal says why, where you clicked. A button on your own screen (a menu you opened) shows the reason on that screen, with a way back, as public or private as your screen is: a refused collect, craft, build, cook, store purchase or sale, chest key or scratch card from the menu, an expedition, fishing trip or hunt you can't start, a Crown skill that isn't ready, a refused bid, an auction listing, a heist or a clan action, a chess or Hnefatafl move. Only you see a refusal under a public post (a pile, a challenge, the Crown's post, a chest post, a ladder) or of a slash command: a button or menu that isn't yours, a command used too often, the dice on cooldown, /treasurechest without a key, a scratch card outside its channel, the bot starting up, an old button, /fish and /hunt without energy (with your fishing or hunting menu) or while you're busy, /fire without wood or in a channel where fires can't burn, a /gift you can't make (the receiver doesn't see it). What succeeds stays where it was: a trip, a fire, a gift and a store purchase still show in the channel.
    • Buttons on old messages from an earlier version of the bot only show a note.
    • Commands have a limit per 30 seconds. Past it the bot answers "Slow down!", only to you, every time, and does nothing. 2 uses: /info, /leaderboard, /poll, /price, /ranks, /withdraw; 5: /basket, /buildings, /convert, /craft, /equipment, /fire, /fish, /heist, /hunt, /loottables, /pouch, /quests, /rolepanel, /scratchcard, /settings, /start, /treasurechest, and /browseclans and /heistboard together; 10: /gift, /roll; 20: /hackslash; 30: /store. The buttons of the piles and keys allow 10 clicks in 20 seconds between them; past that, the same "Slow down!", only to you.
