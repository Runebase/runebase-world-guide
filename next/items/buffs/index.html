    🍲 Buffs by game¶

    Every dish and store consumable, grouped by the game it helps (and the one you eat from your basket). Each name leads to its entry (where you get it), each game to its page.

    Fishing¶

    On the  Fishing page.

    Buff What it does
    Pan-Fried Trout with Lemon and Dill one more energy of fishing when the day's energy is gone
    Fishing Beer +2 seconds of fishing per energy, one Fishing Beer per energy

    Hunting¶

    On the 🏹 Hunting page.

    Buff What it does
    Wild Boar with Cherry Sauce one more energy of hunting when the day's energy is gone
    Hunting Weed +2 seconds of hunting per energy, one Hunting Weed per energy

    Expeditions¶

    On the 🧭 Expeditions page.

    Buff What it does
    Braised Rabbit with Mustard Cream Sauce +2 seconds per energy on an expedition, one charge per energy

    Heists¶

    On the 💪 Heists page.

    Buff What it does
    Mackerel and Smoked Paprika 1 less experience lost when you're hit, and when you're counter-attacked as the attacker
    Seared Tuna with Wasabi Butter 3% chance to counter-attack when you're hit
    Grilled Eel with Sichuan Peppercorns 5% chance to keep your energy when you attack
    Fried Squirrel with Sawmill Gravy the defender loses 1 more experience when you attack (and in your counter-attack)
    Wild Turkey with Cranberry Glaze +2 experience when you attack (and in your counter-attack)
    Moose with Blueberry Sauce 10% chance to block when you're hit; as the attacker, it blocks a counter-attack
    Einherjar's Barrier Draught a super buff: blocks the next hit on you (as the attacker, a counter-attack)
    Skadi's Retribution Brew a super buff: counter-attacks the next hit on you
    Frigg's Resurgence Elixir a super buff: your next attack keeps its energy
    Assault Fortification earned in a heist, not eaten (see its page)
    Retribution Fury earned in a heist, not eaten (see its page)

    Crown of Chaos¶

    On the 🧪 Crown of Chaos page.

    Buff What it does
    Blackened Tilapia with Cumin Slaw 15 less damage from each attack on you as the Crown, one charge per attack
    White-tailed Deer with Juniper Berry Demi-Glace +15 damage on your attack on the Crown, one charge per attack
    Forgotten Throne Elixir resets every Crown of Chaos skill of both trees, once
    Chaos Relic pays one attack on the Crown, hit or miss

    Hack & Slash¶

    On the 👹 Hack & Slash page.

    Buff What it does
    Anchovy with Anchovy Powder heals a character back to full health and mana (a dead one can then return to its battle)
    Grilled Sardines with Sumac Yogurt Sauce pays a skill reset (5 in total, whatever the points)
    Baked Cod with Old Bay Butter +25% experience on a quick battle, picked before it or on the results screen and spent once
    Grilled Coyote Steak With Lingonberry Sauce pays a stats reset (half a steak per point spent, rounded up)
    Pheasant with Blackberry Balsamic Glaze fills your stamina to its maximum (Restore Stamina now)
    Elk with Huckleberry Sauce double damage on a quick battle, picked before it or on the results screen and spent once
    Engraving Tool pays an inscription: 1 for a Magic one, 3 for a Rare one

    On the 🧺 basket page.

    Buff What it does
    Swordfish Kebabs with Lemon and Rosemary eaten from the basket for experience
