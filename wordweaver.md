# WordWeaver

Guess a hidden word of 5 to 8 letters. WordWeaver has its own leaderboards (all-time and monthly).

**Where:** `/start` → **Activities** → **WordWeaver**.

1. **Get new word** deals the word.
2. **Guess Word** opens a box to type your guess. You have **six guesses**: after the sixth wrong one the game is over and pays nothing, and a seventh is refused. A word that doesn't exist is refused and doesn't cost a guess.
3. Solving the word pays **20 experience**, a prize from the table below and one point on the WordWeaver leaderboards. It pays once; an old Guess button pays nothing.
4. Each solved word counts 1 toward the **WordWeaver - Solved** quest (a wrong guess counts nothing). A solve that completes it shows the quest's rewards with the word's and pays them once, the quest's experience on top of the 20.

A new word comes **4 hours** after your last one was dealt.



| Prize | Chance per solved word |
|---|---|
| 15 to 25 credits | 8% |
| 4 Nail | 2% |
| 1 Rope | 2% |
| 6 Chaos Relic | 5% |
| 1 Common Pet Token | 1% |
| 1 Pet Token (Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical) | 0.5% each |
| 2 Leather Scrap | 25% |
| 2 Metal Scrap | 25% |
| 2 Wood Scrap | 25% |
| nothing | 4.5% |



## Tips

- A word that doesn't exist costs nothing, so try a real word that tests many letters.
